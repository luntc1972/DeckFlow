## Summary

This video rejects template-driven, optimized Commander deckbuilding in favor of a live, intuition-led approach the creator calls the "box method." Frustrated that Wizards' Commander-focused design and content-creator templates make deckbuilding feel on-rails, the creator describes turning a Timna/Crom cEDH shell into a constantly-changing deck while grinding Pioneer, Modern, Standard, and Legacy events. The method: start with a solid shell (commander, ~37-39 lands, some removal, draw, recursion, plus extra swap options), then bring a large box of cards you love regardless of synergy, including a "maybe board." After each game, swap cards by intuition, exploring small five-to-ten-card theme packages (angels, vampires, evoke scam, stifle, energy, esperfine) and cutting what you've seen. Scale power up or down live by swapping pieces (e.g., Force of Will in, blood tithe harvester out for Ragavan) to match the pod. Memorable combos cited: evoke Fury to trigger Vaultripper; Torrential Gearhulk copying Sublime Epiphany to recast Magma Opus. Key principle: don't get attached, the deck is disposable, the moment matters, not the list. Use the Ultimate Guard Twin Flip tray to hold deck and spares.

## Key Clips

- As someone who has been building commander decks since the first precon Heavenly Inferno, commander deck building has kind of lost its spark until I designed this anti-system at SCG Baltimore.
- I brought a box with Timna and Crom, my old cEDH pair. I turned that box into the favorite commander deck I've ever played. And unlike all my other favorite decks that sit and collect dust, this one doesn't exist anymore. I had my fun and took it apart immediately.
- After each event, I took whatever was left from my competitive decks and jammed them into this Timna Crom shell. No perfect mana base, no spreadsheets, no templates, just cards I liked, vibes I chased.
- I don't build decks anymore as finalized, crystallized, perfect little machines where I finesse every last slot and never end up playing them. They're more living playlists, swapping tracks between rounds, chasing moments, and editing in real time.
- So here's the deck building method I swear by now. I call it the box method. The box I use is the Ultimate Guard Twin Flip n Tray 200. I keep the deck on the left and all the other stuff on the right.
- First, you need a solid shell. So commanders, land base, usually around 37 to 39. A bit of removal, draw, and recursion. It's better if you bring a few options aside from the staples to swap them out or bring in more as you play. Then bring a massive box of cards you love. Doesn't matter if they synergize.
- Doing packages of five to 10 cards is enough to explore a small theme like five angels and 10 vampires. Try a new theme, see a new commander, buy the card and nine other cool cards you like that synergize with them and put it all in there and cut the stuff you already seen.
- The key is don't get attached because it's the moment that matters, not the actual deck. And even if you do get attached, that's cool. Bring some emotional support cardboard in there.
