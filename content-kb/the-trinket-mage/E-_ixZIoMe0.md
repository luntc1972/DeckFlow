## Summary

This video argues that planeswalkers should be allowed as commanders in EDH, framing it as a no-brainer rules change comparable to the recent allowance of legendary vehicles. The creator notes 21 planeswalkers already say they can be your commander, so the precedent exists. On power level, he contends planeswalker commanders like Lord Windgrace, Tasigur, and Tevesh Szat are strong but not ban-worthy, no worse than Edgar Markov or Urza. He rebuts the fear that planeswalker commanders force pillow-fort or stax decks, arguing dedicated anti-creature builds are rare and impractical, and that walkers actually encourage creatures—high-toughness blockers protect them, spot removal gets worse, and many ultimates make or buff creatures. On combos, Doubling Season is dismissed as a slow nine-mana two-card combo, weaker than existing Vorinclex lines or combos like Godo/Helm and Kiki-Jiki; Tamiyo, Field Researcher is named the best Doubling Season pairing. The Chain Veil enables some Teferi combos. He flags only two genuine concerns: three-mana Teferi (with Knowledge Pool) and Narset, Parter of Veils (with wheels), which might warrant a ban. He highlights fun options—Dack Fayden, Wrenn and Six, Tezzeret—and notes board wipes and goad become weaker or stronger accordingly.
