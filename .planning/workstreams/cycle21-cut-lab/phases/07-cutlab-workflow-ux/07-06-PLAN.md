  <name>Task 1: The per-cut line</name>

  `CutLab.cshtml:1265` —
  `"@Model.Proposal.ChangedFamilyCount of 7 metric families changed meaningfully."`
  becomes `"@Model.Proposal.ChangedFamilyCount of 7 deck numbers moved."`

  Make the equivalent replacement in `renderProposalCard` and its proposal test;
  the AJAX path is the highest-frequency renderer. Also replace
  `cutLabWhatifPreviewSummaryCopy` (emitted by the what-if preview renderer) and
  update its test expectation. Before finishing, grep all Cut Lab user-facing

  ("moved by more than a rounding step") or drop it. Do not keep an undefined
  qualifier on the most-read line in the tool.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 4: Name the staleness</name>

  Goals and Compare-to-baseline are point-in-time and need a manual
  "Recalculate"; cut counts and findings are live. Today the user must remember
  which is which, and the default state after any cut is silently stale numbers.

  Minimum viable fix for this plan: mark the stale values. Add a "as of your last
  recalculation" marker next to the Goals `Current` column and the Compare
  `Current` column, and reword both buttons to name what they refresh.

  Recomputing automatically on accept is the better fix and is explicitly OUT of
  scope here — it is an engine/perf change, not copy (D-3). Log it as a follow-up
  with the reasoning, do not silently absorb it.
</task>
