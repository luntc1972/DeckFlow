## Summary

This short clip reviews Bria, Riptide Rogue, a creature that grows stronger whenever you cast spells. Its key value is that Bria also powers up every other creature with prowess you control, so each prowess creature adds another point of damage. This makes the damage output of your spells a much better rate across a wide board. Bria also has an evasion effect: when you cast a noncreature spell, it grants unblockable, letting you push damage through. Overall positioning is as a spellslinger payoff that turns a prowess-heavy board into scaling, hard-to-block damage.
