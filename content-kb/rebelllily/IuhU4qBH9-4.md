## Summary

A four-color (WUBG) Thrasios/Tymna creature-combo cEDH brew nicknamed "I love breakfast," built around Nadu, Winged Wisdom and multiple redundant kill lines. White and black splash onto a Simic Nadu base to add Silence effects (Grand Abolisher, Dosan), black tutors and graveyard payoffs. Combo paths: the Breakfast line (Cephalid Illusionist + Nomads en-Kor milling into Dread Return for Thassa's Oracle, using Narcomoeba); Hermit Druid self-mill into the same Oracle finish; Devoted Druid paired with Machine God's Effigy or Hazel's Brewmaster for infinite mana; Kinnan + Basalt Monolith; Eternal Witness + Snap with Gaea's Cradle; and Nadu plus a zero-cost equip (Lightning Greaves/Shuko) with Scute Swarm or Springheart Nantuko to grind out insects and storm off. Thassa's Oracle plus Tainted Pact/consultation is the safe default win. The host stresses flexibility and redundancy: every card is individually useful, so removal on one threat doesn't stop the deck. The 29-land manabase is strained on white, prioritizing green and blue for Thassa, making Arcane Signet and Relic of Legends key. Goldfished hands show passive but resilient starts. Positioned as a fun, strong, interactive pile rather than the top tournament choiceΓÇöpick it to enjoy creature combo, not to chase the meta.
