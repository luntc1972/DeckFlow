## Summary

Lady Octopus, Inspired Inventor is a one-mana blue commander that gains an ingenuity counter when you draw your first or second card each turn (any turn, including opponents' via Brainstorm), then taps to cast an artifact from hand with mana value up to her counter total for free. The deck floods cheap card-draw artifacts (Sleeper Dart, Guild Globe, Golden Egg, Marina Vendril's Grimoire) to stack counters fast, then storms off. Two win routes: beatdown via Cyberdrive Awakener/Rise and Shine animating all artifacts, or combo by pairing her with untappers like Mirran Spy, Charcoal Retriever, or Sting the Glinting Dagger to recast endlessly, drawing the whole deck and winning with Laboratory Maniac. Card-draw engines (Darksteel Garrison-style: Riddlesmith, panharmonicon) refill hand. Runs only ~25 landsΓÇöjustified because it ends by turn four and is all card draw. Light on interaction, fast but fragile; weak in cEDH (Smothering Tithe, Vexing Bauble punish it). Niche tech: change her creature type with Trickery Charm to enable Valley Floodcaller. A bracket-four budget deck that reliably wins turns four to five.

## Key Clips

- Lady Octopus, Inspired Inventor is a one mana blue 0/2 that says whenever we draw our first or second card each turn, she gets an ingenuity counter. Then we can tap her to cast an artifact spell from our hand with mana value less than or equal to the number of ingenuity counters on her without paying its mana cost. This means we can just cheat big artifacts into play really easily and all we have to do to achieve that is draw cards.
- She only costs one mana as a commander, which is so cheap. A lot of commander decks don't have a good turn one play, but with Lady Octopus you're casting her turn one and immediately starting your game plan. Her play pattern wants us to draw cards and then rewards us by letting us mana cheat stuff into play for free.
- We can only get two counters per turn, but it doesn't say our turn, which means if we draw cards on our opponent's turns with something like a brainstorm, we can also get counters. Getting Lady Octopus up to seven, eight, basically infinite counters is trivially easy because past a certain point you can just cast everything in your deck.
- The infinite combo is Miran Spy, a three mana 1/3 flyer that says whenever you cast an artifact spell you may untap target creature. She doesn't just put artifacts into play, she actually casts them. So we tap Lady Octopus to cast an artifact spell, use Miran Spy to untap Lady Octopus, and go again. As long as we have artifacts in hand to cast, we can continuously endlessly do that.
- To stay fueled we cast pretty much any artifact that says when this enters the battlefield, draw a card: Sleeper Dart, Alchemist's Smile, Guild Globe, Mishra Vendril's Grimoire, Golden Egg, Ornithopter scansship. This is basically a storm deck. Once we start storming off these continuously replenish our hand as we go through the deck, and we win with Laboratory Maniac by digging through our entire deck in a single turn.
- There are two valid reasons to reduce your land count. First, your deck just doesn't need that many because it tries to end the game early ΓÇö if the game is over by turn three you only ever play three lands anyway. Second, you play a ton of card draw, so you can just find your lands. This deck tries to end the game by turn four and is oops all card draw. - The best card apart from the main combo pieces is Sting, the Glinting Dagger, a two-mana equipment giving plus one plus one and haste that untaps the equipped creature at the beginning of each combat. Equip it to Lady Octopus and she untaps every combat, so you can tap her again to put something into play on every opponent's turn, generating huge value plus a lot of ingenuity counters.
