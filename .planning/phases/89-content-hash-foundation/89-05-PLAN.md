<objective>
Wire the two sides of the body hash to the same helper: compute `body_sha256` at publish/distill time (D-01/D-02) on the row the orchestrator upserts, and add the detail-render guard (D-05/D-07) that recomputes the on-disk body hash and logs a structured warning on mismatch or missing hash while still serving (fail-open). Both sides call `ComputeBodySha256` over the identical `SplitHeader` body, so the hashes are provably comparable.

Purpose: SYNC-03 (refuse-to-render-but-log) + the publish-compute half of SYNC-01. This is where the mojibake/stale-body corruption class becomes visible in logs instead of silently served.
Output: publish-time hash on the row; detail-render guard with structured warning; controller + orchestrator tests.
</objective>
