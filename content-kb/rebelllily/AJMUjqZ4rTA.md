## Summary

This is a deck tech for an Ezio, Blade of Vengeance Commander deck built around Assassin's Creed Assassins. Ezio costs just colorless and black, gives Assassin spells free running for double black, and can finish players at 10 or less life. The Assassins sort into four roles: recursion (Shao Jun, Rebecca, Desmond) that mine the graveyard, filled via surveil and discard from the Frye siblings and Mary Read/Anne Bonny; historic-matters payoffs (Bayek, Basim, Eivor); Lords (Achilles Davenport, Arno Dorian, Ezio) that buff the team; and self-sufficient Specialists (Edward Kenway, Kassandra, Ratonhnhaketon). The build is mono-black with a light blue splash, since reliable double-black free running matters more than fixing five colors. Black supplies Black Market Connections, Cover of Darkness, and removal (Fatal Push, Go for the Throat, Chain Assassination); blue adds Temporal Trespass, Rooftop Bypass, and Propaganda. ISU artifact equipment and the Staff and Apple of Eden round it out. The mana base is 34 lands targeting four black sources—two Swamps equal one free running cast, roughly two game actions per turn. Piloting is simple: land two Swamps plus a high-value Assassin, deploy Ezio, attack the unblocked player, and chain Assassins, sequencing triggers like Basim for unblockable enablers.

These assassins, including Shay and Rebecca and also Desmond, all gain some value from having assassin cards in your graveyard, which means we want to find cards to help fill the graveyard with the Fry siblings' surveil or Mary Read and Anne Bonny to discard assassin cards.
- The second theme is historic cards. The order supports each other through the historic keyword cards like Bayek, Basim, and Eivor, all reward you for playing other historic cards like legendary assassins, equipment, or sagas.

And lastly we have some Specialists who are individually powerful: Edward Kenway who creates Treasures, Eivor who returns lands and legendary sagas, Cassandra with the Spear of Leonidas, or Bayek who creates more tokens.

The best part of Etso is that you're able to cheat in a high mana value assassin for just double black, so naturally you just want to have a lot of black.
