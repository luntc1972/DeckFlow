## Summary

Sammy, Wildcat Captain is a six-mana Boros commander with double strike and vigilance that grants all your spells affinity for artifacts, reducing each spell's cost by one colorless mana per artifact you control. Though it looks like a Boros equipment commander, the creator argues it is really a mono-red storm commander. This is a $50 (closer to $40) budget Commander deck built for a budget tournament series (budgetcommander.de). The gameplan: deploy mana rocks (which serve as ramp, affinity-count artifacts, and free rituals during the storm turn), draw through the deck with heavy card draw and impulse draw plus treasure generation, then win. Win conditions are few because the deck draws itself out: Lind Bloom Industrial Regency makes a free pinger that deals one damage per spell cast; Reiterate with free buyback copies a spell and goes infinite with any mana producer over two; and Comet Storm finishes via X damage. Recursion (Past in Flames, Recoup, Return the Past) prevents fizzling. Sparse creatures avoid feeding the deck's own sweepers; white provides protection and removal. The deck is powerful but fragileΓÇöit desperately needs Sammy on board to win. A bracket-4 MTGO game demonstrated a one-turn kill.

## Key Clips

- Sammy Wildcat Captain is a six-mana Boros 4/4 with double strike and vigilance. They also give all of our spells affinity for artifacts. Affinity for artifacts then makes it so that the cost of our spells is reduced by one colorless mana for each artifact we control.
- Sammy may look like the leader of a Boros equipment deck, but they really are a mono red storm commander and probably the filthiest since the Modern Horizons 3 Ashling.
- Mana rocks serve triple duty: they let us cast Sammy quickly, they give us artifacts toward affinity, and once we start our storm turn, any mana rock that can create colored mana becomes a ritual as we play them for free.
- Lind Bloom Industrial Regency is a land with an adventure site that lets us create a 0/1 pinger for free mana. This pinger deals one damage to each opponent whenever we cast a spell. Simply playing 40 spells is totally doable, and this is the only pinger in the deck.
- Reiterate is a free mana instant that copies target instant or sorcery and has buyback for free mana. Since buyback is an additional cost, it also gets cost reduced by affinity, meaning for only two mana we can copy any spell and return reiterate to hand. This goes infinite with anything that produces more than two mana ΓÇö Inspire Tinkering, Seething Song, and Mana Geyser are our best options.
- Stormkill Artist is a four mana creature that creates a treasure whenever we cast an instant or sorcery spell. Since most of our spells only have a single colored pip, with Stormkill Artist on the board we effectively have infinite mana.
- This is a $50 budget deck and rituals are awkwardly expensive. We don't really need them ΓÇö we're going to draw the entire deck anyways, so redundancy in the win lines isn't important.
- A strong way of dealing with these situations is to play patiently, give opponents a chance to build up, then hit them with a sweeper. Players feel pressure to rebuild and are likely to give us an opening to cast Sammy. Once Sammy hits the board, we can go for the win.
