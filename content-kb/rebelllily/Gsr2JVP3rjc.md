## Summary

This video reframes Commander deckbuilding through Final Fantasy 10's sphere grid: treat your deck not as 99 cards but as a character skill tree. The commander sits at the center, and every card becomes a skill, piece of gear, or ally on the commander's journey. The method: start with a defining 'central node' card that captures a vibe, then build outward in clusters of 3-7 cards joined by mechanical or thematic 'connectors.' The demo turns Lathiel-style elf-token commander into a 'black mage' deck. Clusters shown include efficient removal (Assassin's Trophy, Putrefy, Snuff Out, Maelstrom Pulse, Exsanguinate as a finisher), a Final Fantasy black-mage token theme (Fandaniel), spell-copying and graveyard recursion (Primal Amulet, Panoptic Mirror, Toshiro Umezawa, regrowth effects), and a mana cluster where elf tokens become resources (Cryptolith Rite, Enduring Vitality) feeding big X-spells, plus pump effects to make the commander a threat. Recap process: pick three anchor cards, add 3-7 around each, add supporting sub-clusters, fill to 46-48 nonland cards, then 36-38 lands (fewer with more ramp), and refine using the channel's other models. The goal isn't winning faster but playing the commander differently and finding its voice.
