## Summary

This clip discusses Force of Vigor in a Stax deck. Force of Vigor lets you exile one of your own weak green cards to destroy two artifacts or enchantments. The presenter argues this is strong here precisely because the deck's overall card quality is so low: trading away two of your own cards costs little, since your cards "don't matter" and weren't going to accomplish much anyway. The Stax strategy's whole premise is that you happily go down two cards to blow up two of the opponent's good permanents. Card disadvantage is acceptable when your individual cards are low-impact and the goal is locking the board down rather than maximizing your own card value.
