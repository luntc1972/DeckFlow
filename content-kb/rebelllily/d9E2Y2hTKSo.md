## Summary

This deck tech explains pushing Hakbal of the Surging Soul, a four-mana Simic Merfolk commander from the Explorers of the Deep precon, into high-power/cEDH. Hakbal makes Merfolk explore each combat and lets attacks drop lands from hand. The builder identifies Hakbal's strengths—generating land advantage and growing Merfolk via explore—and builds around them. The plan is a Tempo/control deck: heavy stack interaction (Force of Will, Force of Negation, Fierce Guardianship, Flusterstorm, Mental Misstep, Mindbreak Trap, Swan Song, Tale's End, Trickbind, Stifle) plus tempo-positive Stax (Chalice of the Void, Trinisphere, Back to Basics, Curse Totem, Grafdigger's Cage, Pithing Needle, Quicksilver Fountain, Oko). Land denial is a theme (Wasteland/Crucible, stifling fetches, Quicksilver Fountain, Back to Basics). Merfolk lords and value pieces (Lord of Atlantis, Master of Pearl Trident, Deeproot Pilgrimage, Svyelun, Tatyova) provide the combat clock and card flow. Wins via combat plus two combos: Tatyova + Walk the Aeons + Trade Routes + Mystic Sanctuary for infinite turns, and Mind Over Matter + The One Ring to draw out, then Bribery into Thassa's Oracle. Agatha's Soul Cauldron grants Ranger-Captain abilities. Philosophy: ask how a commander fights the meta, not whether it's 'cEDH.'

## Key Clips

- Hakbal of the Surging Soul is a four-mana Commander where at the beginning of combat on your turn each Merfolk creature you control explores, and whenever they attack you can put a land card from your hand onto the battlefield tapped.
- The first step of taking a commander and making it as powerful as possible is identifying when it's powerful. Hakbal is strongest when you have a lot of Merfolk, because more Merfolk means more explore triggers, which means drawing lands, hitting land drops, and ramping.
- The real question isn't 'is this Commander cEDH' but how does this Commander help you fight the meta and what unique advantage does it provide over other commanders, and how are you building your deck around that meta.
- This deck targets opponents' lands heavily: stifling fetch lands, Quicksilver Fountain knocking out colors, Back to Basics punishing non-basics, and Trinisphere — together these can lock a player out while your Hakbal explores every turn and your Merfolk grow to win through combat.
- Ledger Shredder is the only non-Merfolk in the deck. Hakbal's explore puts lands into your hand, and Ledger Shredder's connive lets you convert those excess lands into relevant spells, filtering to find interaction or Stax pieces.
- Deep Root Pilgrimage plus Hakbal is great: whenever a non-token Merfolk you control becomes tapped you make a 1/1 hexproof blue Merfolk, so every attack generates another Merfolk and another explore trigger that quickly gets out of control.
- Win conditions: a Tatyova combo with Walk the Aeons, Trade Routes, and Mystic Sanctuary for infinite turns; or Mind Over Matter with The One Ring to draw your deck, then Bribery to steal Thoracle out of an opponent's deck for the win.
- Agatha's Soul Cauldron lets all your creatures gain the best activated abilities like Ranger-Captain of the Esper, and since Hakbal puts +1/+1 counters on your creatures through explore, they naturally qualify to use those abilities.
