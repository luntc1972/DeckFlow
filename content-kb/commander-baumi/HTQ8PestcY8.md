## Summary

This is an Esper control deck for Commander using partner commanders Ishai and Tevish (Tevot), built around the obscure creature The Unspeakable and arcane spells. Plan A: make a token with Tevot, then cast Reweave (an arcane polymorph) to cheat The Unspeakable into play. The Unspeakable recurs arcane spells like Disrupting Shoal (a weak counterspell) and Rend Flesh (removal), giving near-infinite removal in the late game, especially with Evermind spliced for card draw. Loops keep it on the board via Goryo's Vengeance plus Otherworldly Journey, or Mistveil Plains to recycle Reweave. Sift Through Sands assembles it directly if paired with Peer Through Depths and Reach Through Mists in one turn. Plan B: grind out tokens with Thopter Foundry plus Sword of the Meek under Humility, which also benefits both commanders. The deck runs no mana rocks or ramp—43 mana sources, heavy lands—and spends early turns cantripping to dig for Reweave. It is heavily controlling: 28 removal spells, four board wipes (Damnation, Deluge, Doomskar, Vanquish the Horde), counterspells, plus alternate win conditions like Shark Typhoon, Mind's Dilation, and Bribery. The builder rates it a grindy Bracket 3 deck.
