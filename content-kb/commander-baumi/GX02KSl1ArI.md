## Key Clips

- Iceill Explorer is a green four mana two/four insect scout that lets you play an additional land each turn, play lands from your graveyard, and mills you for a card on a landfall trigger. It's a fully self-contained ramp engine in a single card.
- The core combo: play a fetch land like Cabaretti Courtyard, sacrifice it to grab a land, then replay it from the graveyard, sacrifice to fetch again, and repeat. Iceill Explorer supplies all three needed pieces (extra land drops, graveyard land recursion, and self-mill) on one card.
- Horizon Explorer is a three mana green two/four insect scout that makes your lands enter untapped and creates a Lander token whenever you attack a player. Landers sacrifice for two mana to fetch a basic land tapped, but the untapped clause effectively cost-reduces them to one mana, making it a stronger Spelunking.
- Horizon Explorer turns all taplands and big ramp spells like Traverse the Outlands or Boundless Realms into untapped, mana-positive plays, rewarding you simply for attacking with green creatures every turn.
- Tanuk Memorial Ensign is a three mana two/four Gruul uncommon commander: whenever a land you control enters, it deals one damage to each opponent, and on the second land entry each turn you also draw a card. It turns fetchlands and ramp spells into simultaneous burn and card advantage ΓÇö the reviewer calls it the strongest commander of the set.
- Sammy, Wildcat Captain is a six mana Boros 4/4 with double strike and vigilance that gives all your spells affinity for artifacts. Best built as a treasure-generating value pile around Pirate's Pillage, Unexpected Windfall, and Inspired Tinkering rather than an equipment storm list, since affinity is a flat mana reduction with no extra cost.
- Dauntless Scrapbot is a Solemn Simulacrum sidegrade: a colorless one-mana creature that exiles all opponents' graveyards on ETB and creates a Lander token. Lander tokens are centralized around green in the set, which the reviewer argues is a wasted opportunity since green doesn't need extra land ramp.
- Overall verdict: Edge of Eternities is a sincere, well-executed set with strong art, story, and evocative card designs like the Warp and Station mechanics ΓÇö possibly the best set since Bloomburrow ΓÇö but Iceill Explorer and Horizon Explorer are an overpowered, dominance-warping pair of green ramp creatures.
