## Key Clips

- Humility is an inverted anthem. With a token deck you normally anthem to buff your creatures; Humility buffs everyone down, but as long as you have more dudes you're always ahead, which makes it really unique.
- Due to timestamps/layers, if you play an equipment after Humility has been attached, you keep all the keywords and bonuses on it. So you can have one 20/20 with triple strike while everyone else is just a 1/1.
- If you play Opalescence after Humility, all your enchantments become creatures with power and toughness equal to their mana cost and retain all their text. Do it the opposite way and they're just all 1/1s ΓÇö because layers.
- Athreos, Shroud-Veil puts a coin counter on a creature, and whenever a creature with a coin counter dies or is exiled, it returns under your control. Hit the board with a Farewell and you just steal their things ΓÇö it's board-wipe tribal.
- Nexus of Becoming is a six-mana artifact: at the beginning of combat you draw a card, then may exile a creature or artifact from your hand to create a 3/3 artifact creature construct token that's a copy of it.
- With Claire de Lune, tokens you own become cards that are still tokens ΓÇö they keep existing after leaving the battlefield. So you can put a Nexus token in your hand and ditch it to the same Nexus of Becoming again.
- Key advice for hidden/secret commanders: since the card sits in your 99, not the command zone, the deck must function without it. Pick a card with lots of tutors like Sunforger, and run analog cards that do the same effect so removal doesn't shut you down.
- On creative commander choice: pick a commander that solves your deck's weaknesses rather than one that just synergizes. Malfegor makes opponents sacrifice creatures when you discard your hand, working indirectly with a deck of small card-draw creatures and discard spells.
