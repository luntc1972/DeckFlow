## Summary

This video teaches how to build a color-balanced land base for multicolor Magic decks by fitting the plan rather than mirroring spell-color percentages. Two rules anchor it: more color-intense spells need more sources of that color, and the earlier a spell must be cast, the more sources are required (fewer draws to find them). It uses a premodern Rakdos aggro example, applying Frank Karsten's source-count math (e.g., 21 sources for double-black on curve, 18 for a triple-cost single color), and shows fetchlands and dual lands counting toward multiple colors to hit targets. Moving to Commander, consistency comes from package/theme structure and from wanting the commander on curve, so land-color needs depend on when the commander and key drops are cast (Kaalia early vs. Tymna/Esika later). Karsten's full multicolor numbers become impractical at three colors, so the creator's practical method: build the deck in Moxfield, target ~36 lands, add basics first, adjust counts to skew toward the most-demanded early color, then swap basics for owned duals/temples/Command Tower while watching the color-percentage and per-curve bars until production matches the curve. Mana rocks count as half a source; even basics plus a few duals yield a solid base.

- There's a lot that goes into color fixing for a multicolor magic deck. How do you figure out the numbers for each color? - The first rule that most of you know is that the more color intense a spell is, the more of that color source you'll need. Ravenous Rats needs less black in our land base than our Doomsday Slayer.
- The second rule is the earlier we need to cast the spell, the more lands we need to satisfy that requirement. Our Slayer needs more swamps than our Hammer of Bogardan needs mountains, because even though they both have the same requirements of two colored pips, Hammer comes down later ΓÇö turn 4, 5, 6 ΓÇö meaning we have more opportunities to draw mountains.
- Referencing Frank Karsten, he has the math for numbers of color sources required to cast a spell on curve. To cast our double black spells on curve we need 21 black sources, and to cast our three mana spell of one color we need 18 red sources. For gold cards like Terminate, take the requirements of each color, then add one to all of it: 14 sources of red, 14 of black, and 22 that make both red and black.
- Fetches and duals can help us as each counts toward producing the color source we need. Bloodstained Mire counts for four black and four red, likewise Sulfur Springs, Tainted Peak, Gemstone Mine, which puts us at 16 of both sources.
- According to the Frank Karsten table, the Kaalia deck will need 19 sources of black, white and red each, and a whopping 37 sources that need all three to cast on curve. - Frank Karsten says rocks and dorks count as half a color source because you still need to spend mana to make mana, and card draw comes out to a fraction of the amount of lands you have of that source out of your full library.
