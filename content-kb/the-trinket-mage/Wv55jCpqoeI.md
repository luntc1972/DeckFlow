## Summary

This podcast discusses Magic: The Gathering Commander cards that improve over time. The hosts argue cards age well when they are non-generic, synergistic payoffs that do something specific few other cards do. ETB-doublers, proliferate, and tutors (Trinket Mage, Perplex) improve as more targets get printed. Cards keyed to singleton decks (Hermit Druid) or high average mana value (Analyze the Pollen, Keen Duelist) benefit from MDFCs and alternative-cost cards. Tortured Existence is highlighted as the prime example: cheap to cast and activate, it scales endlessly as stronger creatures are printed. Other cards getting better include Cloudstone Curio, Inalla (more wizards printed), Hellkite Corsair (more powerful enters/attacks commanders), and lands like Tolaria West and Urza's Cave as utility lands improve. Generic removal and Voltron trend worse due to power creep and better interaction, though protection (Teferi's Protection) keeps Voltron viable. Reanimator and artifact archetypes are predicted to keep improving; graveyard strategies suffer from increasing graveyard hate. For finishing stalled control boards, the hosts recommend assessing your clock and protection rather than a single bomb. They also cover white reanimator commanders (Nymris, Magar) and token/dice organization preferences.

## Key Clips

- Cards can gain new context and get better over time. Rhystic Study is garbage in 1v1 but much stronger in Commander, where three times the opponents means three times the triggers.
- ETB-doubler effects (panharmonicon-style) and cheap creatures like Thassa, Deep-Dwelling get better as more broken, cheap ETB creatures enter the format. Payoffs in general improve over time.
- Cards keying off high average mana valueΓÇöAnalyze the Pollen, delve cards, alternative-cost spellsΓÇöscale up as MDFCs and Murktide Regents artificially inflate deck mana value, making collect-evidence and mana-value payoffs easier.
- Niche tutors like Trinket Mage and Perplex improve as more search targets get printed. Three-drops are now a premium slot (Adeline, Broadside Bombardiers), making Perplex's mana-value-3 search far stronger than at print.
- Utility-land tutorsΓÇöTolaria West, Expedition Map, Sequestered Stash style effectsΓÇögot way better as utility lands improved. Talon Gates of Madara and Urza's Cave are now auto-includes, replacing land-based card-draw engines like War Room.
- Tortured Existence is the golden example: one black to cast, one black to recur a creature, hard to power-creep on cost, and scales as creatures get more powerful. It now defines a whole archetype rather than being a fringe card.
- Inalla and similar tribal eminence/payoff commanders only get better because Wizards keeps printing wizards, constantly adding new tools. A future broken wizard ETB could push Inalla into a higher bracket.
- General trend: cards that improve over time are non-generic, synergistic payoffs doing something very specific that few other cards do, or cards that 'require' something (good three-drops, big creatures) whose enablers keep getting reprinted, raising consistency. Generic good creatures, removal, and mana rocks instead get power-crept.
