## Summary

This video frames a brew for Symbiote Spider-Man, a blue-black Commander that draws cards equal to combat damage dealt to a player and, from the graveyard, can graft itself onto another creature to pass on that ability. The deck leans into theft—Aether Snatch, X-spells like Blue Sun's Twilight, Dominate, Entrancing Melody, Govern the Guildless, Invoke the Winds, Tempted by the Oriq, and Trickster God's Heist—protected by Champion's Helm, Change of Plans, Ripples of Potential, and Slip Out the Back. The core strategy is tempo, treated as a flexible aggro-control hybrid that shifts roles and runs cantrips (opt, ponder, consider). The host stresses that scale matters in multiplayer: single card-per-turn draw is too slow against three opponents, so the deck wants engines (Trail of Evidence, Wavebreak Hippocamp, Nymris, Faerie Mastermind, Ledger Shredder, Counterbalance) and spell-based finishers (Metallurgic Summonings, Murmuring Mystic, Talrand, runechanter-style Voltron pivots). It rounds out with counterspells, cantrip-fueled draw that mills the commander, and removal like Toxic Deluge, giving two win axes—go-wide spellslinger and Voltron.
