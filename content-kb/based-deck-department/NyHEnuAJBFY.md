## Summary

Core principle: decks fail from lack of focus, not lack of card knowledge. A deck can reliably do only ~5 "things" well, so pick specialization and secondary abilities BEFORE looking at cards — treat the deck like an RPG character. Read the commander's printed text as primary abilities that dictate which secondary abilities (ramp, card draw, removal, protection) to add for a cohesive win route; clear vision makes cutting cards easy. Examples: Vincent, Vengeful (budget bracket 3, Mardu assassin) — go-wide small evasive creatures, pump effects, ways to connect and protect him. Galazeth Prismari (budget bracket 3 Izzet wizard) — generate artifacts/treasure to fuel spells, control the board, then burn out; needs protection and card draw. Stella Lee (budget bracket 4 combo) — ramp to a critical mana threshold, dig/draw to find a one-hit-kill spell plus enough spells to copy it, plus self-protection. Below cEDH (bracket 4 and under), strong decks come from a good commander + clear focused vision, not expensive cards. Focus lets you break deckbuilding conventions deliberately. Optionally add a character backstory to reinforce theme (e.g. a no-equipment Voltron brawler). Suggested tools: Scryfall and EDHREC for card hunting.

- **[02:59]** Look at the words printed on your commander as their primary abilities that inform what the secondary abilities are that you're going to add to round out their toolkit. In most cases, you'll think about what you want your character's secondary abilities to be, and then you'll go and find the cards to fill your deck that do those things.
- **[03:27]** It's the act of proactively choosing your specialization and abilities before you start looking at cards for your deck that make or break whether your deck is going to be good or bad.

He wants creatures to hit your opponents, so this ability requires us to create as many small, hard to block guys as we can get.

The first thing our draconic wizard would want is the ability to make artifacts and treasure, then use that overwhelming power to control the battle.

The first thing she wants is the ability to make more mana so she can get up to this critical mana threshold as fast as possible. She'd also want to dig through her spell book till she finds those critical one-hit spells.
