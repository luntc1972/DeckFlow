## Summary

cEDH set review of Marvel Superheroes cards for the 99. Alicia Masters is red treasure ramp that turns on Mox Amber. Hammerhead, Magia Boss is a third unconditional creature/artifact sac (alongside Carrion Feeder, Bartolomé) wanted in Tayam(?). Shang-Chi, Master of Kung Fu is a sub-3-mana green legendary haste enabler (better than Chainer/Tyvar), a Green Sun's Zenith/Sisay(?) target praised heavily. Jennifer Walters/Sensational She-Hulk is a two-mana Grand-Abolisher-style effect that pays off in stax. Black Widow, Agile Avenger and King T'Challa give card-advantage parity to Boros/Winota decks. Wondrous Wasp is flash ability-removal (vs Abigail). Vision Quest tutors artifacts like Lotus Bloom, Lion's Eye Diamond, Sensei's Divining Top onto the battlefield—a new Jeweled-Lotus-like ramp pattern for Niv-Mizzet, Emry, Kodama. The Fantasticar sacrifices on a fourth noncreature spell for 16 hasty power, an alternative storm win-con. Hawkeye's Bow pings each opponent when its creature taps (infinite with Seeker of Skybreak). Hatut Zeraze copies per commander cast to blow up Rhystic Study/Smothering Tithe. Captain America untaps mana dorks (Bloom Tender). Ultron copies artifacts; The Ten Rings refills to ten cards. World War Hulk is a five-mana Atarka(?).

## Key Clips

- **[06:04]** The third version of this effect is wanted—it's this, Carrion Feeder, Bartolomé. I will definitely be playing Hammerhead when I sleeve up Tayam again.
- **[09:56]** To me this is one of the first abolisher-style effects that really pays off to be in a stax deck—if you flood out, She-Hulk doesn't lose any relevant lines of text, it turns into a powerful offensive creature and targeted removal on blocks.
- **[14:12]** This is a legendary haste enabler for less than three mana. The only relevant cards that did that were Chainer at four or Tyvar at three; Shani being a two-drop Sisay can tutor with one extra power is huge, and a Green Sun's Zenith away from the battlefield.
- **[44:22]** In a meta of Rhystic Study and Smothering Tithe copies, Hatut comes in one-sided—'here's three copies of this card, blow up your Rhystic, blow up your Mystic'—best in decks that cast partner commanders.
- **[51:22]** Vision Quest is two mana to put a Lotus Bloom onto the battlefield, or Lion's Eye Diamond, or Sensei's Divining Top—a to-the-battlefield artifact tutor and a brand-new play pattern like what we lost with Jeweled Lotus, great for Niv-Mizzet or Kodama.
- **[57:56]** Hawkeye's Bow: whenever the equipped creature becomes tapped it deals one to each opponent. Put it on Seeker of Skybreak—it taps to untap itself—infinite pings, a clean combo in low-color decks.
- **[64:41]** If you cast four noncreature spells in a single turn you get 16 hasty power. It's a real alternative win condition for storm decks—cast Fantasticar, ritual, ritual, ritual, pop it, and pressure the Rograkh player.
