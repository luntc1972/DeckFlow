## Summary

Core deckbuilding thesis: build for a single, focused win condition rather than spreading across multiple unrelated strategies. Decks pulled in many directions lack synergy and their win-condition momentum is easily disrupted. Analogy: one giant snowball plows through disruption (rocks, trees, board wipes, politics) where many small snowballs get stopped. Every card should support and synergize with one singular focus—whether commander damage, a wide pumped board, or a combo win. A plan can use multiple enabler cards, but the type of win condition should stay the same. Avoid diluting the deck with alternative win conditions; instead invest that space in strengthening and adding resilience to the primary win condition. This applies to combo decks too: rather than running several different combo lines, build redundancy and resilience around one main combo line so it survives bad draws, multiple board wipes, and table politics. Focus and resilience, not backup plans, produce the most consistent winning decks.
