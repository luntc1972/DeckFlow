## Summary

This video reviews winning decks from a deckbuilding challenge using choose-a-background commanders. For Alora, Merry Thief (bounces an unblockable attacker at end step), the winner runs Sneak Attack to cheat out 16 high-cost creatures sacrificed end of turn, using polymorph effects (Chaotic Transformation, Reality Scramble via Retrace) to tutor Sneak Attack effects by transforming the Guild Artisan background, plus flicker to keep cheated creatures permanently. Amber Gristle O'Maul (discard hand, draw per attacked player) wins with a budget cleric/dwarf sacrifice deck using Haunted One for undying, nine boardwipes broken via parity, counter-removal, and reanimation/madness synergy. Ellyn Harbeeze (token-count library dig) wins with a Bellringer untap deck pairing tappers (mana, tokens, treasures) with mass-untap effects and Agent of the Iron Throne for implied damage. Viconia, Drow Apostate (random graveyard creature return) wins with a high-mana-value cycling deck using 13 landcycling creatures, MDFC lands (14 land count), and Tavern Brawler for big attackers. Halsin (animate tokens into 4/4 bears) wins with an incubate deck, since incubator tokens gain counters without flipping, survive boardwipes, and become bears with Inspiring Leader.

## Key Clips

- Weird background commanders deckbuilding challenge: build a deck using one of the choose-a-background commanders partnered with a background of the builder's choosing. Judging criteria were creativity, cohesion, and likelihood to produce dynamic gameplay, with creativity and cohesion as the main two. A winner was picked for each of the five commanders.
- Alora, Merry Thief winner: Filthystaxplayer's Sneak Attack deck with Guild Artisan as the background. It uses polymorph effects (Chaotic Transformation, Reality Scramble with Retrace) to tutor out a Sneak Attack effect by polymorphing its own background, castable from the command zone. It runs 16 creatures of mana value 6+ to cheat out, plus a flicker subtheme to recycle ETBs and keep Sneak Attacked creatures permanently.
- Amber Gristle O'Maul winner: Lord Reverend's clerics and dwarves deck with Haunted One as the background. It dumps cleric/dwarf creatures that sacrifice for value, swings with Amber to wheel, and uses Haunted One's undying to break parity on nine boardwipes (including Jokulhaups). A deliberately janky, very low-budget brew (under $50 excluding Haunted One) with a strong death-and-dying thematic core.
- Ellyn Harbeeze Busybody winner: atrus159's Bellringer deck with Agent of the Iron Throne as the background. A twiddle-storm deck built on effects that untap all creatures, paired with creatures that tap for tokens, mana, and treasures. It taps for value, untaps to refresh, and digs deeper with Ellyn each cycle. Agent of the Iron Throne provides implied damage from a board of tokens instead of needing an explicit wincon.
- Viconia, Drow Apostate winner: Chimes' high mana value cycling deck with Tavern Brawler as the background. It runs 13 generic cycling creatures, 13 landcycling creatures, and 11 MDFC lands, dropping the official land count to 14, with a 4.3 average card cost so Tavern Brawler buffs Viconia hard. A funky midrange battlecruiser brew that gestures toward many archetypes.
- Halsin, Emerald Archdruid winner: CubiCCL's incubate deck with Inspiring Leader as the background. Incubate tokens carry +1/+1 counters without needing to be flipped, so Halsin animates them into 4/4 (then 6/6 via Inspiring Leader) bears for one mana while keeping counter value. The deck runs 12 incubate cards plus proliferate and seven board wipes the tokens survive, since they aren't creatures on opponents' turns.
