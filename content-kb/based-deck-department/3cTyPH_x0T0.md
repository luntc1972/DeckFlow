## Key Clips

- **[11:30]** At bracket three and below, you probably need to start paying attention if opponents have the ability to cast multiple stronger spells in a turn, and the six mana threshold is likely that point. In most cases you don't need to stress too much before turn five, but take into account how much someone has ramped.
- **[12:58]** If your opponent's playing a graveyard deck, their 25 card strong graveyard is basically an extension of their hand. The player who has seven cards in hand has actually drawn another 15 across the game and has a cherrypicked hand about to catapult them to a win.
- **[13:22]** An aggro player with four creatures will often look far more threatening than a control deck with none. But that aggro player has one card in hand and the control deck has seven, and after the control player board wipes the balance of power will shift dramatically.
- **[14:40]** Panlaza as an immediate threat doesn't seem scary, but getting an extra card every time you play a dinosaur is powerful over multiple turns, and Panlaza makes it worse by making those cards free to play. That less in-your-face resource advantage you should watch like a hawk.
- **[16:46]** For Voltron strategies, you typically want to deal with the commander and slow them down before they have some form of protection or before they attack, because if they're attacking it probably means they've got instant speed protection.
- **[17:32]** When it comes to control decks, punch them in the face. Putting them on low life makes it harder to stabilize. Control decks win at sorcery speed, but a control deck going to turn six or beyond on 35 or more life is a recipe for disaster.
- **[18:01]** Unless you have a really good reason not to, always kill the combo player first because their decks accelerate into a win the fastest, usually in a single turn. Their wins are the hardest to see coming, and the only good combo player is a dead combo player.
- **[20:30]** Any card that can instantly generate a lot of resources or be cast for free breaks the cadence of the game and catapults a player ahead in a burst, like Sol Ring, Dark Ritual, Jeska's Will, and Fierce Guardianship.
