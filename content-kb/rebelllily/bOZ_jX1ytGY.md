## Summary

This Commander deck is built around the Infinite Guideline Station, a five-color artifact vessel that creates a 2-power robot for each multicolor permanent on entry and, once crewed with 12 power, awakens into a 7/15 flyer that draws a card per multicolor permanent. The deck's core is chaos via cascade: Chimil, Dark Apostle, Deny Reality into Time Vortex, Maelstrom Nexus, Let the Galaxy Burn, and Yidaro/Ydrris fuel repeated free spells, supported by space-time effects (Decaying Time Loop, Planetary Annihilation, Quantum Misalignment, Saint/Space Valerin). It overlays two token swarms: Eldrazi (Anti-Causal Vestige with warp, Chittering Dispatcher/host, Kozilek's Unsealing, Titan's Vanguard, From Beyond, Sire of Stagnation, World Breaker, Hideous Taskmaster) and Slivers (The First Sliver's cascade, Sliver Queen/Smith token-makers, plus board-control and mana-fixing slivers). ## Key Clips

- Our commander is the infinite guideline station, a five-color artifact vessel. Upon entering the battlefield, it creates a number of service units, two robots, one for each multicolor permanent under your control. Once you've assigned 12 power to crew the station, it awakens. The infinite guideline station becomes a 7/15 flying construct that draws a card for each multicolor permanent you command.
- This deck plays a variety of cascade spells such as Chimil, the Inner Sun, which discovers five on each of your end steps. Dark Apostle adds on cascade to a non-creature spell we cast for three. Maelstrom Nexus gives each of our first spells cascade. Let the Galaxy Burn is an expansive potential board wipe that helps clean up smaller creatures as you cascade into a bigger one. And lastly, Yidris, a co-pilot that helps us navigate this chaos.
- A remnant of the Eldrazi is found in Anticausal Vestige, an Eldrazi with warp that cheats in a permanent from your hand with mana value less than or equal to number of lands you control. Sire of Stagnation, a six-mana Eldrazi, exiles the top two cards of an opponent's library while you draw two every time a land enters the battlefield under your control.
- The sliver queen, Root Sliver, and Sliversmith all help create sliver tokens to evolve into the many augmentations available to them, such as Taunting Sliver, which gives your slivers the ability to control the battlefield by goading opponents' creatures. Regal Sliver makes you the monarch. Necrotic and Harmonic Sliver turn these sliver tokens into board control while Manaweft and Gemhide Sliver help color fix.
- The biologist Rukarell Vex links the greater species together by allowing us to choose Eldrazi and turns slivers into Eldrazi as well. Meanwhile, the Hivestone merges the hive's evolution with the Eldrazi. A forbidding card to truly bring them together would be to play the Slivdrazi Monstrosity, but that is up to your podmates to decide.
- The deck is quite simple. Since most of the threats sit within the 3 to five mana range and higher, most of the hands we're looking for want a few sources of mana and preferably a variety of colors. There's at least 14 sources of ramp in the deck, including Conduit of Worlds and Crucible of Worlds as a way to recur fetch lands to guarantee your land drops.
- There's an emphasis on multicolor permanents in this deck to make sure the guideline station can refill your hand turn after turn, but you won't need that much once you start setting up cascade cards like Chimil, Yidris, and the Maelstrom Nexus. How you win is just building a strong board with the perfect harmony of Eldrazi spawns and monsters and the evolving horde of slivers.
- There's limited interaction or removal. The deck plays to be the threat and re-threaten again and again as the table deals with your board state.
