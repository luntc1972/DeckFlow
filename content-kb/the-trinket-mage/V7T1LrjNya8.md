## Key Clips

- This is a Jeska Falthus Obosh deck aiming for bracket three. Falthus gives deathtouch to Jeska, which is a really cool combination. Use Jeska's zero ability to allow Falthus to get in for triple damage. With a pump spell and Obosh, you can get reasonable damage. Main issue is dealing with hasty evasive creaturesΓÇöthings like boots hinder the deck.
- Remember with Obosh you can't play anything with an even mana valueΓÇöeverything has to be odd. Sensei's Divining Top isn't enough advantage in this deck. Rituals feel bad in a deck that's not a combo deck. Duress is better at higher brackets; Thoughtseize is better in lower brackets because it can hit creatures.
- For the hexproof and shroud boots problem, instead of more spot removal, play edict effects so they have to sacrifice the creatures. Ensnaring Bridge stops big attackers from hitting Jeska. Jeska triples the damage dealt by a creature but doesn't triple the power, so you can cast Tainted Strike after attacking for big damage.
- This commander makes spells you cast from your graveyard cost two less, and when opponents lose life you may cast an instant or sorcery from your graveyard once each turn. So you want to make people lose life every turn to activate it. The curve is a tad highΓÇölowering it lets you cast on each person's turn more often. Consider Polluted Bonds, Bloodchief Ascension, Breathstealer's Crypt for repeated life loss.
- For the graveyard-value control deck, the crabs are slowΓÇögreat turn one but bad later. Add Careful Study and Forbidden Alchemy for self-milling and early plays; spells can be doubled up and reused later, plus they trigger the commander. Domineering Will is always weaker than it should beΓÇöIllusory Gambit and Reins of Power are better.
- Reanimator is a combo deck: Entomb plus a reanimation spell is your A-plus-B. The key problem is what you reanimate. Cheating Sheoldred or Toxrill into play turn one does nothing because you have nothing else in the yard. Pick targets that affect the board immediatelyΓÇöConsecrated Sphinx, Hullbreaker/Tyrant style counters, things that generate value the turn they land.
- For bracket four, Wash Away is very good because lots of bracket-four decks just want their commander to combo off, and this beats that. Lean harder into reanimator by cutting value-oriented midrange creatures and weaker reanimation targets for better targets plus more interaction and removal. Abdel animate-dead lines fit the deck better than the Thassa's Oracle / Demonic Consultation combo.
- For the Hearthhull lands-matter deck that gets too much value without ways to close, add big thematic finishers: God-Eternal Rhonas doubles your commander's power to crack in for 12, Greater Gargadon, Avatar Kyoshi earthbends an 8/8 every turn. Mayhem Devil may be a trap if you're not sacrificing enough per turnΓÇömore big beaters get you there.
