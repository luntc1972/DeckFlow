## Summary

The creator critiques Cascade as a Commander mechanic despite owning and enjoying a Cascade deck (Ratadrabik). Two core problems: First, Cascade adds variance—a six-mana Cascade can hit a five-mana bomb or a one-mana dud (Apex Devastator, Maelstrom Wanderer can win or whiff). EDH is already high-variance (four players, 100-card singleton, multiple plans), so piling on more variance is questionable. His Rata deck works because it's otherwise low-variance (two-mana dork commander, four-mana ramp), letting Cascade be the fun wild card. Second, Cascade discourages situational and instant-speed cards (counterspells, protection) and encourages modal cards, lower-cost-alternative cards, and removal stapled to creatures. Players can deck-build around this limitation (Yidris with Spellskite, Unsubstantiate; Rata's creature-based removal), but interesting deckbuilding rarely yields interesting gameplay, and players may just run less interaction—pushing solitaire snowball decks. Discover partly fixes this by letting you put the card in hand to cast at the optimal time, reducing variance and dead cards. He welcomed Discover but criticized Wizards for continuing to print Cascade cards. A 2025 update notes no new Cascade since, plus two new Discover cards.
