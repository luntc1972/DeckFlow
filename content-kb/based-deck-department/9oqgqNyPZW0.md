## Summary

Riku of Many Paths (Temur) modal-spells deck reviewed. Core problem: modal spells are interactive one-for-ones that cede card advantage; fix by adding vanilla draw, cast-triggered draw engines, or graveyard recursion. Diagnosis: too many win lines (ping, birds, Voltron) spread thin—commit to one; ping (Guttersnipe, Fiery Inscription, Arcane Bombardment) too slow for turn 6-7, so prioritize Riku commander damage plus go-wide birds. Cut expensive low-impact modal spells (Titania's Command, Seasons of the Bold, Sublime Epiphany), Blast spells (too narrow), most ramp and cost-reducers (ramping into nothing on a low curve), and greedy tapland-heavy manabase. Keep 4-5 more cheap protection. Recommended adds: Cryptic Command over Archmage's Charm, Heritage Reclamation, Fierce Guardianship, Secret Identity, Defend the Rider, Three Steps Ahead, Jeska's, Trash the Town, Skullclamp, Frostcliff Siege, Airborne Aid, Distant Melody, Winged Portent, Beast Whisperer/Shared Animosity, Beastmaster Ascension, Cryptolith Rite, Bloom Tender, Roaming Throne (Wizards), Quandrix apprentice. Principles: focus by cutting not adding; every modal spell should hit protection/draw/wincon buckets; expensive spells must be game-changing; balance ramp against castable payoffs. For higher power, go all-in commander damage with counter-doublers (Fangs of Kalonia) and damage amplifiers plus free protection. Deck peaks in bracket 2-3.
