## Summary

The creator, a CAG member, reacts to Commander bans: Dockside Extortionist, Mana Crypt, Jeweled Lotus, and Nadu. He only agrees with banning Dockside and Mana Crypt. Using his 'escalation theory'—the idea that each player's power scales upward over a game until reaching a satisfying apex—he argues free-mana cards like Mana Crypt and Dockside push one player's power curve far above opponents too early, breaking the expected play pattern and worsening games between strangers. His ideal would cut all free mana (Mana Crypt, Dockside, Sol Ring, Chrome Mox) to compress the power gap so precons approach high-power levels and pregame power talk becomes unnecessary. He disagrees with banning Jeweled Lotus, since it lets niche high-cost or low-play commanders (his Razia deck) get cast, a problem solvable by table conversation. He disagrees with banning Nadu, calling it a self-selecting cEDH-only card with a slow, non-deterministic, unfun play pattern that casuals reject anyway. For cEDH, he notes losing Mana Crypt and Dockside hurts aggressive mulligans, Rhystic/Rhystic Study starts, color-fixing, and combo decks like Tymna/Tivit and blue Farm, while Rograkh/mox-based decks gain relative strength. He urges respectful discussion.

## Key Clips

- I only agree with two of them and I actually only agree with dockside and Mana Crypt... I think these cards should get banned for kind of like power reasons to make it easier for untrusted play, that's the word for like just playing with strangers.
- Escalation theory: throughout the course of a game your average power goes up. Everyone is scaling. The expected play pattern of Commander is that each player is going up and eventually we hit a point where the power is at its apex that the game ends and everyone has a good time.
- Mana Crypt basically sets one player at a different chart. They're able to reach the levels of power faster than everyone else and it's turn after turn of amplifying power. That's why I think Mana Crypt is an issue, because it puts you that much ahead versus other opponents.
- If I had my dream I would cut Dockside, Mana Crypt, I would go even harder, I would cut Chrome Mox, Sol Ring, all of that. I don't want any free Mana in this game. What needs to get banned is the Mana supporting that top end.
- I don't agree with the Jeweled Lotus and Nadu bans. Jeweled Lotus lets me play Razia, a do-nothing commander in a Boros deck that doesn't have as much access to ramp. A lot of decks needed Jeweled Lotus to work, and I think the Ward-commander problem is one we can solve by talking about it.
- Nadu is a self-selected issue. It won't really get played in the casual space because the play pattern takes long non-deterministic sequences that can't be shortcut and might eventually fizzle out. Casuals will quickly realize this card sucks to play with.
- For cEDH, losing Mana Crypt and Dockside makes your main-phase Ad Nauseam significantly harder. Dockside Extortion fixes your colors to easily play Thoracle or whatever else you need. Storm decks like brew get hit hard because Dockside is one of the best combo decks that just wins with a big Dockside under protection.
- Rakdos stocks are up. Compared to everyone else you still have Mox Amber and Mox Opal, so you're still able to generate more Mana than everyone else. The game cares about mana and the investment of mana, so Rograkh or other Rakdos decks are still really really powerful.
