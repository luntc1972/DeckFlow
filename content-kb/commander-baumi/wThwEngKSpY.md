## Summary

This video showcases Lady Octopus, Inspired Inventor, a one-mana blue 0/2 commander that gains an ingenuity counter on your first or second card draw each turn, then taps to cast an artifact from hand (mana value ≤ counters) for free. Its strengths: a cheap turn-one play that immediately advances the plan, and a draw-rewards-cheat loop that scales fast to near-infinite counters, especially since drawing on opponents' turns (e.g., Brainstorm) also adds counters. The builder targets a budget Commander tournament, so instead of pricey big artifacts he runs two win routes: a beatdown plan animating artifacts (Cyberdrive Awakener, Rise and Shine), or a storm/combo plan. The combo uses untappers—Mirran Spy, Chakram Retriever, Sting the Glinting Dagger, Valley Flood Cooler (needing a creature-type swap via Trickery Charm)—plus cheap card-draw artifacts (Golden Egg, Guild Globe) and draw enablers (Riddlesmith, Grand Architect) to chain casts through the whole deck, winning with Laboratory Maniac. Key tech: God-Pharaoh's Statue, Panharmonicon, foundry-style cost reducers. The deck runs only 25 lands due to heavy card draw and a turn-four clock, plays minimal interaction, and is fragile—vulnerable to removal and poor against cEDH hatebears—so he won't bring it to the tournament.
