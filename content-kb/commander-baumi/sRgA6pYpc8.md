## Key Clips

- My top pick out of Murders at Karlov Manor is Aftermath Analyst. It's a two-mana 1/3 that when it enters mills you for three cards, and then for four mana you can activate it to sacrifice it and get all of the lands out of your graveyard onto the battlefield tapped. It works particularly well if you play fetchlands, which come back into play for landfall triggers. Foundational for landfall decks, graveyard decks, and self-mill decks of any kind.
- Smirking Spelljacker is a five-mana 4/4 in blue with flash and flying. When it enters the battlefield it exiles a spell an opponent controls, and you can then cast that spell for free. Because it exiles rather than counters, it works on cards that can't be countered like Supreme Verdict. I started playing it for my Edric tournament deck to play around cards that can't be countered.
- The best creature from Outlaws of Thunder Junction is Claim Jumper, a four-mana 4/4 with vigilance. When it enters it checks if your opponents have more lands than you; if they do you search your deck for a Plains and put it into play tapped, then does that again. This is white catch-up ramp ΓÇö you deliberately set it up by playing Lotus Field, Lotus Vale, or bounce lands so opponents have more lands than you.
- Dream Tide Veil is a four-mana 7/5 with Vanishing 2, but whenever a player casts their second spell on a turn you proliferate. It never dies to Vanishing because it keeps proliferating endlessly, so effectively it's a four-mana 7/5 with upside. Plays incredibly well in any deck that cares about counters.
- Six is a four-mana 2/4 with reach. Whenever he attacks you mill four cards and pick out a land from among them to put into your hand. He also gives nonland permanent cards in your graveyard retrace, letting you cast them from your graveyard by discarding a land. Add Six to any graveyard or self-mill deck and he immediately becomes the strongest card in it. The best card of the year.
- Darkstar Augur is a four-mana 2/3 with flying that, at the beginning of your upkeep, reveals the top card of your library and puts it into your hand, with you losing life equal to its mana value. It has Offspring, so for one additional black you also get a 1/1 copy. A great card-draw engine, though you want some lifegain to go alongside it.
- The Mindskinner is a four-mana 10/1 that can't be blocked, and if a source you control would deal damage to an opponent, prevent that damage and each opponent mills that many cards instead. In a dedicated mill deck it has won me two games ΓÇö swing the 10/1 plus other creatures at an unblocked opponent and mill 20-plus cards at a time.
- Helpful Hunter is a two-mana 1/1 that draws a card when it ETBs ΓÇö the perfect white card. Great in the early game as a small blocker that keeps attackers away, and later it just replaces itself by drawing a card. Scrolling Regent from Foundations is also a four-mana 4/2 that punishes opponents for drawing (they lose one life) while each player draws on your upkeep ΓÇö good glue for decks lacking card draw.
