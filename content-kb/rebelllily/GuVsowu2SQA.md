## Summary

This is a live deck-building stream for a blue-red Commander deck led by Captain Howler, a four-mana shark-pirate from Aether Drift with Ward. His ability: whenever you discard cards, a target creature gets +2/+0 per card discarded, and when that creature deals combat damage to a player you draw a card. Each discard trigger stacks separately, so multiple loot effects (faithless looting, cathartic reunion) on one attacker draw multiple cards. The builder frames it as a fair Tempo deck for 1v1-style aggression inside Commander: rip through cards, deploy cheap evasive beaters (Ragavan, Malcolm, Slither Blade, DRC), and pump them into card-drawing connects rather than relying on big combo turns. The shell leans on rummaging/looting, cycling lands and spells for cheap card flow, mana rocks for ramp, Phoenix-style recursion, and pirates. Payoffs like Ral, Monster of Endurance, and artistic talent amplify discard triggers. Counterspells that discard to cast for free (misdirection) fit naturally. Playtesting showed the deck is heavily mana-dependent and clunky early, strong once online; he trims toward a leaner ~33-land, low-curve build and adds fast mana (Lotus Petal) to deploy threats sooner.
