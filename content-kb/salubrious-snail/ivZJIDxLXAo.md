## Summary

The video advises how to place "pet cards" (cards a player loves) in Commander/EDH decks. It sorts pet cards into three groups. (1) Bad or hyper-specific cards: build a dedicated deck around their aesthetic or effect, accepting it won't be strong. (2) Cards that are simply good/flexible (examples: Dawn Charm, Swan Song, Kambal Consul of Allocation, and Shigeki—run in two ramp-heavy green decks for uncounterable instant-speed recursion): drop them into any deck of their colors. (3) Moderately powerful, somewhat niche cards—the hard middle—have two options. Option A: build a whole deck around one or two of them (the host did this for Astral Slide, Maze's End, and Time Sieve). Option B: put several into one flexible deck with a generic game plan; examples include a midrange Radha deck wanting 6-7 drops and 4-cost ramp, Hans's Jensen Carthalion five-color beatdown, and a low-power proliferate deck (Skeleton Ship) holding Overwhelming Intellect, Mind Unbound, Parallax enchantments, Grimoire of the Dead, storage lands; scalable up to Atraxa. Stated rule: the more generic a deck's plan, the more niche its card choices can be. Ensure a solid core (lands, ramp, draw) plus interaction—protection and removal. The host favors budget deckbuilding to serve many pet cards
