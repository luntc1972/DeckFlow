## Summary

On the Magic Mirror podcast, hosts Con (Trinket Mage), Salubrious Snail, Alex, and guest Tomer present Commander decks built from random wheel spins setting a budget, a required "spicy" core card, and a restriction. Alex's $1200 deck uses The Unspeakable plus 30+ Kamigawa-block cards as an arcane reanimator: Buried Alive/Living Death/Animate Dead fill and revive the graveyard, while Cunning Bandit, Goblin Cohort, and Eternal Dominion copy arcane spells. Con's $60 deck pairs Sibsig Ceremony with Athreos, Shroud-Veiled, leaning on indestructible Myojins and ETB/death creatures to satisfy a 50-subtype requirement. Salubrious Snail's $1200 Yidris four-color deck cascades into Living End/Living Death, cycling and discarding creatures to mass-reanimate, with 20 token types and treasure ramp from discard payoffs. Khan's $60 deck runs 25 copies of Tempest Hawk under Zahor, Glory's Past, aggroing opponents then comboing Axis of Mortality with Tainted Remedy to swap life totals for a kill. Discussion covers brackets, graveyard hate, Tempest Hawk synergy with pitch spells, and Secret Salvage. Q&A touches mechanics players have brewed (curses, level-up, fading), cards they'd reprint cheaply, Spell Table etiquette, the One Ring's design, and why prerelease and product pricing keeps rising for stores.
