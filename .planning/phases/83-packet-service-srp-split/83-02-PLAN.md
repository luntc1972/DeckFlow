- Cluster F — the four combo-reference formatters. Leave as-is.
- Cluster G — NormalizeOracleText / CollapseWhitespace (Analysis full vs Comparison oracle-only vs
  Primer char-by-char). Leave as-is.
- MetaGap's two decklist builders (BuildCanonicalDecklistText / BuildCompactDecklist) are a
  different shape/purpose — they are NOT callers of BuildSectionedDecklistText.
