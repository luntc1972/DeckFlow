### D-21 — every `FunctionalTwins` finding merges into ONE displayed section

Research open question 5 left this unresolved and tied it directly to Success Criterion 5. Absent a
merge case, `CutLabFindingPresenter.BuildFindingGroups` gives each finding its own
`CutLabFindingGroupView`, and `CutLab.cshtml` renders one `cutlab-finding` block per group — so a pool
with four qualifying twin groups would show four separate blocks each headed "Functional twins".

**Decision: merge, exactly as `WeakFloorCase` and `ComboProtected` already do.** One section headed
"Functional twins", containing one `Item` per group, each with its own lead and its own evidence
chips.

Rationale: twins is the most frequently-firing new kind by construction — it iterates eight roles
rather than evaluating a single condition — so it is the kind most likely to flood the panel. The merge
precedent already exists for exactly this reason on the two other multi-instance kinds, and reusing it
means **zero new Razor markup**: `CutLab.cshtml` already loops `findingGroup.Items` and already renders
lock-state and combo-badge chips per evidence entry. This is why plan 04-02 required a constant
`"Functional twins"` heading — `BuildFindingGroups` takes the section heading from `items[0].Heading`.

### D-22 — density is validated twice: an automated bound AND a blocking human check

Neither alone is sufficient.

The automated test is a **regression guard with a numeric bound**. It cannot prove "reviewable" — that
is a human judgement about a real deck — but it converts the bound into something that fails loudly if
a future change makes the detector fire ten times more often.

The human check is the **authoritative Criterion 5 gate**, matching the Phase 3 Task 4 precedent and
the ROADMAP's release posture for this phase ("Deploy dark, flip after UAT on a real pool"). A
synthetic fixture cannot capture the correlations of a real list — a real cEDH deck's mana rocks
genuinely cluster at mana value 2, and whether that reads as insight or as noise is exactly the
question.

The automated bound is deliberately set as an **upper** bound with headroom rather than an exact
count, so it does not become a brittle pin that has to be edited on every unrelated fixture tweak.

The existing code records each merged kind's insert index as `groups.Count` at first occurrence —
a count of *non-merged* groups only — then inserts the accumulated sections sequentially in
ascending order at the end. Each insert shifts every later position by one, so the inserts land in

### F-04-01 — the five pre-existing detectors include locked and commander cards in their evidence (D-15 scope fence)

**The leak.** `CutLabAnalysisContextBuilder.BuildAsync` emits one `CutLabAnalyzedCard` per working-list
entry **unconditionally** — `DeckFlow.Web/Services/CutLab/CutLabAnalysisContextBuilder.cs:199-242`, the
`foreach (CutLabPoolCard entry in workingList)` loop, with the construction site at `:233-241`. Locked
cards and the commander are in `context.AnalyzedCards` exactly like any other card.

**The consequence.** `ComputeCurveCongestion`
(`DeckFlow.Web/Services/CutLab/CutLabStructuralFindings.cs:179-205`), `ComputeStrandedSubthemes`
(`:207-238`), `ComputeRedundantFinishers` (`:240-257`), `ComputeWeakFloorCases` (`:259-282`) and
`ComputeComboProtected` (`:303-385`) therefore all count and name locked cards and the commander in
their leads and evidence. A user sees the commander listed as evidence for curve congestion even
though the commander can never be cut.

**Where the filtering actually happens today.** Only downstream, in `BuildQueue`'s `eligibleCards`
filter — `DeckFlow.Web/Services/CutLab/CutLabCutRoundEngine.cs:219-226`, the
`!card.IsLocked && !card.IsCommander && !accepted && Quantity <= cardsRemainingToTarget` predicate.
That governs the **proposal queue**, not finding computation.

**Why it was left.** Narrowing five shipped detectors would change every one of their leads, evidence
lists and role counts, and would change the discriminating tally for `CurveCongestion` and
`StrandedSubtheme` — a ranking change outside TWIN-01..04, in the cycle's highest-blast-radius phase,
with no flag of its own. Plan 04-01 Task 2 added `IsLocked` and `IsCommander` to
`CutLabAnalyzedCard`, so the data is now available whenever this is picked up; plan 04-01 Task 3's
`Compute_NewAnalyzedCardMembers_DoNotAffectExistingDetectors` test is the fence that will fail the
moment someone starts, which is the intended signal rather than an obstacle.

**Recommended shape when taken up:** its own phase with its own flag, and it needs a product decision
first — a locked card arguably *should* still count toward curve congestion (it occupies a slot and
does affect the curve) even though it should not be *evidence* for a cut. "Count but do not name" may
be the right answer for some detectors and "exclude entirely" for others, per detector. That
per-detector judgement is the work.
