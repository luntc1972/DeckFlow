<objective>
Turn the resolved plan affinity into two of the four engine effects: reorder the proposal queue so
off-plan cards surface first and on-plan cards are demoted, and add the stranded-off-plan-package
structural finding.

Purpose: this is the phase's highest-blast-radius change — it decides which card Cut Lab proposes
next. It must be provably inert when no plan is selected.
Output: two edited engine files and two new test files. No HTTP, no DI, no UI.
</objective>
