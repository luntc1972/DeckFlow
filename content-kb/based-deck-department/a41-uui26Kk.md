## Summary

Core deckbuilding principle: relentless focus. An average Magic deck can reliably do about five things well; adding more trades reliability for whimsy or greed, and reliability is what makes a deck good and feel good. The more those five things synergize, the better the deck plays and executes its plan. Build by starting with the end in mind, then use focus to reach that endgame vision. Example: a Vincent (Matt Mercer) commander deck aiming to deal heavy commander damage. Its five focused pillars: (1) low-cost creatures with evasion to grow the commander; (2) pump spells, ideally granting evasion too; (3) damage multipliersΓÇödouble strike, power doubling, extra combats; (4) protection so the commander survives to attack. Focus produces a fast, powerful mono-red deck that normally doesn't exist in Commander. A deck is a finite resourceΓÇögetting one thing means giving up another; a whimsical 'mystical pile' is only fun when it still functions. Worst outcome is a whimsical deck that doesn't work.
