## Summary

This is the first episode of Slowplay, a Cubrious Snail-hosted gameplay show, featuring a four-player budget Commander game on Tabletop Simulator between four archetypes. Trinket Mage runs an aggro artifacts deck helmed by Imskir Iron Eater, which draws based on artifacts controlled on entry. 33 Elk plays control led by Cormela, Glamour Thief, who supplies mana for big spells. The host runs a fast midrange deck under Duskana, the Rage Mother, rewarding playing two creatures with card advantage and stats while attacking. Hans plays combo with the Jolly Balloon Man, who taps to make one-turn token copies of creatures. In play, the artifact aggro deck snowballs with cards like Cranial Ram, Night Scythe, and a Sojourner's Companion buffed by artifact count, repeatedly losing and drawing four off its obscure draw engine. Prismari Command does heavy lifting for the control player, killing creatures and making treasure. Gatakig Tech effectively mind-twists a player, locking them out. The Balloon Man deck attempts an infinite Village Bell Ringer copy loop via Hedron Crawler, but Gatakig answers it. The game ends messy and unresolved.
