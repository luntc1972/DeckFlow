## Summary

A precon review of Edge of Eternities' World Shaper deck, commanded by Hearthhull, the Worldseed—a 4-mana Jund Spacecraft. Once given two power it taps to sacrifice a land, draw two cards, and gain an extra land play; at eight charge counters it becomes a 6/7 flier that drains each opponent two life per land sacrificed. The deck's core engine sacrifices and recurs lands (six recursion cards, ten budget fetches, 13 land-sac cards, 42 lands total) to build mana while triggering three payoff types: landfall, permanent-sacrifice (Korvold, Mayhem Devil), and lands-in-graveyard checks. Strengths: a high-quality, fetch-dense manabase with tango/bicycle duals, a strong removal suite, and payoffs with high floors so newer players stay engaged. Weaknesses: weak early-game utility and too much sacrifice payoff redundant with the commander. The reviewer makes 10 swaps—cutting clunky cards (Loamcrafter Faun, Scouring Swarm, World Breaker, Mazirek, Centaur Vinecrasher) and adding Spelunking, Lotus Cobra, Tireless Provisioner, Valakut Exploration, early two-drops, Undergrowth Recon, and Hugs—plus land swaps for Twisted Landscape and Kessig Wolf Run. Overall a strong, creative land-fiddling precon needing only minor rebalancing.
