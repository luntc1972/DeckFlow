## Summary

This video responds to viewer comments disagreeing with the creator's earlier defense of the Eminence mechanic in Commander. The creator addresses four critiques. First, the claim that Eminence is inherently unfair because it grants a free, every-turn, uninteractable advantage: he counters that a traditional command zone is itself a powerful passive effect—a free tutor and permanent eighth card—so Eminence is not uniquely unfair. Second, the suggestion that Eminence should only work from the command zone (lost once cast): he rejects this, arguing good Eminence cards like Sidar Jabari of Zhalfir are balanced as a cohesive creature-plus-effect package, and that Edgar Markov's problem is its overpowered token-spewing effect, not having both halves. Third, gating Eminence behind casting the commander: he opposes this because the two-tier early/late design (Arahbo, Sidar) depends on the effect being available from turn one. Fourth, the strongest critique—uninteractivity: he concedes effects should have interactable moving parts (board requirements), but urges players to be flexible, attack other axes (life, hand, library), and keep aggro backup plans. He remains pro-Eminence and optimistic about future designs.
