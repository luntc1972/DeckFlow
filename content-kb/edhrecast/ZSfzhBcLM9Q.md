## Summary

This EDHREC video investigates which Commander is the least popular in the format, showing the question is hard to quantify. The biggest complication is partner mechanics and variants (backgrounds, friends forever, doctor's companions), which create huge numbers of pairings; 283 partner/background combinations have zero decks, a massive tie. Unpartnered single legends also technically qualify (e.g., Kaalia solo has 21 decks, Halden solo has one, Prismatic Piper alone is legal), further muddying results. The host argues partner pairings shouldn't count because individual pieces stay popular in other combos (e.g., Baral pairs poorly with Inspiring Leader but thrives with Folk Hero). Filtering to standalone, non-paired legends with the fewest decks surfaces very new cards (Spider-Man/TMNT/Avatar draft cards, Valor's Flagship), but recency and rules changes disqualify them since data shifts daily. The settled answer is Zhao Zilong from Portal Three Kingdoms with 12 decks: a five-mana 3/3 with horsemanship and weak non-synergistic abilities, mono-white (historically under-supported), from an obscure expensive set (~$100). The conclusion: the answer depends on which criteria you accept, and publicizing it may itself increase his deck count.

## Key Clips

- The biggest hurdle about tracking the least popular legends is the partner mechanic and all its variations. There are 56 legendary creatures or eligible planeswalker commander options with the original partner keyword, which means 1,540 possible partner pairings.
- Some combinations are such duds that they have garnered zero total decks across all the deck-building websites EDHREC draws data from. The zero-deck partner combos are a 283-way tie of pair-up combinations no one has ever built around.
- Every standalone (non-partner) commander has at least one deck in the EDHREC database. None have zero decks, and none have just one deck, but some have just two — like Stockman, and Mikey and Mona, Mutant Sitters.
- EDHREC undergoes daily refreshes and data pulls, so it's a constantly moving target. Newer commanders from recent sets (Spider-Man, TMNT, Avatar's Pipsqueak) are excluded as least-popular because they're too new to have settled.
- Valor's Flagship from Aetherdrift was rejected because it was only eligible to be a commander after a June 2025 rules change in Edge of Eternities allowed legendary vehicles to be commanders — only 11 months of eligibility.
- The final answer for the least popular standalone, established commander is Zhao Zilong from Portal Three Kingdoms, with 12 decks. A five-mana 3/3 with horsemanship that gives himself +1 when he blocks.
- Reasons Zhao Zilong ranks lowest: two bland abilities that don't cooperate or give a build-around hook, it's a mono-colored (mono-white) deck which is an uphill battle, it's from an obscure set, and the card costs around $100, hurting accessibility.
