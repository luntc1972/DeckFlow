## Summary

The creator walks through building their $70 budget Meria, Scholar of Antiquity Commander deck, which pivots from the standard zero-and-one-cost artifact storm shell toward equipmentsΓÇöexploiting Magic's arbitrary rule that artifacts can tap, so tapped equipments feed Meria's value engine. Because Meria is central and a 3-mana green commander, they devote 12 slots (~$35, half the budget) to protection: six protection equipments plus instant-speed and creature-based protection, keeping the commander alive without babysitting. Equipment-synergy creatures (Goblin Gaveleer, Champion of the Flame, Valduk, Chishiro, Armory Automaton) round out the base, while the commander itself serves as the default equipment bearer thanks to commander damage. Ramp is artifact-based treasure generators like Goldvein Pick to hold up interaction and recast costs; removal leans on six burn spells plus artifact hate. Combat equipments split into damage amplifiers (buffs/doublers enabling two-shots) and evasion (unblockable, flying, evasive lands). The result is a fast, flexible voltron deck killing players by turn six or seven, weak to sacrifice and board wipes but resilient via high card velocity. Takeaways: keep deckbuilding fluid, balance protection/ramp/draw/removal ratios, and favor flexible multi-role cards.
