## Summary

This video reviews the Aetherdrift Eternal Might precon, commanded by Temmet, a five-mana Esper 4/4 that draws-then-discards on attack and pumps your zombies +1/+1 whenever you draw. Temmet wants a wide board of small zombies plus bursty pre-combat draw and ample mana to cast him and protect him. The precon is criticized as incoherent: it scatters across conflicting subthemes (tokens, discard for the alt-commander Hashaton, graveyard/death triggers) without committing to any. It lacks enough discard to enable Hashaton, lacks sacrifice outlets to feed its graveyard payoffs (making Gravecrawler dead), leans on oversized 4/4 tokens instead of the small tokens Temmet wants, and offers little bursty draw, no counterspells, and weak protection despite being a blue deck vulnerable to removal and wraths. Praised elements: above-average ramp (Crowded Crypt, Bontu's Monument), an excellent manabase, good 1/1 token makers, and clean fits like Forgotten Creation, Prophet of the Scarab, and Rhet-Tomb Mystic. The reviewer's 15-card upgrade keeps Temmet and adds big mass-draw zombie pumps (Keep Watch, Distant Melody, Pact of the Serpent, Raffine, Varina), flexible draw-protection, more small-token and removal-token makers, and Cephalid Coliseum for closing draw bursts.

## Key Clips

- The commander of this deck is Temmet, a five mana 4/4 in Esper colors with vigilance and menace: 'Whenever you attack, draw a card, then discard a card. Whenever you draw a card, zombies you control get +1/+1 until end of turn.' To be maximally effective, Temmet wants a large board of zombies to attack with as well as a large draw spell to amp up the buff it grants.
- In terms of construction this deck is a hot mess express, a disgusting mishmash of conflicting gameplans and synergistic dead-ends. It fails to meaningfully commit to any of its subthemes, and with the way it's currently built its command zone feels arbitrary at best, just a generic zombie guy that gives the deck its colors.
- The alternate commander for this deck is Hashaton, Scarab's Fist, which can make 4/4 zombie copies of discarded creatures, but this deck just doesn't have enough discard to consistently enable Hashaton's ability. The discard consists of only five or six cards that allow you to discard plus a handful of cycling cards.
- This deck is a rare Gravecrawler L—with no sacrifice, this card just doesn't really do anything. Most zombie decks would be using sacrifice abilities in addition to discard, but this deck is almost entirely lacking in ways to sacrifice creatures, so it lacks a lot of what might give a zombie deck a sense of mechanical identity.
- This deck doesn't have much of the sort of bursty draw that pairs best with Temmet. Most of the other draw options are mana expensive, so you're looking at 10 mana minimum to do a Temmet + draw maneuver. There's very little protection and no counterspells—a blue deck that's vulnerable to removal and board wipes runs no counterspells.
- The manabase here is excellent—really robust symbol counts even with a couple great colorless utility lands, and a moderately high but not overwhelming tapland density. The deck has a healthy 9 ramp spells; Crowded Crypt and Bontu's Monument both stand out as fun ramp options offering token production and drain respectively.
- For the upgrade, sticking with Temmet over Hashaton via 15 swap-outs: add draw and filtering like Keep Watch, Distant Melody, Pact of the Serpent, and Raffine that give zombies a massive pump for reasonable mana. Varina stands out as absurdly good in a Temmet deck. Add zombie token makers Syphon Flesh, Dark Salvation, and Endless Ranks of the Dead, plus Cephalid Coliseum for a burst of draw triggers.
