## Summary

This video showcases Anim Pakal as a commander that creates Battle Gnome artifact tokens whenever you attack with one or more non-Gnome creatures, generating tokens equal to the number of +1/+1 counters on Anim Pakal, with each attack making the commander stronger. Extra-combat effects like Aurelia, the War Leader and Aggravated Assault (Aki Battle Squad) amplify the strategy. To add more +1/+1 counters, the deck leans on counter-theme cards such as Citadel Siege, Angelic Intervention, and Rosie Cotton of South Lane, plus proliferate enablers like Grateful Apparition.
