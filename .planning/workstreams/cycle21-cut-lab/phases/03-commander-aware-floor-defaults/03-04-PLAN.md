<objective>
Make Cut Lab's role-floor defaults commander-aware: `ResolveDefaults` consults the commander role-floor
provider for the six GO roles and takes `max(bracket, commander)`, while `CutLabResolvedFloor` grows to
carry the bracket and commander numbers separately so the UI can show both.

Purpose: this is RFLR-05 as amended by D-04. The bracket bands are prescriptive product opinion — they are
literally marked `[ASSUMED] ... awaiting product sign-off` — while p25 is descriptive of what people
actually build. `max()` is the reconciliation, and it never weakens a guardrail.
Output: commander-raised floors in the resolver, with today's behavior byte-identical everywhere the
commander data is absent.
</objective>
