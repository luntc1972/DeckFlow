## Summary

Go, Master of Mimicry is a three-mana monoblue commander whose only ability taps and pays XX to copy an activated or triggered ability you control X times (X can't be zero, ability can't be copied itself). Commander is full of strong activated/triggered abilities, so targets are trivial to find. This build is intentionally bracket 2.5: no game changers, no infinites, ~$50 budget. Core plan: copy fetchland abilities (Flood Plain, Bad River, evolving wilds, broker's hideout) for repeatable rampant growth, supported by 22 islands and cycle lands for card draw. Mana dorks that tap specifically for activated abilities (Omen Hawker, Enigma Jewel, Pit Automaton, Sage of the Unknowable) plus Training Grounds and Dreamscape Artist enable explosive turns four and five. Blue land ramp (Dowsing Dagger, Realms Sketching, Solemn Simulacrum) keeps lands flowing. Win conditions are diverse: Extravagant Replication token armies, Fleet Swallower mill, extra-turn upkeep value via Sundial of the Infinite-style triggers, Curse of Unbinding theft, and Myojin copy effects. Closes with sweepers, interaction, and Born Upon a Wind for flash recursion. In the recorded game, copying Fleet Swallower's attack trigger milled out an opponent for the win.

## Key Clips

- Go master of mimicry is a three mana monoblue commander with only a single ability. By tapping him and paying xx mana, we can copy target activated or triggered ability we control x times. This ability itself can't be copied and x can't be zero.
- The deck I've built today is very much intended to be a bracket 2.5 deck. No game changers, no infinites, and around a 50 budget. I don't want to loop go and make the poor guy dizzy. I want to explore the cool things he can do.
- Go's ability lets us copy the activated and triggered abilities of fetchlands like evolving wilds and broker's hideout. He can copy the same fetch ability multiple times. For two mana, we copy it once and for four mana twice. This is basically a repeatable and replicable rampant growth. Go is an incredible ramp commander.
- Blue has access to a variety of low-cost cards that tap for mana specifically to be used for activated abilities like Omen Hawker, the Enigma Jewel, Pit Automaton, and Sage of the Unknowable. They give us something to do in the early game while setting up explosive turns four and five.
- Dreamscape Artist has a very powerful ability that is balanced by a high activation cost which makes it a perfect target for duplication since we don't have to pay that cost again. We pay three mana, tap it, discard a card, and sacrifice a land to search our deck for two basic lands and put them into play untapped.
- Our deck is absolutely filled with a wide range of completely different win conditions. Extravagant replication creates a token copy of a nonland permanent we control during each of our upkeeps. Duplicating this trigger allows us to create an army. When fleet swallower attacks, we put a trigger on the stack that mills half the deck of target opponent. Copy that a couple of times with go and they're gone.
- Curse of Unbinding is a seven mana enchantment that at the beginning of the enchanted player's upkeep lets us reveal cards from that player's deck until we find a creature and then we put it into play under our control. Duplicate it with extravagant replication and scatter the curses around to completely lose track of who owns which cards.
- Mujin of Cryptic Dreams lets us copy target permanent spell we control three times. Since this is an activated ability, Go can duplicate the three-times duplication, creating 600 copies of a permanent spell. They both enter with a counter that makes them indestructible if they were cast from hand.
