## Summary

This is a budget four-color Commander deck pairing Saheeli, the Sun's Brilliance with Wyleth as a rule-zero partner pair, themed humorously around dinosaur-loving girlfriends. The deck runs 32 dinosaurs; Saheeli copies them to multiply effects, while Wyleth tutors a basic land (useful given only one or two Islands) and can flip into a saga that makes Dino tokens, animates creatures, tutors a dinosaur, and pumps the team before sacrificing itself.
