## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| creator corpus → whitelist | Crawled deck data (possibly containing since-banned or off-color cards) becomes "safe to suggest" content unless re-validated |
| whitelist → ChatGPT packet | The whitelist is the ONLY constraint on the external LLM's swap suggestions (D-08) — a leaked bad card there ships unguarded |
