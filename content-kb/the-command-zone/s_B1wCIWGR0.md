## Summary

The Command Zone hosts Brandon and John count down their top 10 Commander (EDH) lands. #10 Command Tower fixes any of your commander's colors, is cheap (~$1), and dodges land destruction. #9 Ancient Tomb produces two colorless mana for negligible damage, enabling turn-two ramp like Coalition Relic or Cultivate. #8 Cabal Coffers plus Urborg turn lands into Swamps and generate black mana equal to your Swamps. #7 recursion lands Volrath's Stronghold and Academy Ruins return creatures or artifacts for repeated value. #6 Gaea's Cradle taps for huge mana in creature-heavy green decks. #5 protection lands Kor Haven and Maze of Ith blunt attackers, with Maze rated stronger for color-independence. #4 Wasteland and Strip Mine destroy problem lands while still tapping for mana. #3 shock lands offer two basic land types, fetchable, entering untapped for two life. #2 fetchlands grab basics, shocks, or duals for one life and fit any deck sharing a land type. #1 the original dual lands do everything fetches do, enter untapped without damage, and are reserve-list, so they keep rising in price; shocks are the budget substitute.

## Key Clips

- Command Tower is number 10. Produces mana of any color your commander can produce, and can't be affected by traditional fetch lands or fetch spells. Great for hitting all colors on a two-, three-, or five-color deck, and very cheap at about a dollar to a dollar fifty.
- Ancient Tomb is number 9 — the most powerful colorless mana source you can have in EDH. The 2 damage is negligible, and it lets you play cultivate, Kodama's Reach, or a coalition relic on turn two. Unlike City of Traitors, it doesn't sacrifice itself when another land enters.
- Number 8 is the combination of Cabal Coffers and Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth. Urborg turns all lands into Swamps; Cabal Coffers taps plus colorless to produce black mana equal to the number of Swamps you control. With seven lands you generate seven mana from one activation.
- Number 7 is the recursion lands: Volrath's Stronghold and Academy Ruins. Volrath's Stronghold returns creatures to the top of your library; Academy Ruins recurs mana artifacts and equipment. Pair Academy Ruins with Mindslaver to lock the table.
- Number 6 is Gaea's Cradle — the epitome of efficient mana in a green creature deck. It taps for green mana equal to the number of creatures you control, so with a board it can make five or ten mana for free, acting like a Gilded Lotus or better. Price has been pushed up to around $130-$140 by Legacy Elves.
- Number 4 is Wasteland and Strip Mine — two of the best lands ever produced. They produce their own color/mana so you don't miss a land drop, and they let you destroy an opponent's key land. Strip Mine is banned in every format except Vintage and is only a couple dollars; Wasteland is a Legacy staple at about $100.
- Number 3 is the shock lands — ten dual-typed lands that can be fetched by forest/island/mountain fetch effects like Farseek or fetch lands. They enter untapped if you pay 2 life, which is how Wizards made dual lands fair.
- Number 1 is the original dual lands — the only lands on the list on the Reserved List, so they'll never be reprinted. They have two basic land types, can be fetched by Onslaught/Zendikar fetches and Farseek, and enter untapped with no damage, making them strictly better than fetches and shocks but far more expensive.
