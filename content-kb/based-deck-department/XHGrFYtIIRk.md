## Summary

The video lays out non-negotiable Commander/cEDH deckbuilding fundamentals ('eat your vegetables'). Core quotas: run at least 38 lands (MDFCs count, but treat them as spells only when they'd be dead draws). Run at least 10 sources of mana acceleration—ramp spells, mana dorks, or mana rocks—so you aren't left behind opponents who have them. Run at least 10 forms of single-target interaction; these need not be staple instants—sorceries or creatures with attached removal count—so long as you can disrupt opponents' plans. Run at least 10 sources of card advantage, noting it takes many forms: a board wipe, a Fact or Fiction, or a Sylvan Library-style effect ("a six") all qualify. Run at least 2 board wipes, ideally asymmetrical ones (host references a separate board wipe video for the best asymmetrical options). These rules are essentially mandatory: only about one deck in a collection should break them, and even the host says he regrets breaking his own rules ~90% of the time. Skimping on fundamentals makes decks slow, inconsistent, and prone to losing.
