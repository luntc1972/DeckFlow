## Summary

This video explores building a Commander "resistance" deck themed around allies, inspired by Magic's Avatar set and the ally theme first seen on Zendikar. It argues resistance is expressed mechanically through three pillars. First, synergy: Zendikar allies (Akoum Battlesinger, cohort cards, Hero of Goma Fada, Lantern Scout) and Avatar allies (Katara the Fearless, Sokka, Great Divide Guide) strengthen each other while keeping individual identity. Second, sacrifice/martyrdom from Aether Revolt's revolt mechanic (Vengeful Rebel, Solemn Recruit, Renegade Rallier, Call for Unity, Cradle of Vitality-style payoffs, Promise of Loyalty, Breath of Fury, Smile at Death, Glare of Subdual, Opposition) turns expendable ally tokens into meaningful losses that empower the board. Third, battlecry/inspiration from Mirrodin and New Phyrexia (Hero of Bladehold, Roar of Resistance, Gerrard, Weatherlight Hero) to rally attackers and protect against board wipes. Suggested commanders are Sidar Kondo of Jamuraa (granting evasion to small tokens, enabling Toski, Kindred Discovery) and Lavinia/Livaan-style partner Levvic for card draw. The creator stresses the commander is interchangeable; the deck's identity comes from devotion and sacrifice.
