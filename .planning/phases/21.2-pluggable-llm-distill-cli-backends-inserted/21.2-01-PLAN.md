| T-21.2-01 | Tampering / Injection | `BuildCommandSpec` / ProcessStartInfo.ArgumentList | mitigate | Command parts come ONLY from per-provider const defaults or the operator-set `DECKFLOW_LLM_CLI_COMMAND`. Transcript content is NEVER added to FileName/ArgumentList (it goes to stdin).
