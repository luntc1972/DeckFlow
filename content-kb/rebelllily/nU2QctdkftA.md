## Summary

This is a goldfish demonstration of a budget (under $50) coin-flip Commander precon built around Krark, the Thumbless, made in collaboration with the Professor (Tolarian Community College). The deck's core loop: cast cheap instants and sorceries, and Krark's trigger lets you flip a coin to copy or recast each spell. Mulligan guidance is simple—keep any two lands (one red) plus mana and spells, then start flipping. Key pieces shown include Stormchaser Drake (a recurring draw/bounce target), Zada, Hedron Grinder (which copies single-target spells onto your whole board), and treasure-generating spells like Involuntary Employment to ramp explosively. Spells such as Frantic Search, Prize Fight, Otherworldly Outburst, and Monstrous Rage get repeatedly copied via Krark flips, generating card draw, treasures, and untapped lands to chain into long turns. The pilot demonstrates building up many treasures and spell copies to fuel a big turn that ends by casting a Lava Loss-style sweeper to clear opponents' creatures. The deck is presented as chaotic, swingy, and reliant on luck—taking long turns that can fizzle—but fun and powerful for casual play.
