## Summary

A streamer reviews Magic: The Gathering Commander decks submitted via Reddit and chat. First deck: a Zimone, Paradox Sculptor +1/+1 counter / Hydra deck. The builder found it clunky and slow, doing nothing until turn five, so he overhauled it (~24 cards): adding mana dorks (Elvish Mystic, Birds, Boreal Druid) and rocks for early ramp, card-draw payoffs (Fathom Mage, Danny Pink, Inga and Esika), Hardened Scales, more counterspells, and X-spell Hydras, while trimming win-more doublers like Master Biomancer and Vorinclex. Second deck: a Saramov-style mill/control/theft commander that mills two and steals a spell on the second cast each turn. He rebuilt it as a slow, mana-hungry Esper control deck—heavy ramp, 38 lands, counterspells, board wipes, cantrips, and steal/reanimation finishers (Breach the Multiverse). Key additions: Roaming Throne to copy the commander trigger, Unwinding Clock, Border Post and alternative-cost spells (Gush, Commandeer, Snuff Out) since the deck builds no graveyard for delve. He stresses control decks need lots of mana because their answers are inefficient. Goldfishing repeatedly confirmed the commander arrives late but pays off once online. He prioritized draw over mill, calling both finished decks solid.

## Key Clips

- The stream's premise: going on Reddit to find decks people need help building, then fixing them live — budgeting roughly 30-40 minutes per deck to keep moving.
- On the Zaxara/Paradox Sculptor +1/+1 counter Hydra deck: goldfishing showed it does almost nothing until turn five — the X-cost Hydras are too clunky in hand, so the fix is adding more mana early via dorks (Elvish Mystic, Birds, Boreal Druid) and rocks/signets.
- Combo idea for the Hydra deck: Evolution Witness returns a permanent from graveyard to hand each time one or more +1/+1 counters are placed — pair it with channel/cycling permanents (Tanuki, Mirror-shell Crab) so every counter recurs a free card.
- Win-more discussion: doublers like Doubling Season aren't useless because 'your deck isn't doing enough in the first place' — you need those payoffs to hit the scale to win. But Master Biomancer and Vorel are cut here since the commander already adds and doubles counters.
- Control deck theory for the Saramon mill build: control spells are inefficient (a counterspell costs double a Lightning Bolt), and going one-for-one is extra bad at a 4-player table, so a Commander control deck must run a very mana-hungry shell with lots of ramp and board wipes, not just counters.
- The Saramon deck needs 38 lands plus rocks because it must hit every land drop as a control deck and its commander is slow to come online — bricking on lands repeatedly during goldfishing proved the land count can't be cut.
- Breakthrough for the Saramon engine: lean on alternative-cost and free spells (Border Post, Misdirection, Snapcaster effects) to trigger the cast-second-spell ability cheaply, and run Roaming Throne to double the commander's triggers.
- Closing: two decks rescued in about an hour (longer than the planned 30-minute fixes); future deck reviews to be routed through Discord, with all built lists posted in the video description and Moxfield.
