<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Phase 110 shipped the reusable **card disclosure component** (an anchored popover + inline
`<details>` under Structural evidence chips) and the pool-discovery UX. Phase 110.1 is the
**data layer** that fills that disclosure with combo intelligence:

- Distinguish, per Structural evidence card, whether it belongs to a **complete combo**
  ("Combo piece") or a **near-combo** missing one partner ("Needs {MissingCard}"), including
  weak-floor cases where combo context is the reason a card is protected. (CLUP-17)
- Badge matched Structural evidence chips with a combo-state badge and expose the combo
  role/context inside the Phase 110 disclosure, **without changing** the canonical
  lock/unlock behavior of those chips. (CLUP-18)
- Group near-combos that differ only by one interchangeable card into a **single finding**
  that lists the alternatives, instead of one finding per variant.

**This phase is data-layer-primary.** Net-new UI is minimal: one combo-state badge (two
states) rendered on existing chips, plus combo role/context text inside the **existing**
110 disclosure. It reuses Phase 110's disclosure component and `110-UI-SPEC.md` — no new
UI-SPEC (user decision 2026-07-24: `--skip-ui`, reuse 110's spec). The badge must remain
readable across all themes; theme-token/CSS coverage is a planned task and is re-verified by
the Phase 111 regression gate.

**Out of scope (explicitly deferred — see `<deferred>`):** Spellbook template-slot candidate
matching (enumerating the "37 cards"), combo-aware cut-round *engine* ranking, and a full
per-variant browser with EDHREC deck counts.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions (LOCKED)

### Combo evidence depth — badge + disclosure detail
- Chips get **badge + disclosure detail**, NOT badge-only. Badge-only fails CLUP-18's
  "show the relevant combo role/context" requirement.
- **Two distinct badge states**, not a single generic badge:
  - **"Combo piece"** — card is part of a *complete* combo present in the pool.
  - **"Needs {MissingCard}"** — card is part of a *near-combo* missing exactly one partner.
- The combo role/context (what the combo does, the card's role in it, the missing partner
  for near-combos) renders **inside the Phase 110 disclosure** for that chip — same
  anchored popover / inline `<details>` component, no second UI surface.

### New combo-protected finding kind
- Introduce a **new "combo-protected" finding kind** with its own heading and grouping in
  the Structural findings section — do NOT overload the existing Structural finding shape.
- This finding kind is where near-combo grouping (below) is expressed.

### Thread full `IncludedCombos` (replace the name-only HashSet)
- Thread the **full `IncludedCombos` / `AlmostIncludedCombos`** data into a
  **card→combo lookup** that replaces the current name-only HashSet. This lets a chip's
  card resolve to its combo(s), role, results, and missing partner.
- The current classification path only knows combo *names*; that caps what CLUP-18 can show.

### Near-combo variant grouping
- Near-combos that differ **only by one interchangeable card** are grouped into **one
  finding listing the alternatives**, rather than one finding per variant. (CLUP-17 / SC-3)

### "Don't cut a complete combo in round 1" — advisory copy ONLY
- If at least one full combo is complete, surface **advisory copy** ("cutting this in
  round 1 is inadvisable") — this is **copy only**.
- **Do NOT** add any rule to `CutLabCutRoundEngine`. No engine/ranking change this phase.
  (That engine rule is deferred to its own future phase.)

### Template slots / `requires` — research now, implement later
- Commander Spellbook exposes a **template slot** (screenshot: "Noncreature permanent
  castable for {0}" → *View 37 Cards*) via the `requires` field, which
  `CommanderSpellbookService` currently **drops**.
- This phase **researches and records** the `requires` template-slot shape and the variant
  grouping shape so candidate matching can be scoped as follow-on work. It does **not**
  implement candidate enumeration/matching. (CLUP-17 / SC-4)

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact record/DTO field names for the card→combo lookup and the new finding kind.
- Where the combo-state resolution lives (service vs presenter) — keep pure combo→card
  mapping testable in `DeckFlow.Web` or `DeckFlow.Core` per existing CutLab layering.
- Badge markup/token naming, as long as it reuses 110's disclosure and stays theme-readable.
- How advisory "don't cut in round 1" copy is attached to the finding (presenter-level).
</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- User supplied three Commander Spellbook screenshots during 110 discuss: a combo page with
  a **template slot** ("Noncreature permanent castable for {0}" → *View 37 Cards*) and a
  **"Variants of this combo"** view (4 variants sharing two cards, differing in the third,
  with EDHREC deck counts). These drove the split and define what "template slot" and
  "variant" mean here. The 37-card enumeration and per-variant EDHREC counts are OUT of scope;
  recording the API shapes that expose them is IN scope (research deliverable).
- Badge copy strings: **"Combo piece"** (complete) and **"Needs {MissingCard}"** (near-combo).
- Weak-floor case (CLUP-17): when a card sits below its role floor but is a combo piece, the
  combo-protected context is the reason it is protected — the finding must make that legible.
</specifics>
