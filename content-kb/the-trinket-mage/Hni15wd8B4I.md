## Summary

This stream is a set review of Magic's Marvel Super Heroes release. The creator first critiques Universes Beyond cohesion: the set is dominated by legendary creatures (128 legends vs 42 non-legends) that act as character references rather than telling a connected story, unlike in-universe sets (e.g., Rise of the Eldrazi, Lurrien) where cards collectively convey narrative. He praises the set's toned-down power level and strong art (especially comic-cover and panorama treatments). New mechanics covered: power-up (buffed monstrosity), teamwork (tap creatures of total power 4+, kicker variant), and hero/villain types whose value hinges on future support. Standout cards highlighted as strong or cEDH-playable: Arc Reactor, Cosmic Cube, Bruce Banner/Hulk, She-Hulk (Grand Abolisher), King T'Challa (Faerie Mastermind), Nick Fury, Scientist Supreme of A.I.M. (Salvaging Station commander), Thanos (board-wipe control), Thor God of Thunder (mono-red spellslinger), Tony Stark, Ultron, Sentry, and MODOK. He pans Galactus as overcosted/fragile. Precon commanders and the four-color cycle judged competent but unexciting; bonus-sheet reprints diluted by 60 cards of chaff. Overall rating: roughly five-to-six out of ten pending play.

## Key Clips

- **[31:02]** Arc Reactor — five mana improvise artifact, enters tapped, adds three colorless. Great in clue or food decks with dinky artifacts that don't tap for mana: tap five clues to play it. Even on curve, artifact turn one, artifact turn two, then turn three cast this. With untap effects it gets a billion times better. Legitimately a strong card for casual EDH.
- **[34:46]** Bruce Banner / Hulk MDFC: cast the front Bruce half to pay double-X, tap, draw X cards, or cast the back Incredible Hulk. As a commander it's a powerful team control commander — ramp into the Hulk, and if they remove him just play Bruce and draw cards. A control deck with a big finisher in the command zone. Counts both halves for color identity in the 99.
- **[37:37]** Cosmic Cube — five mana, ward 2, whenever you attack look at top six and cast a free spell with mana value up to your greatest attacker's power. Powerful value piece in the right deck; you can cast Sculpting Steel off it to copy the cube itself.
- **[39:25]** Daredevil as a Boros control commander: haste, look at top card any time, and a card-draw engine when he attacks and hits a hero card. Just make him evasive and swing — card advantage right in the command zone in Boros colors.
- **[53:41]** Modok — pay three life to connive, activate only on your turn, with lifelink. Effectively pay-three-life connive: with 40 life you draw and discard deep, looting through your deck. There's a storm build here, especially in Green Goblin giving your stuff mayhem so you loot into rituals. With lifegain you keep conniving.
- **[58:48]** Scientist Supreme of AIM — the one card from this set I'd put in the command zone. Pay two life to copy an activated or triggered ability from an artifact source you control, choosing new targets. It's essentially Deir-to-winos; a very strong salvaging-station commander.
- **[64:43]** Thanos — Mardu 4/4 deathtouch lifelink with a power-up that costs three less if he entered this turn, then board-wipes creatures by even/odd quantity. A powerful five-color blink control commander: blink him, board wipe, repeat, then Voltron him up to close. Easier to break parity than Child of Alara since he only kills creatures.
- **[72:16]** Thor, God of Thunder — when you cast a non-creature spell he deals damage equal to that spell's mana value to any target. Cast Blasphemous Act and deal nine damage to someone. A really cool mono-red spellslinger commander worth building around.
