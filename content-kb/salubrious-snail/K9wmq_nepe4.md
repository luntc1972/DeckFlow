## Summary

This video gives a framework for deciding what counts as "card draw" in Commander decks, arguing draw is really a web of effects serving two goals: spending excess mana and finding particular cards. For mana-hungry decks (battlecruiser midrange, ramp-heavy builds like the host's Radha deck), X-spells, X-abilities, and mana sinks (e.g. Kessig Wolf Run) substitute for draw by spending mana; best sinks are creatures with on-attack draw that synergize with the commander. Recursion (Seasons Past, Evolution Witness, Scrap Trawler, Phyrexian Reclamation, flashback/escape) reuses cards and needs graveyard fuel from self-mill or discard. The second goal, finding cards, favors filtering (Ledger Shredder, draw-and-discard, Dig Through Time, Impulse) and tutors (Demonic Tutor, Solve the Equation, Steelshaper's Gift), stronger when cards vary in power. The host places effects on a spectrum from pure selection to pure advantage; Pull from Tomorrow is his favorite draw-X, Necropotence and Greater Good also cited. Whether tutors count as draw depends on deck goals: he groups them with draw in Archidekt when they find card advantage or function like drawing (Breena aggro, combo decks digging for Time Sieve).
