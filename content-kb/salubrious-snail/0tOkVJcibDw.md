## Summary

Builder is handed three cards by patron pollΓÇöThe Sibsig Ceremony, Aether Vial, Birthing RitualΓÇöand constructs an EDH deck around them. Sibsig makes creatures cost two colorless less but destroys cast creatures, replacing them with a zombie, so the plan leans on Aether Vial and Birthing Ritual to put creatures into play without casting. Colors land in Golgari (triple-black Sibsig pushes toward Urborg support). Commander is Iname as One: cast from hand, it tutors a spirit on ETB and recurs one on death, looping Iname, Death Aspect into Iname, Life Aspect via Sibsig's destroy trigger, then a sacrifice outlet. Sac outlets pair well with Sibsig since creatures die anyway. Wincons are a patchwork: spirits midrange (Myojin, Kodama, Infernal Kirin), Aether Vial value, and a lands package (Field of the Dead, Dark Depths, Urborg, Accursed Duneyard). Setup needs commander-to-hand enablers (Command Beacon, Campfire), heavy ramp for 12 mana, plus 14 draw and 4 tutors, leaving little room for interaction. Indestructible or regeneration (Accursed Duneyard, Malakir Rebirth) protects key creatures from Sibsig.

## Key Clips

- The Sibsig Ceremony makes all of our creatures cost two colorless less but destroys them when they enter if we cast them, replacing them with a zombie. The silver lining is that the other two cards offer ways to get creatures into play without casting them.
- Birthing Ritual is a two mana enchantment that offers a sort of limited birthing pod effect, letting us look at the top 7 cards of our library on each end step and sacrifice a creature to replace it with one from those cards. Really the only thing this needs is a moderately large creature count and a decent spread between mana values to allow stairstepping up in mana costs.
- Indestructible works since Sibsig destroys the creatures rather than making you sacrifice them, leaving options such as Sapling of Colfenor, Kotis, most Gods, and any partner pair that includes Anara. The Mimeoplasm can copy a creature with indestructible, and Indoraptor will likewise be indestructible if you have an indestructible creature to discard.
- My choice of champion is Iname as One, a 12 mana 8/8 that, if played from hand, tutors out a spirit into play when it enters, and recurs a spirit to play when it dies. The forbidden option for The Sibsig Ceremony is a commander that wants to die.
- The default plan: Iname as One enters, two triggers go on the stack ΓÇö one to destroy it, one to tutor a spirit. Resolve the spirit-search first to tutor Iname, Death Aspect, which searches for any number of spirits into the graveyard including Iname, Life Aspect. Then the Sibsig trigger kills Iname as One, and its death trigger returns Iname, Life Aspect into play.
- Sacrifice outlets are pretty sweet with The Sibsig Ceremony in general, because it destroys the creatures. You've got all these creatures that are going to die anyway, and you may as well sacrifice them. Some care about power or mana value, and for those, sacrificing Iname as One itself can be a fantastic power burst in an already big turn.
- The commander is 12 mana and we need it in hand before playing it ΓÇö the ETB trigger doesn't happen if we cast it from the command zone. Four cards get our commander into the command zone: Campfire, Netherborn Alter, Road of Return, and Command Beacon, and getting one is a requirement for commander-based gameplay.
- The lands package is the final wincon: Field of the Dead gets us more zombies, Dark Depths can do its dark and depthy things, and a couple manlands can swing real big. Specific lands help the gameplan ΓÇö Urborg for casting The Sibsig Ceremony, Reliquary Tower for holding a fat hand, Accursed Duneyard for getting around Sibsig, and Command Beacon for getting Iname into hand.
