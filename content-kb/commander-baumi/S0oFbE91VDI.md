## Summary

This video profiles Glory, a white five-mana 4/4 flyer whose real value comes from the graveyard. While Glory is in the graveyard, paying four mana activates it at any time to give all your creatures protection from a chosen color until end of turnΓÇöa repeatable Akroma's Blessing effect with no restrictions. Protection stops damage, enchant/equip, blocking, and targeting from that color, dodges removal like Path to Exile and Blasphemous Act, can be activated twice against two-color threats, and survives counters since it's an activated ability. Glory is part of the judgment Incarnation cycle (Anger, Brawn, Filth, Valor, Wonder, plus Genesis). It sits at 0.1% EDHREC adoption, which the creator calls criminally low. Two requirements: get it into the graveyard (ideally by discarding, easy in red/blue loot decks, harder in mono-whiteΓÇöoverdraw and discard to hand size) and keep four mana open. It shines in blue-white flash/flicker control decks that hold mana anyway, and pairs with cost reducers like Convergence of Dominion. Downsides: graveyard hate and the four-mana cost; it can't stop board wipes that destroy or exile all creatures. Use it as a flexible supporting utility piece, not a deck cornerstone, in flyers, Spirits, or Elementals shells.
