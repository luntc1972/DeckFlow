## Summary

Deck resilience is essential to win Commander games; decks that fold to minor disruption fail. Build with good fundamentals, razor-sharp focus, and favor stable cards over high-risk/high-reward picks. Expect opposition and bake in countermeasures matched to your strategy: single-target protection spells for a Voltron commander, board-wide protection for a go-wide token strategy, your own counter magic to stop your finisher being countered, or graveyard recursion to rebuild after a wipe. Include enough of these effects to reliably draw one when needed. Rule of thumb: assuming ~25 cards drawn across a 10-turn game, run at least four copies of a given effect for a reasonable chance to have drawn one. If games run faster or you expect multiple instances of disruption (very likely), roughly double that count. Magic is a battle of resources, so redundancy in protection and recursion is worth the deck slots.
