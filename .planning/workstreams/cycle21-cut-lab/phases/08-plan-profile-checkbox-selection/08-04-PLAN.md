Make a checked generic strategy move the role floors it implies, composing overlapping selections
as the maximum delta per role, clamped by the existing floor validation, and leaving the bracket
and commander numbers on screen untouched.

already sees. It must not corrupt the side-by-side bracket/commander contract Phase 3 shipped.
Output: a delta table beside the floor defaults, one new resolved-floor field, and its tests.
</objective>
