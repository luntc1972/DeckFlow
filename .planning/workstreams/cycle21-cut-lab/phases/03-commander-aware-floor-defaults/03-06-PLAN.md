<objective>
Warn when a commander's raised floors cannot all fit in one 100-card deck, using an overlap-corrected
required-slot count rather than a naive sum.

Purpose: `max()` only ever raises floors and nothing pushes back, so some commanders end up with a floor
set no legal deck can satisfy. Cut Lab's existing contract is to warn before breaking a floor, never
silently — D-06 extends that to the aggregate. D-06a is the correction that makes the warning honest:
Cut Lab roles are explicitly not mutually exclusive, so a naive sum over-counts the cards actually needed
and would fire on commanders who are perfectly fine.

**Scope fence:** the correction is analytic — it is derived from facts about the classifier, not from a new
corpus measurement. Do NOT run a corpus pass, do NOT add a research task, and do NOT try to measure overlap
magnitude. Where the magnitude is genuinely unknown, the copy says the estimate is conservative.

**D-06a authorizes exactly two corrections, and this plan implements exactly those two:**
1. count `max(engines, draw)` in place of `engines + draw` — justified by the *proven strict subset*
   relationship, not by mere co-occurrence; and
2. treat `wincons` as free-riding rather than slot-consuming.

Every other role — `ramp`, `interaction-targeted`, `interaction-mass`, `protection`, and **`payoffs`** —
counts additively. Adding a third correction is out of scope for this plan and for the executor: a change to
the correction set is a decision change and goes back through the user, never through a plan or code edit.

**Discretionary decision, already made — implement it, do not re-open:** this is a **panel-level notice**,
not a new `CutLabFindingKind`. A finding kind attaches to individual cards and feeds the round-1/round-2
tally; this advisory attaches to no card and would immediately have to be added to
`ExcludedFindingKindsFromTally`, which is the signal that it is not a finding. Record that rationale in the
class xmldoc.
</objective>

<overlap_facts>
Established in `03-RESEARCH.md` §O-1 from the classifier's own code and its existing regression tests. These
are code facts, not estimates — do not re-derive them and do not measure them.

- `CutLabRoleAssigner.AssignRoles`' own docstring: "Multi-role membership is allowed; cutting a card reduces
  every role count it currently fills." (`CutLabRoleAssigner.cs:13-14`)
- `CutLabAnalysisContextBuilder.cs:219-224` increments **every** role a card carries by that card's quantity,
  so one physical slot inflates two or more role counts at once.
- **engines is a strict subset of draw.** The engines gate requires `IsDrawCard(oracle)`, which is draw's
  sole gate, so 100% of engines cards are also draw cards. Proven by `CutLabRoleAssignerTests.cs:270-284`
  and `:303-318` (Rhystic Study, Phyrexian Arena both assign `["draw","engines"]`).
- **wincons co-occurs with any role** through the `isComboPiece` branch, which is independent of every other
  predicate. Proven by `CutLabRoleAssignerTests.cs:252-267` (Swords to Plowshares + combo piece assigns
  `["interaction-targeted","wincons"]`).
- Only two pairs are exclusive by construction: lands vs ramp (`CutLabRoleAssigner.cs:133-139`) and
  interaction-targeted vs interaction-mass (`:150-163`, with a dedicated regression test at
  `CutLabRoleAssignerTests.cs:230-250`). Both of those therefore genuinely add.

**payoffs is NOT corrected, and that is deliberate.** §O-1 shows payoffs can co-occur with engines through
`PlanRole.Payoff | Engine` co-tagging and with wincons through `IsClosingPowerCard`, but "can co-occur" is not
the *proven strict subset* relationship that justified collapsing engines into draw, and no magnitude was ever
measured. D-06a authorizes exactly two corrections and payoffs appears in neither. Zeroing or discounting its
term would be a third, unauthorized correction — and the most damaging one available, because D-04's driver
table shows all **124 of 124** adopting payoffs commanders sit below the band at brackets 4-5, so `max()`
raises payoffs harder than any other role. A floor set that is infeasible *because of* a high payoffs floor
must still fire the advisory. If a payoffs correction ever looks warranted, it goes back through the user as a
decision change, never through a plan or code edit.

Consequence: D-06's measured counts — 3/841 at bracket 2, 16/841 at bracket 4, 23/841 at bracket 5, worst
case `The Watcher in the Water` at 78 slots against today's 56 — were computed with a naive sum and are
**upper bounds on infeasibility, not measured counts**. The corrected sum fires less often, which is the
intended direction.
</overlap_facts>

relaxing it would therefore not move the deficit. `payoffs` is an eligible candidate and will frequently be
the top-ranked one, since it carries the largest commander-driven raises in the corpus. Order candidates by:
1. the commander-driven raise, `(CommanderValue - BracketValue)` when `CommanderValue` is non-null and

2. then effective `Floor` descending;
3. then `RoleKey` with `StringComparer.Ordinal`.
