## Summary

This video reviews 10 Patron-submitted Commander decks, offering targeted upgrade advice. Recurring themes: Marine's artifact-Brago deck should pivot toward control, cutting standalone wincons (Myr Battlesphere, Tidus, Karn) and weak evasion for tutors, haste enablers, and counterspells. Aster's four-color artifact-token deck runs Lurrus without strong synergy; lean into aristocrats and evasion. ## Key Clips

- Marine's Miku deck is a Brago artifacts deck with around 15 mana rocks that can be blinked with Brago during combat to get an additional round of mana. To power it toward high bracket 3 to low bracket 4, pivot into more of a control playstyleΓÇöBrago generates absurd value, so lean on snowballing through pure value rather than standalone wincons like Myr Battlesphere, Kappa Cannoneer, Tidus, or Karn. Add haste enablers (Swiftfoot Boots, Lavaspur Boots) for an immediate Brago swing, better tutor ETBs like Tribute Mage and Recruiter of the Guard, and another counterspell or two since a board wipe on your artifacts is a likely way to lose control.
- On Hugh Grant's Huge Grants (Forge, Neverwinter Charlatan), the biggest issue is the treasures package: the effort spent creating treasures isn't matched by the benefit. The commander is a treasure payoff in theory (+2/+0 per treasure) but the deck lacks evasion or blocker removal for a reliable beatdown. Trim weaker treasure generators like Burakos, Mari, Petty Larceny, and Treasure Dredger to free space for the creature aristocrats package and more creature removalΓÇösomething like Soul Shatter or Vona's Hunger helps push damage with Grim Hireling and swing big with the commander.
- Aster's four color tokens deck runs Lurrus companion with two commanders (Wernog and Bjorna), but Lurrus doesn't feel cohesively tied to the artifact-token gameplan. To make the restriction feel at home, lean into aristocrats as a subtheme: add sac outlets, a couple more recursion spells, and Skullclamp to crunch value off the ETBs once Lurrus is in play. Push harder in the ETB direction with flicker instants to both protect Lurrus and help generate more artifact tokens, which Aster mentioned struggling to produce.
- BadNat's Sigarda hatebears deck is a green-white midrange list that crunches value with its commander while slowing opponents with stax creatures, backed by two soft-lock combos: Kudo plus Elesh Norn, and Brusela plus Gaddock Teeg. The 2-mana ramp humans are strongΓÇöthey can ramp into the commander on turn 3ΓÇöbut board wipes are the bane of mana dorks, so add a couple more protection pieces like Laezel's Acrobatics or Ghostway. The 34-land count plus modal options looks good since the commander digs for gas; Verge Rangers and Case of the Locked Hothouse are good additions.
- Paul's Mono Blue reanimator deck with Alexi, Zephyr Mage is full of personality with a realistic plan of cheating big creatures via tutors, plus robust draw and filtering and well-balanced interaction for mono-blue. The one change worth making is adding more rampΓÇöit's a very mana-hungry deck with a high curve, dense draw, and an X-ability commander. The current suite is fully rockless; keep that theme with Wayfarer's Bauble, Extraplanar Lens, Savor the Moment (which also retriggers sagas and reanimator artifacts), and Terrain Generator.
- IdesHatred's Simic Land Voltron deck copies a land with Moritte of the Frost, animates it, and swings for commander damage while running eight-plus cards that wipe or bounce all nonland permanents. IdesHatred noted it feels pubstompy, which is typical of board-wipe and mass-bounce decks since low-mid power EDH decks can't redeploy efficiently. To make it less polarizing without losing power, crank up ramp and draw, add top-end creatures or removal-on-creatures for an alternative beatdown, or lean harder into the pre-voltron game with indestructible lands to copy and animate.
- On Lurvyx's Aragorn political deck (becoming the monarch, goad, and giving opponents creatures), the most out-of-place element is the lifegainΓÇöthere's almost no payoff and in a Monarch deck you don't want creatures hitting you in the first place, so if lifegain is useful you're already in trouble. The broad fix is to make the deck less political: add actual removal for threats the defensive suite can't contain, and broaden the creature base with unblockable-payoff cards like Professional Facebreaker, Grazilaxx, Bloodthirster, or Medomai the Ageless.
- Jax's Zimone deck is a high-powered stax deck using Zimone's counter-doubling to build value toward a lockout combo, pairing planeswalkers with cards like Encroaching Mycosynth and Mycosynth Lattice that turn walkers into artifacts for immediate ults. The execution problems: cut 3 or 4 planeswalkers that lack either a game-winning ult or strong early utility (Nissa, Ugin the Ineffable), pare back the weak artifact wincons (Grindclock, Magistrate's Scepter, Darksteel Reactor), and run more tutors plus accessible draw and rampΓÇöeven a Stock UpΓÇöto find the key pieces and smooth the early game.
