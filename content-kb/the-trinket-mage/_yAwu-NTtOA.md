## Summary

In this stream a Commander content creator rates patrons' "nine cards that made me" lists, surfacing several card interactions. Mishra, Artificer Prodigy enables artifact tricks: stacking triggers with Blood Funnel lets artifacts effectively cost two less (Funnel counters the spell, Mishra retrieves it from the graveyard and replays it), and Nether Void breaks parity since your countered artifacts return for free while opponents must pay; Possibility Storm works as a budget substitute. Coveted Jewel is broken when blinked, re-stealing repeatedly. Blazing Shoal is busted with Progenitus or other high-cost red cards: exile them to grant +10/+0 for zero mana, lethal on a Blighted Agent or other infect creature (banned in Modern). Eye of the Storm goes infinite-feeling with cheap repeatable spells like Pox, Stone Rain, or Shaharazad. Power Conduit removes counters to reset Sagas, looping construct tokens. Kate Stewart pairs with Millennium Calendar to dump hundreds of time counters into a massive team-wide pump. Dash Hopes is a black "counterspell" unless opponents pay five life, but fails against cards that simply win the game. ## Key Clips

- Power Conduit lets you put a charge counter on an artifact or put a counter on something, but you can remove a counter from a permanent, which means you can move your sagas back up. So you can get to saga two and then use Power Conduit to remove a counter, and it keeps going back and forth, and you can keep making constructs over and over again.
- Eye of the Storm: whenever any player casts an instant or sorcery, you exile it, and then that player copies every other instant and sorcery exiled with Eye of the Storm. You stack up a massive pile of instants and sorceries, and whenever somebody casts, say, Rampant Growth, it casts like 10 things.
- Mishra: whenever you cast an artifact spell you may search your graveyard, hand, or library for a card with the same name and put it on the battlefield. With Blood Funnel, the funnel triggers, counters your artifact, and then Mishra grabs it from your graveyard and puts it back into play ΓÇö so all your artifacts cost two less for free. With Nether Void you can break parity: let them counter your artifact for not paying three, and it just goes from the graveyard back into play.
- Blazing Shoal is red red X, give a creature plus X plus 0, and you can exile a red card with mana value X from your hand instead of paying its mana cost. You'd play Blazing Shoal and exile something like Progenitus for free, give a Blighted Agent plus 10 plus 0 for zero mana, and instantly delete people. In modern this was a menace ΓÇö that's why it's banned.
- Coveted Jewel: when you first see it as a casual player you think 'oh, it gets passed around, that's cute,' and then you realize you can blink it, and it's one of the most broken Magic cards of all time. I've lost to this in cEDH.
- Kate Stewart: whenever it attacks you may pay eight, and if you do, attacking creatures get plus X plus X where X is the number of time counters among permanents you control. With Millennium Calendar it's one of the only things that lets you put hundreds of time counters on things, so you can put 500 counters on it and give your team plus 500 plus 500.
- Dash Hopes is a counterspell in black ΓÇö black black, counter target spell, if you ignore the part where any player can pay five life to counter Dash Hopes. It can't be a win con, because if a player has less than five life they just can't pay; you can't pay life you don't have.
- Akroma, Traveling Merchant: remove a counter from a nonland permanent you control, draw a card. That lets you reset your sagas.
