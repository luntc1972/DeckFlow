## Summary

Argues board wipes are a neglected deckbuilding slot; every wipe should be chosen with intention and synergy, not defaulted to generics like Farewell, Vanquish the Horde, Blasphemous Act, or Toxic Deluge. Prefer bespoke fits: for a Muldrotha graveyard/lands deck, use wipes it can recur (Massacre Girl, Meathook Massacre, Mutual Destruction) over Damnation/Toxic Deluge/Deadly Cover-Up. For decks with enchantment tutors (Idyllic/Enlightened Tutor), run Out of Time as a tutorable wipe; likewise Perplex + Toxic Deluge in blue/black. Three wipe categories: parity creators (Wrath of God, Damn, Out of TimeΓÇögood for slow/low-permanent decks), parity breakers (Winds of Abandon/Wrath, By Invitation Only, Mizzium Mortars, Culling the Weak, Crippling FearΓÇöfor proactive/go-wide decks like Adeline, Myrel), and synergistic weirdos (Star of Extinction with Brash Taunter, Living End in sacrifice, Mutinous Massacre to untap the commander, Nevinyrral's Disk/Boompile with indestructible artifact boards, Avatar's/Airbender's-style ETB rebuy). By Invitation Only and Mizzium Mortars are underplayed relative to worse generics. Match the wipe to your curve and tempo; sometimes run none (e.g. Ruric Thar). Use Scryfall's 'otag:wipe' to find options EDHREC omits. Justify every wipe with real synergy, not just 'it's a good wipe,' and keep experimenting.
