## Summary

Kellen, Daring Traveler is a Selesnya (GW) hatebears/stax deck with a soft-combo top end, played at bracket 3. Kellen's adventure makes Map tokens; his attack reveals the top card, putting creatures of mana value 3 or less into hand and milling other cards, giving card advantage plus top-deck manipulation. The deck is built around Congregation at Dawn, which stacks Bruna, Gisela, and Gaddock Teeg on top; you draw/mill and reanimate the angels (Bruna's cast trigger returns an angel or human), then meld Bruna and Gisela into Brisela on the end step. With Brisela and Gaddock Teeg out, opponents can't cast spells of mana value 3 or less, nor noncreatures of value 4 or greater, locking the table out while a 9/10 flier ends the game by turn 11-12. A softer secondary line uses Kudo plus Elesh Norn to shrink opposing creatures. Ramp (about 11 pieces) is mostly on creatures. Protection is heavy and built to beat Farewell (phase-out and delayed-exile effects), plus silence effects (Voice of Victory, Cutlass, Ranger-Captain) to protect the meld. Eladamri and similar cards let you cast off the top. Mill enablers like Analyze the Pollen double as tutors and lands.
