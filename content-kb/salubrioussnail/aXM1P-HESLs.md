## Summary

This video reviews submissions to a deckbuilding challenge: build a 99-card singleton EDH deck using only cards whose names contain no letter E, accompanied by a ~100-word E-less written piece. Excluding E eliminates roughly 83% of Magic cards and even bans Forests, so finding viable archetypes was the central struggle. Some card types survived well: Polymorph effects, Rat Colony, and Ratadrabik Nazgul combo. Highlighted decks: Arcum Dagsson stations, which tutors artifact creatures into the four Fifth Dawn Stations (Blasting, Grinding, Summoning, Salvaging) for an infinite combo, backed by 19 artifact creatures, nine counterspells, and protection. Momir Vig biovisionary copies Biovisionary four ways to win, using Simic creatures, ramp, and ETB-bounce loops. An Iron Man deck wins via the silver-border Now I Know My ABCs by assembling artifacts spanning all 26 letters, with Spy Kit as a shortcut. Tiamat serves a Food Chain combo: Misthollow Griffin makes infinite mana, Tiamat fetches Dragonlord Dromoka (protection) plus Hoarding Broodlord and Joyful Stormsculptor to burn opponents via convoke, with eight counterspells and five-color consistency. Honorable mentions include K'rrik, Asmoranomardicadaistinaculdacar, Godo, Gonti, and Glarb Doomsday decks. The challenge showcased creative navigation of severe restrictions.

## Key Clips

- The format is Oulipo Dragon Scotsman (ODS): a 99-card singleton EDH deck led by a Commissar (a legendary creature castable at any time), where no card name may contain the letter E. The challenge also required an approximately 100-word written work with no Es. About 180 submissions were received.
- Excluding Es eliminates roughly 83% of all Magic cards, so if you have 6 cards that do a particular thing, on average all but one will be eliminated. Some card types survived well: only about half of Polymorph cards were eliminated, leaving five for red-blue decks, and Rat Colony let players fill out large portions of a deck.
- Nell's Arcum Dagsson station deck: Arcum (four mana, sacrifices an artifact creature to tutor a noncreature artifact each turn) fetches the four Fifth Dawn 'station' cardsΓÇöBlasting, Grinding, Summoning, and Salvaging StationΓÇönone of which contain an E. With all four in play plus a low-cost artifact in the graveyard, their untap clauses merge into an infinite combo that kills opponents. Supported by 19 artifact creatures, nine counterspells, and five other protection pieces.
- The no-E format excludes a basic landΓÇöForests. Despite this, several mono-green decks were built using roughly 11 E-less green-producing lands plus green mana rocks, workable with an aggressive mulligan policy. Two-color decks fare notably better because nonbasic lands tend to have fewer Es.
- Shuckle's Momir Vig biovisionary deck: Momir Vig tutors creatures to the top of library on green creature spells and draws on blue creature spells, so blue-green spells go straight to hand. Biovisionary wins if you control four copies on your end step, so the deck runs 12 copy effects (basic clones, Sakashima's Will on a wide board, exiling Biovisionary under Soul Foundry). Nine Simic creatures support tutoring; 15 green land symbols plus two from ramp total 17.
- Iron Man deck built around Now I Know My ABCs (silver-border card that wins on upkeep if you control permanents containing all 26 letters of the alphabet). Iron Man makes Treasures (supplies an E) and acts as a birthing-pod for artifacts. The deck hunts rare letters via Tamiyo's Journal, Blazing Torch, Jhoira's Familiar, Acquisition Octopus, and Spy Kit (gives a creature all legendary creature names). It can pivot to artifact beatdown via Sharding Sphinx and flyers.
- Mirrorwing's Tiamat deck is a Food Chain combo deck. Food Chain exiles a creature for its mana value plus one (creature-only mana); combined with Misthollow Griffin (castable from exile) this makes infinite creature mana. Tiamat (seven mana, tutors five dragons) fetches Dragonlord Dromoka for protection and Hoarding Broodlord, which grants convoke to spells cast from exile and tutors Joyful Stormsculptor to deal 1 damage per convoke castΓÇölooping Griffin burns opponents down. Runs 8 counterspells and 9 slowdown cards.
- Because Food Chain exiles as a cost and Dromoka is played first, almost all creature removal can't interact with the Tiamat comboΓÇöonly enchantment removal (to destroy Food Chain) and counterspells. Five colors keeps the manabase workable despite losing Es: a full 8 original dual lands contain no E, with two untapped and four tapped fetches available.
