## Summary

Core lesson: avoid "autopilot" deckbuilding—including cards that don't fit the deck's plan. Common causes: over-relying on netdecks or tools like EDHREC, defaulting to the same staples for a color, or lacking awareness that a card is subpar for the specific build. Concrete examples of autopilot mistakes: running Cultivate and Kodama's Reach when a 3-mana commander is almost always your turn-three play; loading up on ramp spells when the commander already provides ramp; and running symmetrical board wipes like Blasphemous Act or Farewell in a Voltron deck, which destroy your own commander—use asymmetrical wraths that favor you instead, such as Single Combat or Slash the Ranks. Heuristic: question the value every card adds and challenge its right to be in the deck; don't take inclusions at face value. Don't assume general deckbuilding rules or the reputation of a "good" staple automatically apply to your specific deck—a generically strong card may or may not be good in context. Evaluate each slot against what the deck is actually trying to do.
