## Summary

EDHRec aggregates deck data to show commonly played cards per commander. The creator uses it at two stages of deckbuilding. Early on, he checks a commander's page to get a lay of the land—seeing the flashiest or most powerful plays and the points of agreement across builds—but deliberately clicks away quickly so popular cards don't subconsciously dictate the deck, preserving the creative Scryfall-search exploration phase. Later, once he has a strong concept, he returns to find specific cards Scryfall searches miss (e.g., flicker enablers like Dour-port Mage, self-bouncers, or cast-from-exile payoffs). The key technique is filters. The base page mixes every archetype, so for diverse commanders it becomes 'soup.' He uses 'indicator cards'—cards that cleanly signal an archetype (e.g., Map the Frontier for big-mana Kellan)—to include or exclude decks. He also excludes anti-synergy cards (goad cards on Kardur) and combines includes/excludes (Thalia in, Soul Warden out on Liesa) to isolate the build he wants, and can filter by price for budget. He stresses detachment: the page just reflects choices others made, so think about why cards appear there.
