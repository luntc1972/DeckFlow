## Summary

The creator revisits whether Sol Ring should be banned in Commander, having recently argued Rhystic Study should be. He leans against banning Sol Ring on both theoretical and practical grounds. Theoretically, banning depends on high power level plus a bothersome play pattern; Sol Ring's pattern is less bothersome than Rhystic's. Using a 'pipeline to victory' scale, he notes Sol Ring is strongest earliest and falls off fastΓÇöan ephemeral, task-oriented enabler that fades into the background, unlike scalable cards (Smothering Tithe), draw engines (Rhystic, The One Ring), or endgame cards (Bolas's Citadel).
