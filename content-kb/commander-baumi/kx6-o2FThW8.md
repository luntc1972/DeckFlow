## Summary

This video covers a four-step green ramp strategy in casual Commander aimed at reaching effectively unlimited mana, while avoiding infinite combos. Step one is early-game basic ramp: mana dorks, Rampant Growth, Cultivate, plus efficient options like Khalni Heart Expedition, Gaea's Touch, Nissa's Pilgrimage (in green-focused decks), Primal Growth, Far Wanderings (scales with game length via threshold), Skyshroud Claim, Sequestered Stash/Search routes, and Map the Frontier for utility lands like deserts and Gates. Steps two and three are interchangeable: build ramp engines or cast big ramp spells. Engines include Bristly Bill plus Fertilid (reusable Rampant Growth), Retreat to Coralhelm with Sakura-Tribe Scout for land loops, and Azusa with Crucible-of-Worlds fetchland recursion. Big spells include Open the Way (X=3 or 4), Traverse the Outlands (scales with creature power), and Boundless Realms (doubles your lands). Step four multiplies mana with Mana Reflection, Virtue of Strength, and Nyxbloom Ancient, which become free once you have a large land base and can stack multiplicatively. Key takeaways: play Open the Way and Boundless Realms in every green deck.

## Key Clips

- What I mean by unlimited Mana is getting our Mana pool to a point where we no longer worry about how to pay for our spells. There are four steps that we're going to complete on our journey.
- Konda's Hard Expedition is a two Mana enchantment that gets a quest counter whenever land enters under our control, then if there are three quest counters on it we can sacrifice it to search Our Deck for two basics and put them into play. It's slow but very efficient.
- Gaea's Touch is a two Mana enchantment that once a turn lets us put a basic Forest into play from our hand, and has an additional ability that allows us to sacrifice it to add two green Mana to our Mana pool. Use it for extra land drops while that makes sense, then sacrifice it as a ritual once we don't need it.
- Far Wanderings has a threshold ability: usually we search for one basic and put it into play tapped, but with seven or more cards in our graveyard we search for three basics and put all of them into play. It's a ramp spell that scales with game length.
- Bristly Bill puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature when a land enters. Fertilid enters with two +1/+1 counters and can remove one to pay two mana and search for a basic land. With both in play, Fertilid ramps a land, triggers Bristly Bill, and replenishes the counter ΓÇö an infinitely reusable rampant growth limited only by mana.
- Retreat to Coralhelm is a four mana enchantment with a landfall ability to untap a creature. Combine with Sakura-Tribe Scout, which taps to put a land into play from hand; this triggers Retreat, untaps the Scout, and repeats as often as we have lands in hand. Add Tatyova or Azusa to draw a card each loop.
- Traverse the Outlands lets us search for X basics and put them into play, where X is the greatest power among creatures we control. Just controlling a four power creature makes it more mana efficient than Open the Way, and it only uses a single green mana symbol, making it easily splashable.
- Mana multipliers like Mana Reflection, Virtue of Strength, and Nyxbloom Ancient double or triple our Mana. Once we reach this stage they're actually free ΓÇö cast Nyxbloom for seven, leaving four lands open that now tap for twelve total, gaining mana on the cast and reaching 36 next turn.
