## Summary

A deckbuilding guide on constructing bespoke Commander mana bases, framed around building a five-color budget The Ur-Dragon (?) deck. The host argues most mana-base problems come from autopilot copying (e.g. EDHREC lists) instead of analyzing the deck's own color pips, pip counts, and mana curve to determine how many sources of each color you need and by what turn. He targets roughly 44-45 mana sources on average but says to break that rule per deck. Because the budget Dragon deck has no one-drops, its first turn is dead, so it can afford more tapped lands; the curve really starts at four/five (Dragons reduced by the commander). Land choices: pain lands (two colors, negligible life loss, cheap, underrated), Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, tricolor tap lands that produce green, searchable tap duals that fetch Forests, one of each basic, and check lands. He rejected the budget triomes because they act as tap lands, only fetch basics, and he rarely used their colorless ability. Ramp turns 1-3 as setup: Farseek, Nature's Lore, Three Visits on turn two; Hunting Wilds, Ranger's Path, Skyshroud Claim, Tempt with Discovery on turn three, plus green-producing mana rocks.
