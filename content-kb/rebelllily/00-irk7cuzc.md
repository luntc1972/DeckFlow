Four players battle with Magic: The Gathering Woodland Kingdom Commander precons: Senpai on Zenia (Offspring tokens), rookie on Hazel (squirrel synergy), "your boy" on Bellows (raccoon enchantments), and designer on Miss Bumbleflower (politics).
