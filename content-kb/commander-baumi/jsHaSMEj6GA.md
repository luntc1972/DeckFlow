## Summary

Celix creates a 1/1 horror token whenever an opponent mills creature cards and can tap to make players mill three. Its key synergy is Altar of the Brood: a one-mana artifact that mills opponents whenever a permanent enters under your control. - Tivot, seller of secrets, is an incredibly powerful commander that has a nearly instant win combo with one other card, time. Hit somebody once, create five artifacts to sacrifice for an extra turn, and then repeat until everybody is dead.
- My point is that I like taking shitty commanders and pushing them to their limits. In this particular case, by finding one specific piece of synergy that really elevates them and improves their capabilities to a point that they don't even feel like the same card anymore.
- While they will definitely make the deck stronger, the huge power spike they invoke might disrupt the pace of the match too much to remain a positive experience. Particularly in the lower brackets, a sudden increase in tempo can be undesirable.
- Meil, the pretender is a Grixis 4/4 who when he enters lets us exile artifact or creature from our hand or graveyard and then put a cage counter on it. Marcil also has all activated abilities of exiled cards we own with a cage counter on them, but may only use each of them once a turn. The fix is Ephilling, which has a self flicker effect for only one mana, so once Mile enters we exile Ephilling and gain its abilities to flicker Mercil once per turn.
- Bridget, hero of Kinsbale is a white four mana 2/2 with first strike and a tap ability that deals two damage to each attacking or blocking creature target player controls. If we give her deathtouch through something like Basilisk's collar, Bridget suddenly takes complete control over combat as her ability starts to simply kill any attacking or blocking creatures.
- Celix, Sanity Flayer is a blue three mana 2/4 that lets us choose a background as a secondary commander. Celix also creates a 1/1 horror creature token whenever a player mills one or more creature cards. And for one mana, we can tap it to make a player mill three cards. Importantly, Celix only creates one horror for each mill step our opponents are taking.
- Altar of the Brood is a one-mana artifact that has each opponent mill a card whenever a permanent enters the battlefield under our control. With Celix in play, every time we mill a creature like this, we create a permanent, which then mills our opponents again. Depending on how many creatures our opponents play, this can end the game on the spot.
- This all started because I found Seki, season's guide, a green eight mana 0/0/0 that enters with eight plus-one plus-one counters on it. It goes randomly infinite with Warstorm Surge: Seki enters, we shoot it with Warstorm Surge, creating eight 1/1s, then we sacrifice the spirits to bring Seki back, shooting itself again. Boom, everybody dead. Unfortunately, Warstorm Surge isn't in the right colors.
