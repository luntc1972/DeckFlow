## Summary

Argues most Commander decks should follow a deckbuilding template rather than getting greedy. Baseline for the average deck: reach five lands by turn five, with mana acceleration on turn one or two. Recommends staying near ~38 lands; cutting to 36 to fit extra gas is acceptable, but dropping to 32 (especially while running four mana rocks) is a mistake he repeatedly regrets. ## Key Clips

- **[00:28]** If we look at the most popular commanders on EDHREC, most of these commanders and decks will reliably want to get to five lands by turn five, and they most likely want mana acceleration on turns one or two.
- **[00:40]** Going down to 36 lands to squeeze in some extra gas is far better than squeezing down to 32 lands. Especially if, heavens forbid, you're packing four mana rocks.
- **[00:18]** Removal in my Sauron deck looks very different to removal in my Marin deck, which looks very different to removal in my Aragorn spellslinger deck. And this is true for other card categories like ramp.
- **[00:29]** If you combine this with cards that do double duty and straddle multiple categories at once, you suddenly create so much room for activities within your deck.
- **[00:38]** A lot of my ramp or removal is strapped to creatures, meaning it's really easy to get those 10-plus numbers, and because that synergizes with my commander, it allows me to recycle that removal and ramp, making it even more powerful than if I'd simply run the best 11 ramp or removal spells in black and green.
