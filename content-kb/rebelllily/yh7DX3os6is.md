## Summary

This deck-building video presents a horse-themed Commander deck led by Shadowfax / Gold Ship, framed around a horse-racing/training concept. The primary win path is combat damage: suit up the commander with auras (All That Glitters, Mantle the Ancient, Light Wheel Enhancements, Aether Spark) and pump effects, then close with double-combat spells like Aggravated Assault, Full Throttle, Great Train Heist, and Overpower. A secondary plan exploits the deck's many legendary horses (Kellith, Calamity Galloping Inferno, Thurid/Tokai Teo) plus copy effects from Delina, Jaxis, Mirror Style Master, and Echoing Assault to flood the board with token copies of non-legendary horses for extra damage. Thunder Mare and Calamity tap down blockers to push through. Strixhaven Stadium is a fringe alternate win condition. The builder identifies card draw as the deck's biggest weakness, adding Mask of Memory, Outpost Siege, Thorough Investigation, Tomorrow Legends, and Valikut Exploration. Because the combat wincons are fragile, protection matters: Boros Charm, Legion's Initiative, Mother of Runes, Unbreakable Formation, and Greater Auramancy guard creatures and enchantments. Sythis/Ondo-style enchantment doublers and Nyx-born Unicorn add value. The core design principle is scaling low-power cards through copying and enchantment-doubling effects.
