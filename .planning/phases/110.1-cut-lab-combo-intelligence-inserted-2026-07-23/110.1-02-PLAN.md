<objective>
Introduce a new `CutLabFindingKind.ComboProtected` finding kind with its own detector, near-combo
variant grouping, weak-floor cross-reference, and advisory-copy-only "don't cut in round 1" text
(LOCKED decisions #2, #4, #5). Resolve two data contracts the rendering plan (110.1-03) consumes:
a per-card combo-state map on the Razor view-model AND on the Phase 108 patch DTO, so badges
round-trip through what-if / re-decide. Add `ComboProtected` to the cut-round tally-exclusion set
so it does not silently push combo cards toward cutting.

Purpose: This is the data/contract layer (Wave 2). It produces the finding kind and the two
server-authored maps that Plan 03 renders as chip badges + disclosure context. No Razor/CSS/TS
rendering happens here — interface-first ordering so the executor of Plan 03 receives contracts.

Output: `ComboProtected` findings, a per-card combo-state map on `CutLabViewModel` and
`CutLabUiPatchDto`, presenter grouping, the tally-exclusion addition, and xUnit coverage
(structural-findings cases + patch round-trip).
</objective>
