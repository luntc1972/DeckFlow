## Summary

This is a live panel from the Ancient Mirror podcast where hosts (Trinket Mage, Salubrious Snail, Quips, Quok, guest Travis Gafford) crowd-source Commander deck ideas from the audience. Trinket Mage wants a grindy bracket 3-4 Humility deck with no superfriends and no combos, exploiting layers and timestamps: equip after Humility to keep keywords, play Opalescence after Humility so enchantments keep text, abuse counters and backgrounds that still apply under Humility. Suggestions included Ephara, Kudo, Karametra, Athreos Shroud-Veil (steal creatures via coin counters plus board wipes), Calix, and an 'air bending' flicker-recast plan. Kribbs wants a non-infinite Goblin Bombardment loop sacrificing one recurring creature (Dargo with Pitiless Plunderer, persist/undying, At Knifepoint mercenaries, pariah redirection). Salubrious Snail needs a non-Golgari home for 25 Nexus of Becoming copies; ideas centered on blue (Claire de Lune token-cards, Vhal, Precursor Golem, Bosh, Encroaching Mycosynth). Travis wants a mono-white Lucy MacLean political token deck, with cards like Amulet of Safekeeping and Genesis Chamber turning gifted tokens into liabilities. Q&A covered creative commander selection, hidden commanders needing redundancy and tutors, and self-imposed deckbuilding constraints.

## Key Clips

- **[04:05]** Because of timestamps and layers, if you play an equipment after Humility has been attached, you actually keep all of the keywords and bonuses on it. So you can have one 20/20 with triple strike, and everyone else is just a one-one.
- **[05:00]** Commander of Ephara, God of the Polis, full of colorless creatures so you don't get your devotion too high — so that you always get the draw trigger. The problem is your devotion eventually gets high enough that you can't cast more enchantments and you're turning your commander off.
- **[06:38]** If you want grindy long games, the Theros god is Karametra. Every turn you search your deck for a forest, play non-creature enchantments, get Humility out, then clone a Humility and reanimate it with Starfield of Nyx.
- **[08:16]** Build an air bending (flicker) deck where all your creatures have a cast trigger. Use air bend spells to flicker out your Humility on your turn so your whole board recasts it and locks people under it, while your creatures' abilities still function as you cast them through Humility.
- **[16:08]** Dargo Shipwrecker can be your commander and costs two less for everything you sacrifice this turn. With treasures he pays for himself, so you can loop him by sacking him to Goblin Bombardment with a Pitiless Plunderer in play.
- **[21:35]** With Claire de Lune, tokens you own become cards that are still tokens — they continue to exist after they leave the battlefield. So you can put a token in your hand and then ditch it to Nexus of Becoming again to make a copy.
- **[24:22]** Play Precursor Golem with a blue commander because both it and Nexus of Becoming are tutorable by the mages — tribute mage, trinket mage, treasure mage, transmute mage. You become a blue version of the red commander that duplicates spells for every creature you target.
- **[43:35]** Pick a commander that solves the weaknesses of your deck or works with it indirectly. With Malfegor you discard your hand and each opponent sacrifices that many creatures; your discard makes opponents play out big creatures, then Malfegor eats them — and opponents don't expect your commander on a particular turn.
