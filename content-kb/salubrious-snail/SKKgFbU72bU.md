## Summary

Combo occupies a useful niche in EDH: it can cut through slow, greedy, board-centered decks that pile on creature value, which suits the format's 40-life, three-opponent structure. But combo also draws complaints for being un-interactive (solitaire-like spike wins from behind on board, life, and hand) and for breaking the conventional resource language of aggro/midrange/control. The core lesson: pure combo decks fail in high-power EDH because everyone runs 120-plus total life, so stax and counterspells become ubiquitous. Run alternate wincons as backup. Dargo-Thrasios shows this at cEDH level: multiple infinite-mana combos plus a beatdown fallback (Dargo three-tapping) make it flexible. At lower power, the combo must fit the deck naturallyΓÇöeither central with tutors/draw, or blended so its required resources (mana, creatures, loops) come from the deck's normal gameplan. The Vadrok mutate deck illustrates this: a convoluted infinite-turns combo, but every piece has standalone utility and the setup arises from generating value and protecting Sunforger for commander-damage wins. Inversely, a beatdown deck (budget Grismold) can add a combo backup (Blood Artist/Syr Konrad) using the same token resource. When in doubt, just include a beefy creature backup.
