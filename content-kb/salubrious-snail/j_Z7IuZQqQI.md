## Summary

The creator recounts building several "creative combo" Commander decks and what he learned. His Archelos, Lagoon Mystic deck weaponizes Maze's End by dropping all gates at once with Scapeshift, Reshape the Earth, and Boundless Realms; Archelos makes lands enter untapped (like Amulet of Vigor) while also ramping and taxing opponents. It runs 12 gates plus bounce lands, basics, and utility lands (43 lands total, not counting Maze's End), 20 dedicated ramp spells plus 4 that double as commander tap-down, Emergent Ultimatum as an extra combo tutor, 11 counterspells, and 6 other control cards; it wins by turn 7-8 for ~$100. A Yidris cascade deck and a budget (~$50) Tribal Fiend Hunter deck (commander Torsten, Founder of Benalia for its ETB draw, blink to loop three of eight GW Fiend Hunters, 42 lands + 29 ramp creatures) are also described. His lesson: creative wincons play like any 1-card+commander combo and are fragile—creature-based ramp made Fiend Hunter easily disrupted. He praises his Radha and Sharuum decks for consistency, flexibility, and resiliency (Radha's basic-land focus is hard to hate). Heuristic: build with constraints, and pick decks whose engine/interaction are defined but wincons are open-ended.
