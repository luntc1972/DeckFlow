## Summary

This Magic Mirror podcast discusses Commander cards that improve over time. The hosts argue generic cards (removal, mana rocks) get power-crept, while non-generic, synergistic payoffs age well. Examples: Mystic Remora-style cards gain value with three opponents; doubler effects (Panharmonicon) improve as cheap broken ETB creatures print; cheap deploy-able creatures like Tasigur; proliferate payoffs as counter cards grow; singleton enablers like Hermit Druid eased by MDFCs/channel lands; high-mana-value-matters cards (Analyze the Pollen, Keen Duelist) inflated by Murktide/MDFCs; niche tutors (Trinket Mage, Perplex) that gain more targets, especially strong three-drops; utility-land tutors (Tolaria West, Expedition Map) boosted by Talon Gates and Urza's Cave; Tortured Existence, a hard-to-power-creep one-mana rebuy engine; Cloudstone Curio; Inalla and Hellkite Corsair, which scale with new wizards and commanders carrying enter/attack triggers; and original green Selvala. Archetype trends: reanimator improves as bigger closers print; artifacts always grow via treasures/clues; aristocrats stay strong being cheap and recursive; Voltron weakens as removal outpaces protection; graveyard strategies suffer from ubiquitous graveyard hate. The through line: cards that do something specific, act as amplifiers, or require options that keep getting printed gain consistency and power over time.
