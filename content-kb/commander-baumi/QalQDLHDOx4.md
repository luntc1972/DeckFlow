Eluge is a four-mana elemental fish whose power/toughness equals islands controlled; when it enters or attacks, it places a flood counter on a target land, turning it into an island. The plan: run eight two-mana rocks to land Eluge turn three, then accumulate flood counters fast. Each counter makes more interaction (counters, removal, card draw) free, on every player's turn.
