## Operator decisions (2026-07-29, AskUserQuestion)

**D-04-A — MV dimension = EXACT mana-value equality.** Not bucketed. Chosen over
reusing `CurveCongestion`'s coarse `["0-1","2","3","4","5+"]` boundaries and over a
split-0-from-1 variant. Rationale: matches the ROADMAP goal's "compete for the same slot
**at the same cost**"; avoids grouping Sol Ring (MV 1) with the Moxen (MV 0); fires less
often, which lowers finding-density risk on a 130-card pool (Success Criterion 5).

> ⚠ **Traceability tension.** REQUIREMENTS.md TWIN-01 says "mana-value **bucket**".
> Exact equality is a degenerate bucket. The planner must surface this and record an
> amendment decision rather than let the plan silently contradict the requirement text.

**D-04-B — add `TypeLine` / `IsLocked` / `IsCommander` to the shared
`CutLabAnalyzedCard`.** Chosen over threading a separate exclusion parameter into the
twins detector only. Rationale: clean long-term shape; the data becomes available to the
other 5 detectors, which currently leak locked and commander cards into their evidence.
