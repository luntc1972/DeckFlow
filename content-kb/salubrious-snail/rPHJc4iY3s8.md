## Summary

The creator streams MTGO Standard piloting an Azorius (white-blue) artifacts deck built around United Battlefront and Simul Synthesizer. Battlefront grabs two non-land, non-creature permanents costing three or less and tutors the Synthesizer, which generates a construct sized to your artifact count whenever you cast a three-plus-cost artifact. The deck crams in three-mana removal artifacts to snowball: land Synthesizer, spam cheap artifact removal, build constructs. The ideal curve is turn-three Synthesizer into turn-four Battlefront, which overwhelms slower decks. It struggles against Insidious Roots, where looping Haywire Mite with Tasha's Cauldron is the real threat, and needs maindeck Temporary Lockdown as a catch-up tool since the deck folds when far behind. Sideboarding adds Rest in Peace against graveyard decks and more Lockdowns versus aggro (Selesnya convoke), while cutting inefficient single-target removal and Carrot Cake against non-aggro matchups. Against Dimir tempo and control, the plan is grinding card advantage with Famished Vault, Braided Net, and repeated Battlefronts to bait counterspells, adding Spell Pierce post-board. The matchup against hard Dimir dragon control is grindy and unfavorable, often lost on the clock.
