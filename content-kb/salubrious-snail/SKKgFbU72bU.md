## Summary

The video argues that pure combo decks struggle in EDH and that combos should be embedded in decks with alternate win conditions. Combo is defined broadly: two/three-card wins on the spot (e.g. Splinter Twin + Pestermite), infinite mana/life combos needing extra pieces, and situational or value combos. Complaints (uninteractive, wins from nowhere, ignores board/life/hand resources) are acknowledged, but breaking convention suits EDH's 40 life and three opponents. As power rises, combo becomes ubiquitous but so do stax and counterspells, so high-power decks should run backup wincons. cEDH example Dargo/Thrasios: Thrasios is a command-zone infinite-mana outlet and value engine; Dargo adds utility (Life's Legacy, Greater Good, Neoform/Pod to fetch Tidespout) plus a beatdown backup. For lower power, combos should fit naturally: if central, back with tutors/draw/control; if slow, pair with a concurrent plan (ramp, draw, or tutors). Vadrok mutate toolbox (Mr. Hans) builds around Sunforger, Glorious End, Chance for Glory + Angel's Grace for an infinite-turns combo, but wins normally via a manland-protected stack and Sunforger boosting Vadrok to 7 power. Inverse example: a $25 Grismold token-Voltron deck adds Blood Artist/Syr Konrad so both wincons share tokens. Principle: alternate wincons should reuse the main plan's resources.
