## Summary

The streamer plays a 2004 Lord of Tresserhorn EDH deck that won Sheldon Menery's original contest, run in its historical form without a command zoneΓÇöthe commander is shuffled into the deck and declared aloud so opponents know its colors. They note Lord of Tresserhorn is a four-mana 10/4 with regenerate, but costs 2 life, a sacrificed creature, and gives an opponent two cards on entry. Played as a slow, removal-heavy control deck with many lands and few creatures; once Tresserhorn lands it's hard to kill via repeated regeneration. Later they brainstorm a Giants tribal commander deck, finding giant creatures are fine but giant-support cards are scarce (~5 good ones), pushing them toward either a spellslinger build (Archmage of Runes), an Atsushi/Korvold-style reanimator using red/white/black giants, or Saskia as a color-fixing shell.
