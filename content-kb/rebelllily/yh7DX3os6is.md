## Summary

This is a red-leaning Commander deck built around Gold Ship (Shadowfax stand-in) as commander, themed on horses and the Aether Drift racing set. Gold Ship has haste and can win through combat; Kellith Sunmane buffs the commander on attack. The deck runs legendary horses (Bill the Pony, Calamity Galloping Inferno, Thurid) plus support horses like Loyal Unicorn, Crested Sunmare, Guardian Sunmare, and Nyx-Fleece Unicorn. Primary plan: pump the commander with auras (All That Glitters, Mantle of the Ancients, Tempest Technique) and add double-combat spells (Aggravated Assault, Full Throttle, Great Train Heist, Overpower) to push lethal combat damage. Ondu Spirit Dancer doubles enchantment value; Aetherspark adds flexibility. A secondary 'glue factory' plan uses copy/trainer effects (Delina, Jaxis, Mirror-Style Master, Echoing Assault, Calamity) to make tapped attacking copies of nonlegendary horses for extra damage. Strixhaven Stadium is an alternate win condition at 10 counters. Card draw is the deck's main weakness, patched with Mask of Memory, Outpost Siege, Thorough Investigation, and Vessel-style draw. Protection is critical given combat reliance: Boros Charm, Legion's Initiative, Mother of Runes, Unbreakable Formation, and Greater Auramancy guard the board and key enchantments. Core build principle: scaling and copying to offset low individual card power.
