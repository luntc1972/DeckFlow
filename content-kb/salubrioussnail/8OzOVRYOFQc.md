## Summary

This video reviews Blight Curse, a Lorwyn Eclipsed -1/-1 counter precon. Its commander, Auntie Ool (4-mana 4/4, Ward: Blight 2), draws a card whenever -1 counters land on a creature you control, or drains 1 life if an opponent controls it—with no once-per-turn limit, enabling explosive card draw. The deck pairs -1 counter sources (some hitting all creatures, others incremental) with payoffs that trigger on counter-creature death, reward placing counters, or amplify via proliferate (Evolution Sage). It also reanimates creatures dying with -1 counters, including alt-commander The Reaper King No More. Strengths: 'Johnny' synergy variety, a viable alternate commander, and a narrow card pool that paradoxically opens many build paths (proliferate, graveyard, opposing-counter Reaper builds). Weaknesses: dead cards, a too-low creature count (27), and Liliana, Death Wielder (an overpriced intro-deck planeswalker). The reviewer's 10-card upgrade cuts dead/niche cards (Liliana, Archfiend of Ifnir, Grim Poppet, filler) and adds low-cost blight creatures, proliferate/removal (Staff of Compleation, Grim Affliction), and lifegain stabilizers (Obelisk Spider, Elegy Acolyte), raising creatures to 32. He praises the precon as grindy, low on obvious cuts, and great for synergy-loving or roguelike-deckbuilder players entering Commander.
