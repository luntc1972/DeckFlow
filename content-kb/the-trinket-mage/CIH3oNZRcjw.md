## Summary

A patron deck-review stream covering several Commander decks. For a Zurgo token deck overloaded with three-drops, the reviewer argues casting the commander on turn three is a trap unless a two-drop is already down; the real fix is maximizing card advantage to draw into pieces and recover from board wipes, plus more flexible sack outlets (Plumb the Forbidden), card-draw engines (Staff of the Storyteller), and cheap token generators (Bitterblossom). Weak cuts: Dalek Squadron, Legion Warboss, Ironwill Forger. For a mono-red Queen Marchesa wizard-tokens deck weak to board wipes, protecting tokens is nearly impossible in red, so the answer is impulse-draw and card advantage (Outpost Siege, Visions of Phyrexia, Bonehoard Dracosaur) to draw out of wipes, while protecting the commander with equipment rather than fragile Boros-style protection. A Flubs Valakut deck wants tutors and redundancy: Muddle the Mixture, Dizzy Spell into Crop Rotation, Kura, Urza's Saga, and Field of the Dead as a second win condition, cutting excess crucible effects and slow fetch lands. For a Dan Dan Haunted Horror deck, cut Fresh Start (poor removal) for more Dress Down, and drop Smoke Bomb.

## Key Clips

- **[18:46]** Either you want to play your commander on three after you have a relevant two drop like a loyal apprentice... Or you want to play your commander later, because if you play him on three, unless you have a two drop, he doesn't do anything. I think casting Zurgo on three is a trap in this deck.
- **[21:06]** Even if it's not an overhaul, I do think you want more sack outlets. The ability to draw cards is still really good. The flexibility of the sack outlets to be let's draw cards now or let's do damage now is really good.
- **[29:52]** What you need is card advantage so you can draw into all of these pieces, dump them all, and then if you get board wiped actually recover. Maxing out on card draw where we can is the best way to improve this deck. You don't need tutors because all the things are kind of interchangeable.
- **[33:34]** Ironwill Forger has lieutenant, so your commander already has to be in play for this to do anything, and then it copies a non-legendary creature giving it myriad. It doesn't work as often as you want it to. I think it is not as consistent as people think it is.
- **[44:45]** You need to juice it up. Don't hold anything back if you're building a commander like this. You can have as many fast mana tutor pieces as you want, it's not going to actually do anything scary when your deck's theme is so jank.
- **[55:53]** Card draw is the answer to beat board wipes in colors like red. What you really want is just the ability to draw your way out of the board wipe. Instead of going for the protection route, we just play all of the effects that let me draw an extra card every turn.
- **[68:10]** When you do decks like this, the answer is ultimately going to be tutors, because you can only do so much with really specific effects. Muddle the Mixture and Dizzy Spell are tutor chains that will get you almost anything you need.
- **[80:52]** I would cut two Fresh Starts for four Dress Downs. The problem with Fresh Start as removal is it doesn't put Haunted Horror in the yard, so you miss out on synergies where you can remove your Haunted Horror, Unearth, get it back.
