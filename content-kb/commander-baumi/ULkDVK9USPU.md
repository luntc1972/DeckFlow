## Summary

This budget Commander deck is built around Celeste, Rune Knight, a four-mana Mardu commander. Her enter ability lets you discard any number of cards and draw that many plus one, filtering your hand to find combo pieces; cast from the command zone this is pure card advantage. Her second ability puts a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control whenever a creature enters from your graveyard. This enables persist combos: persist returns a dying creature with a -1/-1 counter, but Celeste's +1/+1 counter cancels it, so with any sacrifice outlet a persist creature loops infinitely while buffing the whole board. Standout persisters include Murderous Redcap and Putrid Goblin; sacrifice outlets like Carrion Feeder and Goblin Bombardment, plus payoffs Mayhem Devil, Molten Gatekeeper, Ellis-El Core, and Sadistic Pilgrim, finish games. Flicker spells (Cloud Shift, Feign Death) re-trigger Celeste's wheel card-neutrally and act as protection, as does reanimation via Jolted Awake and Phoenix Down. The graveyard becomes a combo toolbox. The deck is streamlined, fast, hard to interact with, and its wins are protected. It went undefeated through tournament prelims before being ganged up on in the finals.
