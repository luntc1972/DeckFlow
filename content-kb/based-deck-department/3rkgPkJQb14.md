## Summary

Video covers three honorable-mention commanders from the Avatar MTG set for Commander brewing. Zuko, Firebending Master: an aggressive Voltron experience-counter commander that wants to generate and spend mana in combat; supported by instant-speed spells, card draw, pump effects, and power doublers. Fits bracket 2, maybe 3, but isn't the strongest Zuko build. Katara, Waterbending Master: an experience-counter control/monoblue commander rewarding instant-speed interaction; after controlling several turns you draw many cards on attack. At only 2 mana she's cheap to recast if she dies in combat, and experience counters persist through recastingΓÇögood for bracket 2 or 3. Lo and Li, Royal Advisers: the base for an Esper/Dimir 'Advisers' typal deck, with roughly 50 advisers available; they pay off mill and discard effects, solving the usual mill/discard problem of depleting opponents without a win condition. Suited to bracket 2. Deckbuilding takeaways: match commanders to power brackets, lean on instant-speed value and recastable cheap commanders that retain experience counters, and pair depletion strategies (mill/discard) with built-in payoffs to close games.
