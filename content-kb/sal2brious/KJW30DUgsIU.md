## Summary

This is a Patron deck-review stream covering 10 Commander decks from normal to experimental. Recurring advice: cut single-purpose cards (evasion-only, wincon-only, situational filler) unless they fill another needed role, and favor cards that unite multiple packages. For the artifact Brago deck, lean into a control/value snowball, add haste enablers and tutor-ETBs (Tribute Mage, Recruiter), and more counterspells against wipes. The Forge treasures-aristocrats deck overspends on weak treasure generators not matched by payoffs; trim them for more sac support and removal. The four-color Lurrus tokens deck should justify the companion via an aristocrats subtheme and add evasive fliers to carry Cranial Plating. Sigarda hatebears needs better hatebears over filler humans and protection for mana dorks. Mono-blue reanimator wants more ramp despite being rockless. Extus affinity lacks instants/sorceries—add recursion like Charmbreaker Devils. Simic land-voltron is pubstompy; add ramp/draw or alternate beatdown. The Aragorn monarch deck should drop pointless lifegain, add real interaction, and unblockable threats. Tayam/Soul Cauldron is intentionally non-combo; add early utility (Green Sun's Zenith) and trim weak Summon creatures. Zimone stax should cut weak planeswalkers and wincons, run more tutors, draw, and accessible ramp.

## Key Clips

- **[01:47]** I think this deck could stand to pivot into more of a control playstyle than it's in currently. Brago can generate an absurd amount of value, and paired with a heavy ramp up of mana this deck is likely going to be looking to snowball and subdue its opponents with pure value as its most potent gameplan.
- **[03:21]** One would be haste enablers—Swiftfoot Boots, Lavaspur Boots. Being able to get an immediate Brago swing has the potential to smooth over a lot of problems—reset some mana rocks to have mana available, repeat some draw ETBs to find protection or answers.
- **[05:53]** Really my biggest issue with this deck is with the treasures package, and that's that the amount of effort it's spending on creating treasures isn't matched by the benefit it's getting from them.
- **[11:56]** A category of card I'm more on board with is the 2 mana ramp humans. They can ramp into the commander on turn 3, and they provide easy acceleration in a deck whose commander provides value. The one thing I will say is that I wouldn't mind seeing them accompanied by a bit more protection. Board wipes will always be the bane of mana dorks.
- **[14:41]** The one thing about this deck that strikes me as being good to change would be to add more ramp. This is a very mana hungry deck, between its high mana curve, its draw density, and its X-ability in the command zone, and some additional ramp seems likely to help it feel able to do what it wants to do earlier.
- **[17:38]** The biggest thing that sticks out to me as a problem for this deck is its lack of instants and sorceries. This is a really critical card type to the success of the deck, and the fact that we only have 16 total in a deck based around a magecraft commander feels like a bit of a problem.
- **[24:03]** The most out of place element here for me is the lifegain. There's not much payoff for it at all. In a Monarch deck, you don't want creatures hitting you in the first place, so lifegain cards don't really make sense—if they're at all useful, you're already in trouble.
- **[30:50]** This deck's plays are complex in the sense that they require a lot of elements to put together, but the strength of those plays doesn't always justify that complexity. I think this deck would be best served to pare back on the sheer number of wincons, and run more tutors and draw to find the ones that are here.
