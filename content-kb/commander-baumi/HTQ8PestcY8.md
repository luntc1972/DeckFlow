## Summary

Esper (WUB) control deck commanded by Ishai and Tevesh Szat, Doom of Fools. Plan A: cheat The Unspeakable into play by making a token (Tevesh) and using Reweave, an arcane polymorph, to flip it into The Unspeakable. The Unspeakable recurs arcane spells, letting the pilot loop Rend Flesh (removal) and Disrupting Shoal (counter) for grindy, near-infinite value. Goryo's Vengeance reanimates The Unspeakable, with Otherworldly Journey flickering it to dodge exile; Mistveil Plains rebuys spells from graveyard to library to keep polymorphing. Sift through Sands (with Peer through Depths and Reach through Mists cast that turn) is a backup way to fetch it. Plan B: Thopter Foundry plus Sword of the Meek under Humility, flooding the board with tokens that commanders beat (Tevesh planeswalker ignores Humility, Ishai keeps +1/+1 counters as a 10/10); Cleanfall blows up Humility to swing for lethal. No mana rocks or ramp; 43 mana sources, heavy lands and MDFCs. Turns 2-3 spent cantripping and tutoring (Demonic Tutor, Eerie Procession) to dig for Reweave by turn six. Roughly 28 removal spells, four board wipes. Other wincons: Shark Typhoon, Mind's Dilation, Bribery. Self-described Bracket 3.

## Key Clips

- Plan A is to use the Unspeakable as a value engine by cheating it into play: create a token with Tevesh Szat, Doom of Fools (one of the commanders), then use Reweave ΓÇö an arcane polymorph ΓÇö to sacrifice the token and polymorph the Unspeakable out of the deck. The Unspeakable then recurs arcane spells like Disrupting Shoal (a budget Force of Will) and Rend Flesh (a three-mana Doom Blade that destroys non-Spirits), looping them to grind out value and attack.
- If the Unspeakable is in the graveyard, Goryo's Vengeance reanimates it with haste to swing, then Otherworldly Journey flickers it so it isn't exiled at end of turn and returns with a counter. Hitting a player returns Goryo's Vengeance to hand to repeat. Alternatively, cracking a white fetch for Mistveil Plains puts a card from the graveyard on the bottom of the library, recurring Reweave to keep the engine going.
- Plan B is Thopter Foundry plus Sword of the Meek under Humility. There is no infinite, but it generates a huge number of tokens in the late game. Both commanders synergize with Humility: Tevesh Szat is a planeswalker so he's unaffected and makes 1/1s instead of 0/0s, and Ishai gets +1/+1 counters that Humility doesn't remove, becoming a 10/10 ΓÇö the biggest thing on the board.
- The deck runs no mana rocks and no ramp ΓÇö 43 mana sources counting MDFCs, plus heavy lands. The commanders only exist to make a token to polymorph and to block, so ramping into them does little. Instead, turns two and three are spent on cantrips and tutors (Demonic Tutor, Case of the Stashed Skeleton, Eerie Procession) to dig for Reweave, with Ishai on turn four, Tevesh on five, and Reweave on six.
- This is firmly a Bracket 3 control deck ΓÇö too grindy for most Bracket 2 lists to keep up with. It's built around about 28 removal spells and powerful staples like Humility and Psychosis used to support intentionally bad cards. It uses strong cards to enable janky lines, like Demonic Tutoring for Eerie Procession to find Reweave to eventually polymorph the Unspeakable.
- Against big-green stompy decks, once the Unspeakable engine is online the deck has effectively infinite removal: as long as opponent card draw is countered, it Rend Fleshes a threat, hits with the Unspeakable to return Rend Flesh, and repeats. Evermind, a free arcane instant with splice onto arcane, adds card draw to each arcane spell to refill. Against multiple creatures, board wipes (Damnation, Deluge, Doomskar, Vanquish the Horde) come in and work well since the deck has no creatures of its own.
- Sift Through Sands was the original card that inspired the deck: a blue-blue-one arcane instant that, if you also cast Peer Through Depths and Reach Through Mists that same turn, searches the library for the Unspeakable and puts it into play. All three are in the deck, but it must be done in one turn, and it only pulls the Unspeakable from the library ΓÇö not the graveyard.
- The brewing philosophy is exploring underdeveloped themes like arcane spells and the splice-onto-arcane mechanic. The package is deliberately small because there simply aren't many arcane spells. If a future set printed a flood of strong arcane cards, the builder would take the deck apart ΓÇö once a theme becomes a cookie-cutter the novelty and challenge of building around the unexplored is gone.
