## Summary

Deck-review stream. Reviewer's core test: can you cast your commander reliably and on-curve? Most reviewed decks can't. Recurring advice: casting on curve matters more than raw land count (a 36-land deck stumbled on off-curve lands like Basilica); add mana rocks/talismans and use goldfishing to find cuts. Per deck: a time-theme control deck should run all talismans and cut generic sacrifice cards. A Mardu Bruse Tarl attack deck plays fine but off-color cards (Gaddock Teeg, Glissa) strain the manabase—add Mardu-colored removal. An Ashling loot deck prefers mana flood, wants cheap rocks it's happy to pitch and cost-reducers over Mana Geyser (a one-spell-per-turn deck). A Marchesa deck (Agatha's Soul Cauldron + Fulminator Mage + Wasteland) wants 1–2 surveil lands and more counters/protection (Boots, Spellskite). Sliver Overlord decks need far more actual slivers plus ramp to seven and recursion (Patriarch's Bidding). An Eowyn humans deck needs board-development enablers, not ramp. A changeling/Ditto deck needs more changelings and fewer payoffs, tutoring Maskwood Nexus. A greedy Irenicus ramp deck wants haste enablers (Rhythm of the Wild, Anger) and cheap protection. Reviewer praises land destruction at bracket three.
