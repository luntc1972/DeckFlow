<objective>
Land Sweep 4 of the WDG audit: remove inline `style`, `onchange`, and inline-event attributes from Error.cshtml + AdminFeedback Index views, replacing them with external CSS rules and a TypeScript change-listener. Per D-05, the AdminFeedback Detail Delete `onsubmit="return confirm(...)"` is DEFERRED out of Phase 11; per D-06 add a one-line deferral comment in `Detail.cshtml` linking to 260513-wdg-FINDINGS.md.

Purpose: Move the app one step closer to strict CSP (`script-src 'self'`, `style-src 'self'`). Per D-05 the Detail Delete is held back because removing the inline handler + losing the CSP-blocked `confirm()` would convert "delete with prompt" into "instant delete" — a security/UX regression. That cleanup ships in v1.4 with a proper modal pattern.

Output: Three Razor views updated (one with handler removal, one with style removal, one with deferral comment only), site-common.css gains scoped class(es) for the Error styling, and admin-feedback.ts gets a change listener.
</objective>
