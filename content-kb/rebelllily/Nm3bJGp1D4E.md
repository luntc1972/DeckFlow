## Summary

This is a deck-fixing stream where the host rescues a Reddit user's Esper (white/blue/black) Commander deck led by Kamiz, a Capenna commander. Kamiz's attack trigger makes a target attacker unblockable and gives it connive, then grants another lesser-power attacker double strike. The host playtests by goldfishing opening hands and finds the original ninja build clunky: it does lots of fiddly work (cheating in small 1/1 ninjas via ninjutsu) for little payoff, since the commander already grants unblockable, making most ninja-enabler cards redundant. Diagnosis: power/toughness too low, weak removal, too few counterspells. Fixes: lean into a +1/+1 counter and proliferate plan (Rafine, Throwing Bird, Grateful Apparition, Danny Pink, Brina, Unscythe/Unbreakable, Oswald's-style discard), add protection (Slip Out the Back, Selfless) and more counters (Negate, Disdainful Stroke, Arcane Denial), cut redundant unblockable-enablers, hard-cast ninjas instead of ninjutsuing, and fix the manabaseΓÇötrim ~37 lands toward 35, add cheap dual lands (Shipwreck Marsh, Deserted Beach, Shattered Sanctum). Goal: bigger threats, real card-draw engine, held-up interaction.
