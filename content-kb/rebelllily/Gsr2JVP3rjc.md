## Summary

Framing: build a Commander deck like Final Fantasy X's sphere grid — commander as center node, cards as clusters/skills, favoring personalization over the optimal build. Worked example on Lathril(?)/"Lal"(?), an elf-token commander, reimagined as a "black mage." Central removal node: Assassin's Trophy, surrounded by Putrefy, Snuff Out, Maelstrom Pulse; Exsanguinate as finisher. Black-mage theme cluster: Fandaniel, Black Mage's Rod, Transpose, Encircled by Black Mages (makes black mage tokens; cares about instants/sorceries). Support the spells cluster with copy effects (Primal Amulet, Panoptic Mirror, Sword of Wealth and Power) and graveyard recast (Toshiro Umezawa, Kirk the Punisher(?), regrowth effects). Turn elf tokens into mana via Cryptolith Rite, Enduring Vitality feeding Exsanguinate; pump the commander with Ozolith, the Shattered Spire and Revitalizing Repast (doubles as a land). Connector cards bridge clusters (Kerric Wrangler(?), Sword of the Animist(?)). Build steps: pick ~3 anchor cards, add 3–7 per cluster, add smaller support clusters, fill to 46–48 nonland cards, then 36–38 lands (fewer with heavy ramp), refining removal and draw while keeping themes.

## Key Clips

- **[01:55]** RPG sphere systems usually start with a defining skill, then surround it with supporting nodes that shape how that skill functions. Our commander grid works the same way. We start with a card that captures the vibe, then build outward with mechanical or thematic connections.
- **[02:15]** Let's use Assassin's Trophy as our central node — cheap, versatile removal. Surround that with other efficient kill spells like Putrefy, Snuff Out, Maelstrom Pulse. And for finishers, Exsanguinate is basically a Fireball in black.
- **[02:58]** Once you unlock a spell, you can usually power it up. We can do the same by adding supporting themes to connect our clusters. For our spells cluster we can have supporting themes of copying spells with Primal Amulet, Panoptic Mirror, and Sword of Feast and Famine.
- **[03:22]** We can replace spells from the graveyard with Toshiro Umezawa, Kroxa, and regrowth effects. Because isn't Regrowth just sorcery-speed Snapcaster in green?
- **[04:00]** The commander makes elf tokens. Tokens mean bodies and bodies mean mana with cards like Cryptolith Rite or Enduring Vitality. So now we've added a mana cluster, one that feeds directly into our big spell grid with Exsanguinate.
- **[05:53]** Start with your commander as the center node, defining cards to anchor those clusters — pick three. Add three to seven cards around those to form the cluster so you see the theme consistently across your games. Then build smaller clusters to support your main themes.
- **[06:16]** Fill out the deck until you have 46 to 48 non-land cards. Then add your land base, 36 to 38 lands, or fewer if you're playing a lot more ramp.
- **[06:24]** Once it starts feeling good, refine your deck using the other models on this channel, like playing the right kind of removal and scaling draw, while trying to maintain your themes.
