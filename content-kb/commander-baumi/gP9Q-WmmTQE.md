## Summary

Baumi critiques three poorly-designed Magic cards and proposes redesigns. Captivating Vampire is a flavorful free-mana 2/2 vampire lord whose tap-five-vampires steal ability grinds games to a stalemate, conflicting with vampires' aggressive identity; he suggests instead a tap ability stealing creatures dealt damage by a vampire that turn, creating a pseudo-evasion lord synergizing with first-strike themes. Humble Defector (2-mana red 2/1, tap to draw two then give it to a chosen player) is meant as a politics card but devolves into two players passing it back and forth for a draw advantage; he proposes the activator gain protection from Humble Defector until the recipient's next turn so it can't be immediately returned, though he dislikes using the complicated protection mechanic. Sitta Valma, Masked Racer (Simic 2/2 with an expensive overrun-style exhaust) is a weak card disconnected from her Edge of Eternities story; he argues she should be Izzet given her rebellious, jealous, Chandra-rival character, with the Skulk keyword, vehicle-crew synergy, and a temporary self-buff reflecting her drug-fueled power spike. Throughout, Baumi values playing cards to their fullest and designing rules that resist negative play incentives.

## Key Clips

- Swords to plowshares exiles a creature and has its controller gain life. Path to exile exiles a creature and then has its controller search out the land. These two cards are incredibly powerful and popular and annoy me to no end because their names should clearly be switched. Swords to plowshares: the creature leaves and we get a land instead. It stops fighting and starts farming.
- Captivating vampire is a four mana black two two vampire. It gives our vampires we control plus one plus one and lets us tap five untapped vampires to gain control over target creature making it a vampire in the process.
- When actually played, captivating vampire is a nightmare because it grinds the game to a halt. More often than not, the best strategy with this card is to tap five vampires every turn to change whatever our opponents have on the board. This then stops our opponents from playing anything new that they might not want to have stolen. Combined, this results in nobody progressing their board and us barely attacking, which puts the game into this tedious grind state.
- How about this? Stats, mana cost, lord ability, all the same. But captivating vampire gets a new tap ability that gains control of target creature that was dealt damage by a vampire this turn. It becomes a vampire. This will turn captivating vampire into a bit of a pseudo evasion lord. We now encourage attacking, which is great because it's a lord and a vampire.
- Humble Defector is a two mana red two one that we can tap on our turn to draw two cards. But when we use this ability, we also have to give the humble defector to another player. At first glance, humble defector seems like a really great multiplayer card. But even more frequently, I've seen it turn into a draw engine for two players only. Two players agree that they'll pass it back and forth on each of their turns, letting them gain a significant lead on the other two players.
- Tap: draw two cards. Target opponent gains control of humble defector. You gain protection from Humble Defector until the end of that player's next turn. Activate only during your turn. Making it so that the player that uses Humble Defector's ability gains protection from it results in the other players not being able to pass the Humble Defector right back.
- Sitta Valma, Masked Racer is a two mana simic two three with a single exhaust ability for X two green and a blue. She can put X plus one plus-one counters on herself and then we may have the base power and toughness of every other creature we control become equal to Sitta's power until end of turn. She's basically an overrun except without trample and while needing a huge mana investment. This is just not a very good card.
- Not even her colors make sense. Why is she simic? Blue, I get. Sitta is an engineer and she puts value on her intellect, but green, she should have red or black in there as she's rebellious and self-centered. I think skolk would be a great fit for the character. It's a black blue keyword that makes it so the creature can't be blocked by creatures with higher power. Sitta is also supposed to be an amazing driver, one of the best racers on the plane. She should interact with vehicles in some capacity.
