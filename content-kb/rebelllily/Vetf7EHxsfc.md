## Summary

This deck tech builds around Ela the Pinnacle, a three-mana 1/1 monk with trample and prowess that creates a 1/1 white prowess monk token for each point of combat damage dealt to a player, scaling with spell volume. The strategy is framed around four martial-arts skills. Punch: cheap pump spells (with flashback/rebound upside) and unblockable enablers act as efficient token generators, while Spectacular Showdown, Temur Battle Rage, and Twinferno escalate damage. Kick: extra-combat spells like Overpowering Attack and Waves of Aggression, plus mass instant-speed pumps (Trumpet Blast, War Flare, Mercadia's Downfall), with protection like Unbreakable Formation and Flawless Maneuver guarding against board wipes. Throw: redirection and bounce answers (Deflecting Swat, Misdirection, Narset's Reversal, Return the Favor, Aetherize) keep opponents off balance. Lock: punisher spells (Comeuppance, Deflecting Palm, Refuse, Sudden Impact). Card selection and draw come from Sensei's Divining Top, Artist's Talent, enduring enchantments, and other masters (Monastery Mentor, Narset, Shiko and Narset, Scroll of the Masters). Mulligan for three mana sources or two lands plus a cantrip; flow with combat, leaving mana open to test opponents.

## Key Clips

- As a martial arts master of infinite potential, this Ela three-fold Master deck is inspired by the four fundamental skills of Chinese martial arts: kick, punch, throw, and lock. Ela three-fold Master is a three-mana 1/1 monk with trample and prowess, and whenever Ela deals combat damage to a player, you create that many 1/1 white monk token creatures who also have prowess. The more spells we cast to grow Ela, the more monks we create.
- The 'punch' package uses cheap pump spells as quick offensive jabs. By playing cheap pump spells we can choose when to exert force to push through a defender or unload our fury for a big attack, with the upside of turning pump effects into efficient token-generator spells. Spectacular Showdown gives Ela double strike permanently, while Temur Battle Rage and Twinflame act as hidden offensive tools.
- The 'kick' package is our haymakers ΓÇö extra combat spells like Overpowering Attack (castable for three mana off Foretell) and Waves of Aggression (with retrace to cast multiple times per game). Extra combat steps are especially powerful because Ela creates a board of monks that also grow with these spells. Trumpet Blast, War Flare, and Mercadia Downfall are instant-speed mass pump spells, with War Flare doubling as a defensive untap.
- The 'throw' package keeps opponents off balance: Deflecting Swat, Misdirection, Narset's Reversal, and Return the Favor redirect an opponent's force back into themselves, plus one Aetherize to blunt an entire attack in a single sweep. We also run Unbreakable Formation and Flawless Maneuver as answers to mass board wipes.
- The 'lock' package binds opponents into punishing positions with four punisher spells like Comeuppance and Deflecting Palm to redirect large amounts of damage, Refuse to punish high-mana-value spells, and Sudden Impact to knock out a player who has drawn too many cards.
