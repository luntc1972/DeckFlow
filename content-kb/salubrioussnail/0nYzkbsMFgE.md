## Summary

A streamer workshops a low-budget (~$25, excluding basics) Katilda Commander deck conceived as a white-pivoted riff on their existing Rada deck. The goal is a slower, grindier, more controlling battlecruiser deck with a higher curve, leaning on cheap ramp (explosive vegetation-style two-land effects) to land five-drops by turn four or five. The core plan is a flicker engine: mass-flicker spells like Ghost Way, Lazel's Acrobatics, and Semester's End paired with recursion creatures (Eternal Witness, Timeless Witness, Green Warden) for value loops, board-wipe dodges, and removal via cards like Parting Gust. He adds a Woodland Bellower + Fierce Empath tutor package (cutting Conjurer's Closet for budget) to chain into key green creatures. White provides strong ETB creatures (Angel of the Ruins, Protector of the Wastes) plus cheap board wipes (Cleansing Nova) and a controlling lean red lacked. Human generators (Cigarda, Reverent Hoplight, Maja, Increasing Devotion) make Katilda a resilient mana engine. Throughout he prunes high-cost or non-flicker cards, sorts by price to trim budget, and playtests opening hands, finding the deck floods on ramp but needs more smoothing draw. He notes it won't join his main pool—built for a budget-deck video project.
