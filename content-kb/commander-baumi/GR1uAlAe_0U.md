## Summary

This video reviews the nine cards worth knowing from the TMNT Universes Beyond set, framed around Commander as a struggle for resources rather than flashy finishers. Honorable mentions: Lessons from Life (better Urban Evolution), Ground Chuck/Dirt Bag (green-only mana doubler with an 8/8 trample body), and Swift the Mice (free removal with blowout potential but inconsistent). Group one: Coin of Mastery buffs creatures via artifact-mana counters; Continue, a white instant reanimating up to four creatures, excels in Orzhov aristocrats doubling ETB/death triggers; Endless Bottle Assault copies itself for scaling ninja-token attacks like Adeline. Group two: Cool but Rude (red looting/discard-payoff class enchantment with a Gamble mode), Super Combo (green replicate bite acting as green's reliable board wipe), and Turtles in Time (blue bounce-sweeper that shuffles opponents' threats away). Top three: April O'Neil, Hacktivist (blue 1/5 repeatable end-step card draw), Ninja Pizza (green food-into-treasure enabler and payoff), and The Ooze (colorless artifact generating mutagen tokens with counter synergies, graveyard hate, and infinite-combo potential). More than half the cards appear in the precon, which also has good lands.
