## Summary

The video advocates building a custom deck template per Commander deck by first defining your ideal game plan, the card types needed to execute it, and the cards needels to protect it/yourself, then running enough of each type. Core heuristic: run 38 lands to reliably hit land drops through turn four (wanted in ~80% of Commander decks). For any other card category (ramp, removal, win conditions), probability guidelines guarantee drawing one copy by a target turn: run 11 copies to have one by turn two, 9 copies by turn five, and 6 copies by turn eight (assuming some card advantage online by then). Want more copies in hand, run proportionally more. A byproduct of this quota-based approach is a streamlined deck: it forces cutting non-critical cards, since only cards that do double duty efficiently fill multiple category quotas. The host warns against greedily adding pet cards or alternate win conditions that muddy the deck and crowd out what the strategy actually needs. Deckbuilding takeaway: assign each slot to a functional category, meet the count for each, and prune anything that doesn't serve the plan.
