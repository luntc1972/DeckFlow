## Summary

This is a livestream of a deck-review session for a Magic: The Gathering Commander deck-building website where Patreon subscribers submit decklists for goldfish-tested feedback. The reviewer's core diagnostic is consistent: most submitted decks fail to cast their commander reliably or fast enough, so mulligan and ramp choices should prioritize getting the commander down on-curve. Recurring fixes include adding mana rocks and lands, lowering chunky high-mana curves, and cutting off-color or hard-to-cast cards (e.g., triple-pip spells) that stress the manabase. Commander-specific guidance dominates: board-state commanders (Eowyn) need cheap enablers and payoffs rather than ramp; loot/rummage commanders (Ashling) prefer flooding plus cost-reducers; tribal commanders (Sliver Overlord, changeling Dittos) need far more on-type creatures plus tutors like Maskwood Nexus and protection. Bracket-four spellslinger decks need true acceleration (Moxes, Chrome Mox), Rhystic Study, and Mystic Remora to mulligan into a turn-three commander. Strong 'midrange beatdown' decks earn praise, with minor color-fixing and surveil-land tweaks. Closing advice: build a consistent core plan first, then add interaction and protection (boots, counterspells) afterward.
