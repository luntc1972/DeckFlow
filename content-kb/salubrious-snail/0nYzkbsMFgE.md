## Summary

The streamer workshops a budget (~$25, excluding basic lands) Katilda Commander deck on stream, framing it as a white-inclusive riff on their existing Rada deck: a higher-curve, slower, grindier battle-cruiser leaning toward control. Core plan is value flickerΓÇöexile-and-return-at-end-of-turn effects (Lazel's Acrobatics, Ghost Way, Semester's End, Parting Gust) paired with strong white/green ETB creatures (Angel of the Ruins, Protector of the Wastes) and recursion via three Eternal Witness-style cards (Eternal Witness, Timeless Witness, Green Warden) for value loops; flicker also dodges removal and board wipes. Ramp inverts the curve with two-land effects (Vastwood Surge, explosive vegetation) plus human generators (Maja, Reverent Hoplight, increasing devotion) so the deck recovers mana even if Katilda is removed. Roughly 41 lands with a cheap cave/temple manabase. Budget forces hard cuts: pricey flicker eats the budget, so removal and board wipes must stay cheap (Cleansing Nova, Austere Command, Titanic Command for graveyard hate). A Woodland Bellower + Fierce Empath tutor package is added, cutting Conjurer's Closet. Genesis Storm and Torsten serve as payoffs; Cascade cards trimmed. Goal is a fun, low-bracket-3 grindy deck, pending more playtesting for draw smoothing.
