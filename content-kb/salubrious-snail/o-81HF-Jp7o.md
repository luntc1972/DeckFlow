## Summary

The creator overviews his Sharuum Esper cycling deck, a control deck built around Astral Slide, which flickers a creature whenever a card is cycled to reuse ETBs and remove threats. The other seed card is Oversold Cemetery, a graveyard recursion engine enabled by cycling. Requirements he set: a high-30s count of cycling cards (including all 14 Esper cycling lands), at least 30 creatures (runs 35) for Oversold Cemetery, tutors to find the three Astral Slide-style enchantments, and about 7 draw-and-discard effects to feed the graveyard. Because slots are tight, cards must fill multiple roles or be best-in-class. It's a toolbox of conditional tutors: artifact mages, Sphinx Summoner, Tribute Mage (finds Fluctuator), Treasure Mage, Zur, two Wizardcycling cards, Waterlogged Teachings, and Transmute cards (replacing Idyllic Tutor). Finishers are Magister Sphinx (sets a life total to 10) and Herald of the Forgotten (returns cycling creatures and lands). Other includes: Nimble Obstructionist, Monstrosity of the Lake, Terisiare's Devastation (ramp/wipe in place of a hard wipe), Ertai Resurrected, Tameshi. He argues conditional tutors add variety. Cut as not worth it: Curator of Mysteries, Jo Grant, Drake Haven, Abandoned Sarcophagus.
