## Summary

This is not a strategy video but a comedy game show called "Commander Jeopardy: horribly self-drawn cards edition," hosted by Bobby with guests Pleasant Kenobi, Free Elk, and Quip and Guac. Players guess Magic: The Gathering cards from intentionally bad hand-drawn art across categories: iconic commanders, 2025 cards, game changers, salty cards, and the host's playgroup commanders. Cards identified include Grave Pact, Vexing Bauble, Kenrith, Demonic Tutor (Decipher), Teferi's Protection, Wrenn and Six, Balan, Urza Lord High Artificer, Yuriko, Mothman, Gamble, Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale, Grand Arbiter Augustin IV, Scrambleverse, Vivi, Sauron, and Zurzoth Chaos Rider. Stray strategy remarks surface: Zurzoth plus Goblin Bombardment makes an aggressive burn deck despite a group-hug reputation; Asmodeus is effectively a repeatable Chaos Warp; Mothman is now a mill commander alongside Bruvac; Tabernacle anchors a slow, interactive lands deck. Final Jeopardy reveals a mono-black demon reanimator deck (Ardenn/demon commander) running Blood Money, Massacre Worm, Black Market, and Bloodlust Inciter. Elk wins. The game is sold on the host's Patreon as a PowerPoint/Google Slides file.
