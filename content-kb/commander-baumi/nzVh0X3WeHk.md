## Summary

The creator built a Cxa Titan of Dead's Hunger discard deck that won by making opponents discard, but it ran out of cards mid-to-late game except when Geier Reach Grimoire was in play — a four-mana colorless artifact that lets you draw a card whenever an opponent discards. After disassembling that deck and seeing a friend's Zur chaos deck exploit the same card, the creator theorized you don't need a dedicated discard deck to abuse it: opponents discard constantly anyway via looting, cycling, channel lands, additional costs, and end-of-turn hand-size discards. They began including Geier Reach Grimoire in every deck with zero synergy support and were drawing 10+ cards regularly, especially against opponents with strong draw engines capped by maximum hand size. Weaknesses: four mana is steep, you can't trigger it yourself, and it's blanked by Reliquary Tower / Thought Vessel removing opponents' hand limits. To enable it, they recommend red wheels (Magus of the Wheel), blue looters (Merfolk Looter, Windfall), black discard (Rankle, The Eldest Reborn), white draw-givers (Laurine), green targeted land destruction to kill hand-size enablers, and especially the colorless Geier Reach Sanitarium, which loots all players each turn.

## Key Clips

- Guest Grimoire is a four-mana colorless artifact that says whenever an opponent discards a card from their hand you may draw a card. In a discard deck like Tergrid/CXA it's amazing because you're making opponents discard all the time, letting you replenish resources without much struggle.
- The core theory: you don't need a dedicated discard deck. Opponents already discard constantly in Commander — Shoreline Looter loots, cards like Big Score use discard as an additional cost, channel lands, cycling lands, and most importantly every player discards down to seven at end of turn. One or two such effects per player and Guest Grimoire starts drawing you four, five, ten cards.
- It triggers off opponents' own strong card-draw engines: opponents who overdraw but are limited by maximum hand size feed your Grimoire. Overdrawing was the most common way people discarded, and drawing 10+ cards off it was not unusual.
- The card is included with no synergy support — you can't trigger it yourself, which is clunky and unreliable. It also gets countered by Reliquary Tower / 'Fort Wesser' (no maximum hand size) — opponents with those won't discard to hand size, so the Grimoire draws nothing. The fix is to enable it yourself rather than play it bare.
- Color-specific enablers: Red uses wheels like Magus of the Wheel — with Grimoire a wheel gives you ~600 cards to sculpt from. Blue has windfall plus Ledger Broker, a two-mana 1/1 that taps to make all players loot, triggering the Grimoire each turn. Black has discard attached to good utility cards like Rankle, Master of Pranks and The Eldest Reborn.
- White and green can't force discard directly. White uses Secret Rendezvous on a player who already has too many cards so they must discard at end of turn, and Loran of the Third Path taps to make you and another player draw — wait for a player at seven cards on their end step, draw them up, and you get an extra card. Green just needs targeted land destruction like Demolition Field or Reclamation Sage to blow up Reliquary Tower.
- The best pairing is Geier Reach Sanitarium: a colorless land that enters untapped and for two mana taps to make all players loot — a free-color land that draws four cards every turn. Both Geier Reach Sanitarium and Guest Grimoire are colorless, so the simplest universal shell is those two cards in any deck.
- Honest caveat: the card sometimes does nothing — played against Reliquary Tower / no-max-hand-size opponents it drew zero. It's an A-plus only most of the time. The challenge to skeptics: next game just note how often players discard, and you'll be surprised how many Guest Grimoire triggers you're missing.
