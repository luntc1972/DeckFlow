## Summary

A streamer plays a self-described bracket-three Commander deck on Magic: The Gathering Online, built quickly with unbalanced lands. The commander is a Sultai (Kotis-style) creature that is indestructible, grows with +1/+1 counters, and casts cards exiled from opponents' decks. The plan, as the player states it, is mainly to play protection and counterspells (Misdirection, Negate, Snakeskin Veil, Rewind, Aetherize) while leeching value off opponents' spells via cast-from-exile triggers, then pseudo-Voltron the commander with equipment like Power Fist to flip and stack counters. The player repeatedly notes the deck does 'nothing' on its own and depends on opponents casting impactful spells, calling the design toxic and unfun but wanting to make opponents concede. Key cards seen: Greater Good (sacrifice for cards), Strip Mine plus Crucible of Worlds for a land-lock, Blasphemous Act and a delayed Fireball flipped off exile. The player admits to misplaying mana (not equipping Power Fist early) and loses after opponents resolve Living Death and stick a 6/6 the deck cannot answer. Lands ran too low; the player suggests adding filter lands and Glittering Wish-style fixing.
