## Summary

This video explains the Commander bracket system (1 through 4/cEDH) through two lenses: the "spirit" of each bracket and when/how a deck wins. Bracket 1 is purely theme-driven (examples given: a "chair tribal" deck where every art has a chair, or an "oops all grippers" feet-themed deck); it typically isn't winning before turn 9 or 10, and dinosaur/vampire tribal decks do NOT count as bracket 1. Bracket 2 is where most decks actually sit; it is not for weak or powered-down decks, can do powerful things, but game-winners must be telegraphed and seen coming — you can't win out of nowhere with a two-card combo — and games end around turn 8. Bracket 3 is more powerful than people think: games end turn 6-7 via an infinite combo, often a two-card infinite combo. Bracket 4 covers decks people think are fringe cEDH: games end turn 4-5, often with no combat, relying solely on infinite combos. True cEDH means picking from a set list of meta decks after studying the meta, not brewing your own. The host's stated biggest observed mistake: people mislabel their own or others' decks a bracket higher than they really are.
