## Summary

The deck is built around Infinite Guideline Station, a five-color artifact vessel commander that makes a Service Unit robot for each multicolor permanent on entry, then becomes a 7/15 flyer drawing a card per multicolor permanent once crewed with 12 power. Multicolor permanents both feed the ETB and refuel the draw. The core theme is chaos via cascade: Imskir/Chiml the Inner Sun, Dark Apostle, Deny Reality, Time Vortex, Maelstrom Nexus, Let the Galaxy Burn, and Ydris, supported by space/time cards like Decaying Time Loop, Planetary Annihilation, Quantum Misalignment, and planeswalker Space Vaelerin. A second axis is an Eldrazi swarm using warp, devoid, annihilator, and spawn/scion token makers (Kozilek's Unsealing, Titan's Vanguard, From Beyond, Sire of Stagnation, exile-matters payoffs). A third is a Sliver tribal package (Sliver Queen, taunting/regal/harmonic slivers). Hive Stone, Brukarell Vex, and Ghostflame Sliver merge slivers with Eldrazi. About 14 ramp sources plus fetch-land recursion (Crucible/Conduit of Worlds, Zaxara) ensure land drops. The plan is straightforward: ramp, deploy threats, power up the station, and win by overwhelming the board with Eldrazi and slivers. Interaction is limited; the deck plays to be the recurring threat.
