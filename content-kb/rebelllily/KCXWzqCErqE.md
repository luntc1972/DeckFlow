## Summary

This video promotes Commander Template, a Magic: The Gathering deck-building website built by the creator to speed up online Commander deck construction. The tool recommends ramp, card draw, and interaction counts (12 each for a four-mana commander like Yidris) personalized to the commander's mana value, drawing on the creator's prior research into mana curves and value theory. It auto-suggests themes based on the selected commander, then searches Scryfall for theme-matching cards within the commander's color identity, letting builders add packages of roughly eight cards each. A standout feature auto-builds the land base: it curates land counts to mana value, populates basics by the deck's color pip distribution, updates dynamically as cards change, and offers dropdowns for fetches, shocks, and bond lands. 'Intelligence mode' overlays hypergeometric probability data and a mana-curve chart showing where to cut or add cards, anchored to an adjustable 'operational threshold' (default = commander's mana value). A goldfishing tool lets builders mark cards to keep or cut while iterating toward a tuned 100-card deck. The site runs no ads, offers free accounts saving five decks, and charges $5 for unlimited decks to cover server costs.

It just happens to be that way because Yidris is four mana. And if you select a different commander, it will recommend you a totally different set of numbers based on the commander's mana value.
- When you add cards within these packages, it automatically searches Scryfall for cards of your commander's color identity for that theme. So, here you can see for Spell Singer, I have a page of classic Spell Singer cards with an Esper color identity that I'll add eight of. This way we can stay within our deck building experience instead of jumping back and forth across a hundred windows.
- The land base numbers are also curated to your commander's mana value and commander template can automatically populate basics based on the color distribution of spells you have in your deck. So if my Yidris storm deck has more blue spells it would lean to have more islands versus plains and swamps. And it will also automatically update as you build and tune your deck and swap cards. You can also add common cycles of lands like fetches, shocks, and bond lands.
- Now, let's turn on one of the best parts of Commander Template, intelligence mode. Intelligence mode takes all the hyper geometric probability work we do in deciding our numbers, figuring out how many cards will appear on an average opening hand, how many you'll see in a few draws. One of the most useful features I use is the mana curve chart at the top of the page. It shows which mana value we should be cutting cards and which mana value we should be adding cards.
- The operational threshold is basically the turn or mana value your commander really starts to do the thing. By default, it's always set to your commander's mana value because usually your commander is the enabler to set everything else up. But, if you built your deck to focus more on the late game, you can always toggle it or adjust it to be higher, which will then change all the numbers across your entire list.
- The goal is to use Commander Template to quickly get to 100 cards as fast as you can, so you can start goldfishing to see how it plays, then refine and keep iterating until it feels good. As you goldfish, you can mark cards that you like or you want to cut or maybe you want to keep a close eye on with a question mark. This creates an organic process of getting to 100, goldfishing, making notes, cutting, changing, and iterating.
