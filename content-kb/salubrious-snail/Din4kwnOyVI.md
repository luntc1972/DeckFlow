## Summary

This video walks through building a high-power but budget-constrained cEDH-adjacent Commander deck led by partner commanders Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator and Francisco, Fowl Marauder. The core plan: deploy a one-cost pirate turn 1, Francisco turn 2, Malcolm turn 3 to bring a treasure-generating, filtering engine online fast. Ten one-mana pirates support this. The wincon is an infinite-turns combo with Time Sieve: Malcolm makes ~3 treasures per attack, Time Sieve spends 5 artifacts for an extra turn, and artifact-doublers like Academy Manufactor, Storm Fleet Negotiator, and Worldwalker Helm bridge the gap. Artifact tutors (Fabricate, Reshape, Whir of Invention) plus transmute cards find the pieces. The deck is highly consistent, mapping to a five-turn plan, comboing turn 5 typically. Supporting cards include all four Bident of Thassa draw-on-damage effects, mana rocks, extra pirates, and an alternate Haunted One wincon. Thirteen counterspells exploit treasures as never-wasted mana. The 35-land manabase prioritizes untapped colored mana. Weaknesses: no early interaction to protect Malcolm, reliance on combat damage, and poor recovery from setbacks due to low card quality. The deck illustrates that budget high-power decks need efficient gameplans plus high interaction density.
