## Summary

The creator runs a 50-euro budget competitive Commander tournament ('bracket 50') and is brewing a deck for the upcoming March event. The core obsession is Omniscience, a 10-mana enchantment that lets you cast spells for free—effectively winning once it resolves. Since hardcasting 10 mana is infeasible, the plan is to cheat it into play via Polymorph effects (Reality Scramble, Chaotic Transformation, Reweave) that find an enchantment. Reanimation and Iluna were rejected as too vulnerable. The build uses Izzet colors with commanders Volo, Itinerant Scholar plus the Guild Artisan background; Guild Artisan's treasures enable a smooth curve to six mana by turn four to Polymorph a treasure or background into Omniscience. Volo provides massive card draw. Backup win conditions hidden in the deck include God-Pharaoh's Statue, Hullbreaker Horror, Tide Spout Tyrant, and Niv-Mizzet, Parun, plus Izzet two-card combos. The deck is built as a value/interaction pile that plays the long game, using low-commitment win attempts (like Silence) rather than over-committing. Key lesson: it's easier to win than to stop three opponents, so prioritize your own proactive strategy. Plays 38 lands.

- **[26:13]** This deck can win on turn four, but it usually doesn't. I've had plenty of games where I had the Polymorph in hand ready to go, but I waited because I can also play the long game, whereas my opponents will eventually run out of resources. Once they did, my win was much more secure.
