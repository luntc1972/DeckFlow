## Summary

Haast, Erupting Nul Kite is a Dragon whose cast trigger lets you exile your hand and draw three cards. It carries merge, allowing you to sacrifice another creature so Haast effectively pops out of it, and it also grants emerge to your other creatures. Because emerge discounts a creature's cost by sacrificing another, high mana value creatures like Emrakul, the Promised End make strong sacrifice fodder for casting an early Haast or another emerge creature. An alternative line is to activate Sneak Attack to cheat a big creature into play, then sacrifice that creature to an emerge cost afterward, squeezing extra value before it would be returned or lost. The deck's core loop is using cheap or temporary bodies to fuel emerge and merge, turning Haast into a recurring source of card advantage via its exile-and-draw-three trigger.
