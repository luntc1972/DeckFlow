## Summary

Precious Wanderings is a three-mana green instant from Final Fantasy that fetches a basic land or a town into play tapped and adds a +1/+1 counter to a creature you control. The speaker argues it isn't meant to replace two-mana staples like Rampant Growth or Arcane Signet, but to serve as a redundant, utility-rich ramp option that plays well in mid-to-late-game social Commander, where instant-speed flexibility matters more than raw speed. Its standout value: it can fetch dual-color towns (mana fixing most ramp can't provide) and useful utility lands, and being an instant lets you hold mana open for interaction or react to opponents before committing. The +1/+1 counter doubles as a surprise combat trick in decks that never run them, giving the card a high 'kill count' since players treat combat as static. The speaker stresses instant-speed ramp and ramp-as-removal are both rare niches, making this an easy, powerful include across many decks. He plans to pick up multiple copies.

## Key Clips

- Precious Wanderings is a green free mana instant from Final Fantasy that lets us search our library for a basic land or a town card and put it into play tapped. Then we also put the plus one plus one counter on target creature we control.
- It's not supposed to replace the existing two mana ramp, but instead to be an additional three mana utility ramp option. Most ramp is basics only, while precious wanderings can get two color lands.
- What truly elevates precious wanderings is that it's an instant. It allows us to keep mana open to interact with our opponents or to just take our time figuring out which way to spend our mana is the most useful in the given situation. Instant speed ramp is a really narrow category.
- It also gives us a plus one plus one counter, making it a combat trick. It's a combat trick that's playable in decks that don't usually play them because it's actually a ramp spell. And that means this thing will have a really high kill count. Ramp that's also removal at the same time is even rarer than instant speed ramp.
- Haste Magic is a two mana red instant that gives a creature plus three plus one and haste until end of turn. Then we exile the top card of our deck and we may play it until our next end step. Haste Magic doesn't specify that we need to control the creature. We can use it to help one of our opponents get a favorable attack in or even to manipulate combat into an unexpected situation.
- Diamond Weapon is a nine mana green 8/8 that costs one less to cast for each permanent card in our graveyard, letting it go as low as two mana. It also has reach and is immune to all combat damage. Diamond Weapon is the kind of card that will excel in self focus decks where it will immediately get heated into a greater good to draw eight cards.
- Pew Pew UFO is a two mana colorless 0/4 with flying that also taps to pull a land from our hand into play. The color that benefits most from this kind of ramping is actually red, because they impulse draw a lot, then play their exiled lands, and then don't have any more land drops for the lands in their actual hand.
- Side Quest Catch a Fish is a free mana white enchantment that says at the beginning of our upkeep, we look at the top card of our library and if it's a creature or artifact, we may reveal it. If we do, we put it into our hand, create a food token, and then flip the enchantment. In decks that play enough creatures or artifacts, this is essentially three mana, draw a card, get a food, ramp a strong land.
