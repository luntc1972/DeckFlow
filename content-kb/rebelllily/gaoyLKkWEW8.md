## Summary

Sovereign Okinec Ahau pumps each creature you control by an amount equal to the difference between its current power and its base power. This turns temporary pump spells into permanent buffs: cast Giant Growth on a creature, attack, and Okinec Ahau converts that bonus into permanent +1/+1 counters. The synergy works with any buff source. Pairing it with Arwen, Weaver of Hope means combat triggers stack counters (Giant Growth's +3 plus existing +1/+1 effects). Creatures that thrive on counters, like Steel Bane Hydra, effectively double their counters on every attack. The deck wants Anthem effects (such as Always Watching), equipment (Blackblade Reforged), and auras (Ethereal Armor) since the eventual payoff is permanent +1/+1 counters. Big modifier cards like Hamza, Guardian of Arashin or Clovis from Warhammer 40K make the board especially powerful. The core strategy: layer power-boosting effects, attack with Okinec Ahau out, and lock in the gains as counters that persist across turns.
