## Key Clips

- Resistance is a common narrative theme of magic storytelling. The resistance of the Mirans battling for the fate of their plane against the Phyrexian invaders. The resistance of the Zendikari against the ruination of their land by the Eldrazi.
- Across the sets of Zendikar, allies are unique in how they strengthen each other through their presence. Either through something like Akoum Battlesinger in original Zendikar to the mechanic cohort from Oath of the Gatewatch where you activated a physical cohort to create a unique effect. The individuality of each card is what makes allies feel like a resistance force.

- The way we do so is through the theme of martyrdom. Resistance stories cannot be told without sacrifice. Through sacrifice is how we turn our nameless ally tokens into heroes to be remembered on the ledger.
- The liberators of Aether Revolt like Vengeful Rebel, Solemn Recruit, or the classic Renegade Rallier all come with an effect when a permanent you control has left the battlefield. If Zendikar taught us synergy, Aether Revolt teaches us sacrifice.
- Power of Heroes sacrifices an ally to bring one greater to the cause. Breath of Fury acts as an all-consuming finisher, allowing each ally to push themselves to the end to rally the others for extra combat steps. Smile at Death is a flavorful form of recursion that captures the devotion required by those willing to pay the cost for freedom.
- The Mirrans battling Phyrexia capture the theme of inspiration through the mechanic of battlecry. Roar of Resistance is a greater representation of this rallying inspiration where outside of pumping up your own attackers, you can also rally the other players at the table to battle a common enemy.
