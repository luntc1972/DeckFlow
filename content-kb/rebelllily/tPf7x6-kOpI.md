## Summary

This deck-building video describes a tribal Assassins Commander deck built around playing as many Assassin creatures as possible, which the creator sorts into four main categories. The recommended build is mono-black with a light blue splash. Mono-black supplies most of the Assassins plus other powerful cards from the set, while the blue splash adds access to several strong cards. The deck also runs legendary equipment referred to as ISU artifacts. Piloting is intentionally simple: an ideal opening hand needs two Swamps and a high-mana-value Assassin, and that is generally enough to function. The deck is nearly castable entirely off a single key artifact ("Etso"), so the core game plan is to play Etso, attack the player who cannot block, and then begin casting Assassins. The creator emphasizes the deck's low complexity and consistency, noting it is easy to operate once the early mana and a payoff Assassin are in hand. A full decklist is provided in the video description.
