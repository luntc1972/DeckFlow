- **[02:41]** My opinion is that this deck would benefit from deciding where it wants to focus its attention and then committing to that—both a later game plan that focuses more heavily on Reaper King triggers and an earlier game plan that focuses more on artifact beats are valid plans, and choosing one of these to focus more heavily on will improve the deck.
- **[05:11]** The other question I have is how this deck plans to spend all of its mana, given that it has a lot of ramp and not a lot of draw. There are certainly a handful of manasinks in the deck, but it might not be a bad idea to add a couple more or a bit more draw to reduce the number of situations where the deck has mana and no place to put it.
- **[07:36]** Minimizing dead draws is still useful and it might even be worthwhile to trade out the Counterspell for something more general like Unsubstantiate or Decisive Denial.
- **[10:24]** If the intention was more to go infinite, all it would take is likely adding some haste granters such as Temur Ascendancy and Urabrask the Hidden.
- **[13:12]** Adding in a bit more in the way of defense such as a Dissipation Field or some other options for bouncing opposing permanents seems like it'd help to keep back opposing pressure until the deck is closer to finishing people off.
- **[17:11]** There are a lot of decent options for removal that could be Kellan grabbable—Rec Sage, Haywire Mire, Skyclave Apparition, or even a stax piece like Thalia, Guardian of Thraben. That's what I'd go into at least, something to slow opponents down a bit.
