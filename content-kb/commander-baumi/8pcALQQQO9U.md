## Summary

This deck is built around the Millennium Calendar, a one-mana legendary artifact that accrues time counters whenever you untap a permanent during your untap step, can tap for two mana to double its counters, and once at 1,000+ counters is sacrificed to make each opponent lose 1,000 life. The commander is Zolatoyak the Smiling Flood, a Simic 6/6 that puts flood counters on lands (making them islands) and at end step untaps each permanent with a counter on it—untapping the calendar and lands for mana. The build runs Bracket 2 with no infinites, no game changers, and a ~€50 budget. Untap support includes Unstoppable Plan, Manifold Key, and Sphinx/Shadow of the Second Sun (extra untap step). Mana comes from untap-ramping land enchantments (Utopia Sprawl, Fertile Ground, Market Festival) plus light land ramp. Eluge, the Shoreless Sea synergizes with flood counters and cheapens instants. The deck is fragile, so it leans heavily on defensive counterspells, island-matters sweepers (Scourge of Fleets), bounce, and tutors—all to protect the calendar long enough to trigger it once.

## Key Clips

- The Millennium Calendar is a one-mana legendary artifact from the Lost Caverns of Ixalan. It has three abilities. The first puts a time counter on it whenever we untap a permanent during our untap step. For two mana, we can tap it to double the number of time counters. Finally, once it reaches 1,000 or more counters, we sacrifice it and each of our opponents loses 1,000 life.
- We are playing bracket 2. No infinites, no game changers, and we'll loosely stick to a €50 budget. Yes, that means no Seedborn Muse, which would really have made this idea a whole lot easier.
- Zolatoyak the Smiling Flood is a six-mana Simic 6/6 Salamander Serpent. It puts a flood counter on a land of our choice whenever it enters or attacks, turning that land into an island. At the beginning of our end step, it untaps each permanent we control with a counter on it. Since the calendar gathers its own counters, we can use this to easily untap it, and it also lets us untap a portion of our lands for mana.
- The sickest untappers are Sphinx of the Second Sun and Shadow of the Second Sun, both of which cost quite a bit of mana but give us an entire second beginning phase at the end of our turn, meaning we get to untap, upkeep, and draw. Because the second sun cards give us another proper untap step, they actually work with the calendar.
- Untap ramping is a really good way of quickly generating a lot of mana. This is done by attaching land enchantments like Utopia Sprawl, Fertile Ground, and Market Festival to a small number of lands, often stacking multiple on the same, and then untapping those lands repeatedly. More vulnerable than regular land ramp, but significantly faster, and it works great here because we're already planning on untapping our permanents.
- Eluge the Shoreless Sea is a four-mana elemental fish whose stats are as high as the number of islands we control. When it enters or attacks, it puts a flood counter on a land, turning it into an island, and the first instant or sorcery spell we cast each turn costs one blue mana less for each land we control with a flood counter on it. This works amazingly with Zolatoyak because they both put and synergize with flood counters.
- We play a lot of interaction, most of which are counter spells. These are almost exclusively defensive — we need to keep them in hand to protect the Millennium Calendar. We really only get one attempt at this per game, so we need to be ready to force our way through. We also play island-based sweepers with Scourge of Fleets and Spectral Deluge, which work great because Zolatoyak turns all of our lands into islands.
- This deck is terrible — well, to be fair, it's mostly the Millennium Calendar that's terrible. It is a reasonable, playable deck; it's just really hard to make the Millennium Calendar work. If you want to turn this into a proper deck, defocus the calendar a little, put in some more generic good-stuff pieces, maybe some landfall, maybe an artifact shell with Simulacrum Synthesizer if you want to double the budget.
