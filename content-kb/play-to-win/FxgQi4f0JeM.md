## Summary

A cEDH discussion argues that metagaming is far harder in Commander than in a format like VGC because of the multiplayer, singleton nature of the game. A single answer such as Deflecting Swat may be good against one opponent but useless against others, so you only slow a percentage of your opponents a percentage of the time. Singleton deckbuilding also makes specific silver-bullet answers hard to assemble. The core stated principle: over the past 5–10 years, threats have become better than answers. They claim Necropotence is better than Force of Will, and Underworld Breach ("Under a Breach") is stronger than Into the Flood Maw ("Into the Flood Mod"). The reasoning: it is better to win than to stop opponents from winning, because stopping opponents does not guarantee you win when there are multiple opponents — you must stop all three, and in doing so you are likely also stopping yourself from winning. Takeaway for deckbuilding: prioritize proactive threats/win conditions over reactive answers in multiplayer cEDH.
