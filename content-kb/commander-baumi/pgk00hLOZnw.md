## Summary

General primer on each color's core Commander strategies for newer players, covering monocolor identities only. White: cheap/low-power creature swarms (Tocasia's Welcome, Welcoming Vampire) boosted via token makers like Felidar Retreat and Wedding Announcement; excels at protecting permanents (Clever Concealment, Dawn's Truce, Restoration Magic); has top removal (Swords to Plowshares, Farewell); reanimates mana-value-3-or-less permanents (Sun Titan, Sevinne's Reclamation) paired with fetch lands for ramp; runs pillow-fort/stax (Rule of Law, Spirit of the Labyrinth, Rest in Peace); best equipment color. Blue: strong flicker and counterspells (Mana Drain, Fierce Guardianship), with a stated 3-question rule for when to counter; weak hard removal, relying on bounce (Cyclonic Rift); best generic artifact synergy and clones; Rhystic Study called the second-strongest card in Commander but rare in practice. Black: best at graveyard/sacrifice, reanimation (Reanimate), and tutors (Demonic Tutor), called the most powerful effect in Commander. Red: spellslinger with weak counters/removal but strong treasures, rituals, storm decks, and wheel effects. Green: best ramp and landfall, biggest tokens, strongest +1/+1 counters, weak removal, best creature tutors.
