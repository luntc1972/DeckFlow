## Summary

This is a Magic Jeopardy trivia game show hosted by Bomi featuring creators Salubrious Snail, Panzer MTG, and Unpopular MTG, mixing game-show formats with MTG knowledge. Embedded audience-survey results reveal community Commander norms: from 138 responses, three board wipes per deck was the most popular answer (49 votes), with two also common and very few choosing one. For land counts, 38 lands led with 55 votes, followed by 40, and 37; Panzer admits running only 34-35 lands (counting MDFCs), which the others say causes mulligan and consistency problems unless paired with aggressive, fast, high-powered decks. Combo ranked as the most-feared and considered-strongest archetype, with lands/landfall, stacks, control, and aristocrats also cited; ideal Commander game length clustered around 60 minutes (an hour led, then 90 minutes). The final challenge tasks players with building interesting-yet-functional decks around assigned commanders: Snail wins with a Quintorius artifact deck built around Altar of Dementia for graveyard synergy, beating Panzer's Lyzolda Rakdos sacrifice build (whose commander was underused) and Unpopular's Rashmi polymorph-into-hidden-commanders concept. Trivia covers banned cards, set lore, and pricing of collectible foils.
