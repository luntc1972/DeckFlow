## Summary

A two-host video on seven "unkillable" high-power Commander decks, each built to keep its commander recurring. Slimefoot and Squee: a Jund sacrifice/reanimator deck that wants Saprolings (Tendershoot Dryad, Golgari Germination), free sac outlets, and payoffs (Mayhem Devil, Warren Soul Trader, Phyrexian Altar, Blood Artist/Zulaport Cutthroat) to reanimate the graveyard, going infinite for one BRG per loop; reanimate big targets like Vilis and Razaketh. Etali, Primal Conqueror: ramp/rituals (Tinder Wall, Seething Song, Birds, Cultivate) to hit repeated ETBs via sac/flicker/copy (Twinflame, Conjurer's Closet); greener builds are fairer, redder builds turbo; Food Chain + Squee is the combo. Dargo, the Shipwrecker: sac artifacts/creatures to slash his cost; Prized Statue, Goblin Bombardment, Birgi, Phyrexian Altar, and Throes enable infinite casts. Roxanne, Starfall Savant: meteorites deal 2 and tap for doubled mana; Second Harvest, Academy Manufacturer, treasures, artifact payoffs. Athreos, God of Passage: aristocrats that bully one low-life opponent (Shadowborn Apostle, Cursed Marauder, Blood Pet, Bloodletter of Aclazotz, Razaketh). Uro: turn-3 Uro/turn-4 escape via self-mill (Hedron Crab, Frantic Search, Aftermath Analyst), landfall (Scute Swarm), Greater Good, Crystal Shard. Witherbloom the Balancer(?): affinity-for-creatures reduces spells; Torment of Hailfire, Stinging Study, Spontaneous Artist(?)/Sprout Swarm infinite combo.

## Key Clips

- **[01:55]** You could kill Slimefoot and Squee, but it goes to the graveyard — that's where they want it most, because every time it's there they can reanimate any creature in their graveyard for just four mana.
- **[03:26]** With things like Golgari Germination — whenever a non-token creature you control dies, create a 1/1 Saprolling — you get that steady stream of Saprolings the entire time, so you pay three mana and never worry about needing a Saprolling again.
- **[10:34]** The greener your Etali deck is, the fairer it is; the redder it is, the more turbo-leaning, almost cEDH it is. They all spam an Etali, and that's all you need.
- **[13:29]** If you're going the combo route, Food Chain and Squee is the best way — every time you exile Etali you get eight mana, it goes back to the command zone, and you're hitting other people's creatures too.
- **[16:19]** One treasure equals three mana for Dargo. Prized Statue makes a treasure on entry and another when it leaves — three from the treasure in, three from the treasure out, two from the sacrifice.
- **[19:01]** Birgi is a slam dunk in Dargo — every time you cast a spell you get a red mana, so if you keep sacking Dargo and you've sacked enough for the turn, you can infinitely cast him and get infinite sac triggers like Goblin Bombardment.
- **[21:01]** Roxanne's meteorites deal two damage and tap for mana, and whenever you tap an artifact token for mana you add one more of that type — so treasures tap for double mana, giving you tons of value.
- **[43:41]** Buyback on Sprout Swarm is reduced by Witherbloom, so with four creatures it only costs one green to make a Saprolling and return the spell — infinite creatures for no mana, a two-card combo.
