<objective>
Pin the decide loop so Accept / Reject / Defer never scroll off screen.

**OPTION-CONDITIONAL — execute only under D-1 Option 3.** Under Option 1 (true
wizard) or Option 2 (soft fix), skip this plan entirely and close the phase at
07-06.

Purpose: the decide loop is the tool's core value and is three buttons plus a
short delta list. Even after 07-02..04, reading the full metric breakdown pushes
the buttons out of view on mobile.

Output: `.cutlab-proposal--pinned`, a compact summary line, and the three
decision forms relocated into the pinned header.
</objective>

<environment>
- `position: sticky` interacts with the anchor nav, which is already sticky on mobile (`site-common.css:5071-5117`, `--cutlab-anchor-nav-stuck-height: 4rem`). The pinned proposal must offset below it, not overlap it.
- Reference mockup: `.planning/ui-design/cut-lab/proposed/mock3-pinned.html` and the `.cutlab-proposal--pinned` rules in `mock-shared.css`.
</environment>

<tasks>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Split the proposal into pinned header and scrolling evidence</name>

  Pinned header holds: `Cut {CardName}?`, a one-line glance summary, and the
  three decision buttons. Scrolling body holds: the structural finding, the
  evidence chips, and the full metric breakdown.

  The three decision forms (`RenderDecisionForm`, `CutLab.cshtml:37-62`) move
  wholesale into the header. Each is an independent `<form>` with its own
  antiforgery token and hidden `CutLabStateJson` — keep them as three separate
  forms. Do not consolidate them into one form with a value-carrying submit; the
  AJAX path in `cut-lab.ts` binds per-form via `data-cut-lab-decide-form`.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: The glance line</name>

  Compose from data already on `Model.Proposal`: the changed-family count and the
  one or two largest deltas. Example shape:
  `3 of 7 deck numbers move · ramp 14 → 13 · turn-3 odds −2pt`.

  Build it in `CutLabViewModel`, not in Razor, and cover it in
  `CutLabViewModelWordingTests` — including the `DeltaUnavailableMessage` branch,
  where there are no deltas to summarize and the glance line must degrade to the
  unavailable message rather than render an empty ` · · `.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 3: Sticky offset and overlap</name>

  Set `top` on `.cutlab-proposal--pinned` to clear the mobile sticky anchor nav
  using the existing `--cutlab-anchor-nav-stuck-height` custom property rather
  than a new magic number.

  Verify at 390px with a long card name (e.g. "Sword of Feast and Famine" or a
  DFC display name) that the header wraps to at most two lines and the buttons
  stay on one row or wrap cleanly — not a three-line header eating half the
  viewport.
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 4: Extend the gate</name>

  Add G-5 to `cut-lab-workflow-ux.spec.ts`: after scrolling to the bottom of the
  Decide panel, the Accept button is still within the viewport
  (`boundingBox().y` within `0..innerHeight`). Assert on both viewports.
</task>

<task type="checkpoint">
  <name>Task 5: UAT</name>

  Operator runs a real pool through several accepts and confirms the pinned card
  updates in place, does not double-render, and does not obscure the finding it
  summarizes.
</task>

</tasks>
