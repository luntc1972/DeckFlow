## Summary

Video applies aggro archetype thinking to Commander's 40-life format. Core claim: because 40 life makes lifepay (fetches, shocks, Phyrexian mana, Phyrexian Arena, Dismember) cheap and blocking less necessary, aggro's goal isn't reducing an opponent to zero but keeping opponents scared—host's mantra is getting a threatening player to 15 life ("Aggro Ideology"). Second key feature: commander damage (21 damage from your commander kills), turning a commander deck into a 21-life format. Diagram splits aggro into pure voltron (buff/protect commander for burst kills), pseudo-voltron (commander damage plus other damage, multiple win plans), and disruptive aggro (multi-opponent damage often paired with stax). Deckbuilding advice: get creatures to connect via evasion (innate, equipment, or Wonder) or by removing blockers (example: Malfegor as commander in a budget Rakdos discard deck). Beat boardwipes by being faster and targeting the wipe deck first, permanent-based protection (Selfless Spirit is the white gold standard), instants/counterspells (costs mana efficiency unless treasures/flash), or Blood Artist effects (preferred in black, ties into Aggro Ideology). Balance speed vs resilience (Liesa hatebears cited as slower, stax-heavy, resilient). Develop target prioritization—focus life-payers and players who can shut you down.

- **[06:06]** Run Blood Artist effects—this is my preferred strategy when playing black, as it ties in beautifully with our Aggro Ideology.
