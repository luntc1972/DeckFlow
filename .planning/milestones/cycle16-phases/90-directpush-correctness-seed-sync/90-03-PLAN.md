<objective>
Add the durable "awaiting-confirm" marker (D-10) to `content_site_index`: a nullable column, threaded through the row model and store like `body_sha256`, plus set/clear operations keyed on the composite natural key (never on a timestamp). This is the foundation the DirectPush ordering re-plumb (Plan 90-05) and the DirectPush page resume flow (Plan 90-06) consume so a "content pushed to prod, awaiting deploy-confirm" row is durably distinguishable from a "never pushed" row across a Blazor page reload — making the expand→verify→contract sequence crash-safe.

Purpose: The commit→Render-deploy lag is minutes and Blazor page-local state is lost on navigation. Without a durable marker, a mid-flight push is indistinguishable from an untouched row (and re-diff would reclassify it Unchanged and strand it). This column is that durable marker.
Output: the nullable column (both dialects, idempotent ALTER), the row model property, set/clear store methods, and dialect-guarded tests.
</objective>
