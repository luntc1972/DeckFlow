## Summary

The creator builds a budget (~$60) mono-blue reanimator Commander deck led by Kiora, the Rising Tide, focused on playing permanents from the graveyard rather than instants/sorceries. Core picks include Shoreline Looter (praised as MVP), Gu^^st Grimoire (drawing cards whenever opponents discard, even in non-discard decks), Gisa's/Geier Reach Sanitarium for looting, Containment Construct, Mischievous Catgeist, Nashi, and Overlord of the Floodpits. The deck wants looters and self-mill to enable disturb/escape and reanimation loops; cards are cut if they don't fit the permanent-centric theme (Sword of the Animist, Sol of New Phyrexia, Mass Diminish). A noted weakness is the lack of a strong finisher—blue struggles to close games versus green's overruns; he weighs Dragon Throne of Tarkir and token-copy plans (Mirror Box plus Animate). He stresses the deck must stay active, not just loot. He games on MTGO but constant lag makes it unplayable; he plans to return to Spelltable. He also discusses casual Commander philosophy: dislikes rhystic study and generic staples as boring, runs a quest-sheet system at his LGS rewarding play over winning, and would lower life totals to 30 and decks to 80 cards.

## Key Clips

- I'm building what I like to call monoblue reanimator. The deck is really focused around permanents, so cards like torrential gearhulk are nice but we're not actually that heavy on instants and sorceries. I'm trying to play from the graveyard — that's what it comes down to, I'm trying to be a mono blue reanimator.
- I'm on a journey to find the best card draw in commander. Gisa's grimoire says whenever an opponent discards a card you may draw a card. I'm not playing it in discard decks — I'm playing it in generic decks that have zero way of making my opponents discard, and it's been amazing. Across four games it drew me like 15 cards, because in nearly every match somebody plays something that lets them discard, or an opponent has too many cards in hand and discards at end of turn.
- My current theory is Gisa's grimoire plus Geier Reach Sanitarium, because occasionally you have both and then Geier Reach Sanitarium becomes a two mana tap to draw four and discard one, which is actually kind of insane.
- When you're talking about casual Commander, bringing up cEDH is completely irrelevant since they're basically two different games. I think cEDH is fundamentally a different format from Commander, genuinely just a different game. It's like telling me about competitive Pokemon — I'm sure it's interesting, it's just not relevant.
- If I play against ristic study, I just treat it as a Stax piece. If you put down your Rhystic Study you will never draw a card from it. The only exception is if I can kill it immediately. Though sometimes I won't pay for it because I want my opponent to have a ton of cards in hand so they discard — which triggers my grimoire.
- My number one change to Commander would be to lower life totals from 40 to 30 — I think the game would just be universally better. I also think we don't need 100 card decks; 80 would be fine. 100 is too big because a lot of people struggle to properly shuffle 100 card decks, so the physical pile becomes too large to handle.
- My secret technique to succeeding at Commander is I just play 40 lands. The most powerful thing you can do in Magic is play a land every turn and spend all your mana — build a board and chill. I used to be a 38 person but I'm leaning more towards 40 these days.
- One problem this deck has is that it just can't really finish that well, it takes a long time. I'm not sure what a good monoblue finisher is — how do you actually win? My current plan is kind of mirror box, make a bunch of octopuses. In green you can just overrun people, but blue I find to be a bit more difficult.
