## Summary

Borborygmos Enraged commander deck built as a lands-and-graveyard combo deck rather than creature-heavy. Runs 42 lands (8 Forest, 15 Mountain) to fuel Borborygmos and to support Valakut/Scapeshift as an alternate win. Glacial Chasm reduces all damage to zero to set up combo turns; Strip Mine, Wasteland, Crucible, Azusa, and Oracle of Mul Daya enable land recursion. Heavy artifact mana ramp recommended since spells and the general cost a lot (Gauntlet of Might noted as accidental 100th card; Thread Dynamo cited as an oversight). Key engine cards: Storm Cauldron keeps opponents land-starved while you bounce, throw, or recur your own lands; Creeping Renaissance plus Zuran Orb returns sacrificed lands to hand to throw at opponents via Borborygmos. Equipment like Bludgeon Brawl/grafted exoskeleton turns thrown lands into poison kills. Copy spells (Reverberate, Fork, Increasing Vengeance) and Wildfire/Destructive Force wrath boards. Tutors limited to Gamble; card advantage thin in red-green, helped by Sylvan Library and Horn of Greed. Deck performs well against non-interactive and creature decks but poorly against well-timed counterspells and spot removal due to the general's high mana cost.

## Key Clips

- borborygmos is a 7/6 trampler, and whenever he attacks and deals damage to a player you can mill 3 cards; all those land cards come to your hand, the rest go in your graveyard, and he can also throw lands at players and creatures.
- The way I built this deck I made it to be a graveyard combo deck. I decided not to run a lot of creatures and instead do a lands type of build with a massive lands and spells to ramp me up and eventually lead to a turn where I can kill multiple opponents in one turn.
- This deck does very well against non-interactive decks and decks trying to combo off, but fairly poorly against combo decks with well-placed counterspells, and poorly against creature decks with a lot of targeted spot removal because of his high mana cost.
- There are 42 lands in this deck: eight basic Forest and 15 basic Mountains. You want critical mass of Mountains because you can win with Valakut and Scapeshift, so it's important for the deck to function properly.
- Glacial Chasm says when it comes into play you sacrifice a land and it has a cumulative upkeep of pay life, but it reduces all damage to you to zero. It lets us set up our combo, take no damage from creature decks, and finish off multiple opponents at the same time.
- Grafted Exoskeleton works extremely well with Borborygmos; if you equip him and he isn't destroyed immediately you can throw just four lands and kill each opponent by giving them 12 poison, or three lands with Bladed Bracers attached.
- Storm Cauldron says each opponent can play one additional land each turn, but whenever they tap that land for mana it goes back to their hand. With Borborygmos you can tap all your lands, bring them back to your hand, throw them at opponents or put them in your graveyard and Creeping Renaissance them back over and over.
- Zuran Orb is a combo piece with Creeping Renaissance: you can name lands, and with it on the stack sacrifice all your lands, bring them back to your hand, and then toss them with Borborygmos. Creeping Renaissance can kill two or three opponents in a single turn.
