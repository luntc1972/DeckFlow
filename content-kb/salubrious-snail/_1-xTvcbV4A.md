## Summary

A budget ($25) Boros Commander "eggs" deck built around Gerrard, Weatherlight Hero, whose death triggers a Second Sunrise effect (recurring sacrificed artifacts) — chosen because Commander lacks many Second Sunrise-type cards. The deck plays artifacts, sacrifices "eggs" (cheap artifacts cracked for cards) to loop resources, and most often wins by burning opponents out with Reckless Fireweaver on board. The premium engine/sac outlets — Krark-Clan Ironworks, Ashnod's Altar, Phyrexian Altar — are too expensive for the budget, and Boros (no black/blue) excludes tutors and payoffs like Marionette Master. To compensate, the deck runs three artifact sac outlets (Ravenous Intruder, Slobad Goblin Tinkerer(?), Thermopod as a budget Phyrexian Altar) plus Martyr's Cause, and eight board wipes that hit artifacts and creatures — functioning as a single-use, all-permanent sac outlet and slowing aggressive opponents, since the deck lacks KCI's explosive mana and needs more time. Against an aggressive pool it added blockers/grind: Pilgrim's Eye(?), Wall of Omens, Reveillark, Vesperlark, Sun Titan, giving a non-storm backup plan. Stated principle: a deck's complexity comes from how hard its tasks are and how much it must navigate opponents; budget constraints, by removing easy answers, make this "midrange eggs" harder and more layered than a KCI version.
