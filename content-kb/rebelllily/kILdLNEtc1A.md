## Summary

This video presents "Copium Green," a four-color Timna/Thassa's Oracle (TNT) cEDH brew in Bant-plus-black colors, framed self-deprecatingly as a mediocre deck. The builder argues TNT underperforms despite card-advantage engines: Thassa (an infinite-mana outlet and slow Rofellos-style payoff) and Timna (combat-based card draw) only convert to wins when paired with efficient combos and fast mana. Without Mana Crypt, hitting Thassa's four-mana activation is hard, so the deck runs mana dorks (despite Orcish Bowmasters) and extra lands (~30) for consistency. Combo lines include Thassa's Oracle + Tainted Pact/Consultation, Kinnan + Basalt Monolith, Devoted Druid with Swift Reconfiguration/Hazel/Magus, Faeburrow Elder/Bloom Tender + Freed from the Real, and Eternal Witness + Gaea's Cradle + Snap. Card choices favor flexible beaters that draw with Timna (Shredder, Sevinne's, Faeburrow, Mockingbird, endurance) and versatile mana (Kydele over Grand Abolisher due to color requirements). The builder reluctantly includes Training Grounds/Biomancer's Familiar betting on a slower post-ban meta. Three goldfish hands demonstrate the deck spinning its wheels toward Thassa's Oracle consultation wins, confirming it is suboptimal but functional.

## Key Clips

- Today we're gonna go through my Brew of Timna Thasos. This is a list called copium Green, a reference to medium green, one of the more popular and competitive Timna Thasos decks way back in the day when flash was legal. This is me trying to build a TNT deck in today's new meta.
- Timna is appealing because you have two sides of advantage engines: Thasos can tap mana and activate and draw cards, and Timna plays where if you get in combat you can pay life and draw cards. But somehow even though you have all these advantage engines, TNT never does well. The idea that a slower meta means more time to do advantage is not a real thing — more time doesn't mean more wins.
- The combos we're playing include Thassa's Oracle / Tainted Pact / consultation, Kinnan Basalt classic infinite mana combo, and Devoted Druid. Hazel works even better in TNT — with Survival you tutor for Druid, drop it for Hazel, Exile Devoted Druid, and your land or food now makes infinite green. Also Faeburn Elder / Freed from the Real and Bloom Tender single-card combos.
- I would never play Biomancer's Familiar or Training Grounds pre-ban — spending two mana on a card to reduce a Thasos activation you only do once or twice a game is a bad use of a card slot. The fantasy that you draw three cards every turn cycle rarely happens. But I underestimated how impactful losing Mana Crypt was — without it we can't reliably hit the magic number four to activate Thasos, so I put training grounds back in playing into the slower meta.
- Grand Abolisher is a format staple but I play Kydele instead because of mana. TNT's mana is awful — our costs are everywhere. We have to find green turn one, then white afterwards, and the colorless at the end doesn't matter. Kydele is inefficient compared to Grand Abolisher but easier to cast because the color cost is more versatile, and it attacks better for Timna.
- Everyone says dorks are unplayable in a Bowmasters meta — you play the dork, they Bowmaster it, now you're down a card. But when I goldfish this deck, the only way to come close to Rograkh or rocks-high decks is going turn one dork, turn two pay life play land play a three drop. So you have to take the L and play cope dorks to maximize our strong starts in a mulligan-heavy format.
- This deck needs more lands — a lot of my mulligans just did not have enough land sources to play the game. This deck is really mana hungry. With no Mana Crypt or Dark Ritual, you can no longer go land, Imperial Seal, Mana Crypt and save your hand, so I think you need more lands in general across all decks now.
- I tried to port the much superior Rograkh Thrasios deck into white and black and trim the red cards, keeping the flexible variety of combo cards that layer together. But in my initial goldfishing the hands are just awful — you get a bunch of pieces except this time you don't have the speed or quantity of mana to overcome the issues. If you like jamming mediocre four-color piles that spin their wheels, all the more power to you.
