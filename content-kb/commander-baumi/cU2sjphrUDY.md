## Summary

This is a Magic Jeopardy trivia game with EDH content creators (Salubrious Snail, Panzer MTG, Unpopular MTG) answering questions and surveying audience opinions on deckbuilding. Audience polls reveal community norms: an average deck should run about three board wipes (top answer of 138 votes, with two to four being common), and roughly 38 lands is the most popular count (host recommends 40 and includes MDFCs in counts; Panzer admits running only 34-35, considered too low except for fast or competitive decks where aggressive mulligans compensate).
