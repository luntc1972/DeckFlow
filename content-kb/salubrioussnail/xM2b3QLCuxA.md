## Summary

This video reviews the Duskmourn preconstructed Commander deck "Endless Punishment," a Rakdos group slug deck led by Valgavoth, Harrower of SoulsΓÇöa 4-mana 4/4 flier with Ward-Pay 2 life that gains a +1/+1 counter and draws a card whenever an opponent first loses life on their turn. The reviewer praises how the deck leverages this commander: 11 cards reliably trigger life loss on opposing turns, 15 more sometimes do (including six Blood Artist effects), and 10 cause sorcery-speed life loss, closing games through incremental chip damage. He concludes it's an impressive deck that positions itself as a clear table enemy.
