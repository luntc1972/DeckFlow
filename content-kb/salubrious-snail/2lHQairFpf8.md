## Summary

The video argues against treating ramp spells—especially Cultivate—as universal EDH staples, and gives a framework for picking ramp that fits your deck. Cultivate (and Kodama's Reach) is a Rampant Growth plus Lay of the Land; it suits three-plus-color decks with rough manabases and pivotal 5–6 mana turns, but is overplayed (60% of Toski and Tovolar decks on EDHRec). It's questionable in low-color or mono-color commanders like Toski (which draws cards and wants creature volume, not 5–6 drops) and Tovolar; Rampant Growth or a two-drop card-drawing beater is often better. Key selection considerations: (1) mana curve—match ramp costs to the mana value you're pushing toward (his Glissa deck spikes at 1 and 4); (2) card type—dorks/rocks die to removal/wipes but land-fetch resists land destruction; single green dorks are most mana-efficient at low budget; (3) net vs upfront cost—Skyshroud Claim costs 4 but nets 2 with untapped Forests; he prefers Harrow and Grow from the Ashes over Cultivate in two-color decks for untapped lands. Amount of ramp is best tuned by playtesting. Strong cards (Elvish Mystic, Nature's Lore, Thought Vessel) may still not fit. Scrutinize lands, removal, and ramp rather than glossing over them.

## Key Clips

- **[03:45]** As a general principle Cultivate is best geared toward decks looking for a pivotal 5 or 6 mana turn. This brings me to a major consideration when picking out ramp—mana curve.
- **[04:27]** When you're adding a ramp spell to a deck, you should be asking 'what is this ramping toward?' If the most important mana value you're pushing toward is four, you shouldn't run a bunch of three mana ramp spells.
- **[05:04]** In a mono-colored deck like Toski, Cultivate doesn't ramp into the commander and gives you color-fixing upside you don't need; Rampant Growth is almost certainly better, or even a two-drop beater that draws cards.
- **[05:39]** Card type matters: artifacts and especially creatures die to removal and board wipes, but land destruction is much less common—though dorks and rocks are often more efficiently costed than land-grab spells.
- **[06:04]** Single green dorks tend to be the most mana-efficient ramp option at lower budgets, so I go for them in any deck where the 3 CMC slot is important but the 2 CMC slot is unimportant.
- **[07:04]** Consider net mana cost vs upfront cost: Skyshroud Claim costs 4 upfront but nets 2 if you grab Forests that enter untapped. In two-color decks I prefer Harrow and Grow from the Ashes to Cultivate because they grab untapped lands.
- **[08:01]** The best way to tell the right amount of ramp is to play the deck and see. If you're ramping to a value, does it hit that consistently, or does it eat deck space and leave you with awkward spare mana?
- **[08:41]** There may be strong ramp spells—Elvish Mystic, Nature's Lore, Thought Vessel—that just aren't what your deck wants, and that's okay. The best ramp solution isn't one size fits all.
