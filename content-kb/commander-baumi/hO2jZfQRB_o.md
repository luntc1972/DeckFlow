## Key Clips

- Increasing Vengeance is a two mana instant that copies an instant or sorcery we control. What makes it special is that it has flashback for five mana, and if it's flashback, then it copies its target spell twice. It's very powerful in spell slinger decks and can act as the silver bullet that ends the match.
- Copy Insult to Injury three times with Increasing Vengeance, which doubles all damage we deal until end of turn, and watch our Firebrand Archer ping everybody for 16 damage a pop.
- Fecund Green Shell is a five mana 4/6 with reach. When it or another creature with a larger butt than face enters, we look at the top card of our deck; if it's a land we may put it onto the battlefield, otherwise it goes to hand. It also gives all our creatures plus two plus two so long as we control 10 or more lands. Cloning it is strongest because every new copy sees itself and is seen by all the others.
- With Springheart Nantuko and an untapper like Spelunking in play, the Green Shell can lead to a soft infinite that digs through the entire deck, putting all lands onto the board and everything else into hand, all the while creating 600 turtles.
- Starlight Spectacular is a four mana enchantment that at the beginning of combat lets us create a conga line of creatures; each gets plus one plus one for each other creature chosen before it. A fantastic finisher for go wide token decks, and way less annoying than Cathars' Crusade because it checks once per turn instead of incrementing individual counters.
- Aminatou's Augury is an eight mana sorcery that exiles the top eight cards, puts a land onto the battlefield, and for each non-land card type lets us cast a spell from the remaining cards without paying its mana cost until end of turn. Because it's until end of turn, we get complete control over timings and order ΓÇö cast a board wipe then immediately follow with a new threat.
- Scholar of the Lost Grove is a seven mana 5/5 that lets us cast an instant, sorcery, or artifact out of our graveyard when it enters, giving us another Augury ΓÇö the favorite pairing with Aminatou's Augury.
- Eldritch Pact is a seven mana black sorcery that has target player draw as many cards and lose as much life as they have cards in their graveyard. In late game commander, players often have 15 to 20 cards in the graveyard and hover around that health range, so it more often than not reads kill target player ΓÇö especially if you mill them a bit first.
