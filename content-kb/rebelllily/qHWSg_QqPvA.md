## Summary

This video is a comedic card-design exercise, not a strategy guide. The creators react to Wizards teasing Carmela ("Chamelia"), a new three-mana Golgari squirrel warlock legend for the Bloom set, and each pitches their own version of the card. Designs proposed include: a 1/3 that taps green and black to exile a graveyard card, making a Food token if it was a creature or draining two life if it was an instant/sorcery; a 2/2 magecraft spellslinger that creates Food on instant/sorcery casts and drains life per Food made that turn (compared to Witherbloom Apprentice and called an infinite combo); a 2/3 that creates Food when an opponent's creature dies, then sacrifices Food to reanimate creatures with mana value up to the Food sacrificed; and joke designs like a 10/4 with Menace, or a card that can only attack opponents who control Food, gains cumulative upkeep, and gives opponents "nuts." Recurring theme: Carmela is a "seed miser," so acorns equal Food tokens, tying her to Food and graveyard/sacrifice mechanics. The hosts invite viewers to comment which design they'd build around and plug their Modern Horizons 3 Commander precon gameplay and an Assassin's Creed deck tech.

## Key Clips

- Wizards has teased a new legendary squirrel warlock for Bloom and today we're going to try and design our version of what we think Chamelia does.
- If I got to design this legend I would give them: tap a green and a black and exile target card from a graveyard; if it was a creature card create a food token; if it was an instant or sorcery each opponent loses two life. A 1/3 golgari death shaman.
- Give Chamelia magecraft and when you cast an instant or sorcery you create a food token, then each opponent loses life for each food token you've created this turn. (Critiqued as basically Witherbloom Apprentice / an infinite combo from the command zone.) Revised to: whenever you gain life from a food token each opponent loses that much life; a 2/2.
- I would design Chamelia with: whenever a creature an opponent controls dies create a food token; then sacrifice X food tokens, put target creature card with mana value less than or equal to the number of food tokens you sacrificed from a graveyard onto the battlefield under your control. A 2/3 — opponents kill each other's creatures while you reanimate them.
- First I'm going to make them a 10/4 with menace — critiqued as way too powerful for three mana, then scratched.
- Chamelia can't attack opponents that don't have an acorn (food token). To fix never being able to attack, give Chamelia cumulative upkeep: give your opponents a nut — hand out food tokens so she can attack, then take creatures back.
