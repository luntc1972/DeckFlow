## Summary

A creative deck-building challenge to make an original Atraxa, Praetors' Voice deck while still using the unoriginal commander. After rejecting an Alela/Capenna build and a Myojin hard-control shell as too boring, the builder settles on spreading Atraxa's keywords (flying, lifelink, deathtouch, vigilance) across a wider army using cards like Odric, Concerted Effort, and Bleeding Edge. Goldvein Hydra, dorks, Slippery Bogbonder, and Sabra Cryptomancer add trample, haste, and hexproof, while Arwen and Atraxa's Toolkit supply indestructible/shield counters that Atraxa proliferates and shares. For the actual army it goes Eldrazi spawn/Scion tokens via Basking Broodscale, Titan's myriad copies, upkeep makers (Awakening Zone, From Beyond), and ramp spells. Since spawns are small, +1/+1 counters grow them: Propagator Drone grants evolve, Shalai, Master Biomancer, Vivien's Enigma, and Brokers Ascendancy add counters, and Angelic Aberration forces evolution. The build rounds out with five lightweight counterspells saved for board wipes, flexible removal (Atomize, Beast Within, MDFC slots), and draw from Distant Melody, Kindred Discovery, and Primal Empathy. The result: a four-color tokens/+1/+1-counter/Eldrazi deck unified by keyword-spreading—an original take on a boring commander.

## Key Clips

- Atraxa, Praetors' Voice has been one of the most popular, dreaded, boring commanders you can build because she's simply so good. For four mana you get a 4/4 with a million keywords and one of the best triggers we've seen on a commander. The challenge: build a creative take on the most unoriginal commander, with the only rule being you still have to use Atraxa in your deck.
- The settled-on theme is Atraxa's keywords. She comes with four fantastic keywords — flying, lifelink, deathtouch, and vigilance — and when paired with something like Odric or Concerted Effort, you can turn an army of small creatures into mini-Atraxas. Bleeding Effect is an enchantment that can grant the army keywords from creature cards in the graveyard.
- More keyword-spreaders: Goldvein Hydra gives creatures trample and haste plus is a good proliferate target with +1/+1 counters that translate to more treasure tokens. Dorks like Beastcaller Savant grant haste; Sylvan Caryatid and Paradise Druid share hexproof while fixing mana. Slippery Bogbonder and Sabra Cryptomancer are instant-speed ways to protect Atraxa and spread hexproof.
- Arwen, Mortal Queen enters with an indestructible counter that Atraxa can proliferate and share across the army. Atraxa's Agent's Toolkit comes with +1/+1, flying, deathtouch, and a shield counter, all of which can be proliferated with Atraxa and spread across other utility creatures as you play them.
- Rather than go on-theme with Phyrexians, the deck pairs Atraxa with Eldrazi. Spawn and Scion generators include Basking Broodscale, Titering Dispatcher (Myriad copies leaving spawns), upkeep token makers like Awakening Zone and From Beyond, plus cast-trigger makers. Spells like Growth Spasm, Malevolent Rumble, and Path of Annihilation ramp and find lands while making spawns.
- Deathtouch and lifelink don't do much on 0/1 spawns, so +1/+1 counters are key to grow the army. Propagator Drone generates spawns and grants evolve so tokens get that first +1/+1 counter for Atraxa to proliferate. Shalai gives hexproof and can be a mana sink. Master Biomancer lets spawns enter pre-buffed. Verdurous Embrace and Broker's Ascendancy grow Scions — stack Broker's to resolve before Atraxa's proliferate.
- The rest of the deck: a half-package of five counterspells with light color requirements saved for board wipes, a half-package of removal with two MDFCs taking land slots plus Atomize and Beast Within as flexible removal, and three draw spots — Distant Melody, Kindred Discovery for mass draw, and Primal Empathy as a flexible counters-or-draw card.
- Altogether this Atraxa deck takes tokens, +1/+1 counters, and Eldrazi and smooshes them together with the central concept of spreading keywords to create an unstoppable army — a very original deck from very unoriginal themes.
