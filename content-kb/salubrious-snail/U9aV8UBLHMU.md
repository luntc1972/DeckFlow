## Summary

Video argues Commander lacks distinct archetypes like 60-card formats; combo is better seen as a finisher decks use, and reanimator in EDH means general graveyard decks that reanimate here and there rather than turn-2 Entomb-plus-reanimation-spell decks. Pure Griselbrand or Archon of Cruelty style reanimator is called a poor EDH plan because snowballing an early threat gets you pummeled by the table, unless the threat is oppressive enough to stop opponents playing, like Jin-Gitaxias or Void Winnower. Sevinne's Reclamation is called a utility spell for graveyard decks, not a reanimator card. Control in EDH is mostly counterspells and board wipes (Baral, Chief of Compliance-style decks cited as Baral, Child of Alara (?)). The speaker's preferred control is a multi-card engine that repeats value or removal effects via flicker or graveyard recursion, choosing which opposing threats to leave alone, though board-wipe piles are equally valid and strong in four-player games. Aggro in Commander is generally midrange that is stronger early; pings, commander damage, or infect dodge one of the three-opponent or 40-life problems, never both. Almost all decks want card draw, ramp, and removal. The video says decks now have 'themes' and things they do, not things they are.
