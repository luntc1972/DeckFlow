## Summary

This video covers upgrading the Magic: The Gathering energy precon led by Saheeli, a four-color (Jeskai+green) commander that grants energy when you cast artificer or artifact spells and lets you pay three energy at combat to copy a permanent you control. The builder goldfishes the deck first, finding it plays well when you have both a consistent energy source and consistent card draw, but stumbles when one is missing or when the manabase produces too many tapped check-lands. Key synergies: Saheeli copying value creatures like Solemn Simulacrum, Wary Refugee/Rogue Refiner, and gearhulks; Stride Hanger Automaton and thopter-makers stacking power; Panharmonicon doubling ETBs. Upgrade approach: steal all energy and artifact payoff cards from the other precon and the kaladesh/energy pool, cut weak high-cost cards, add gearhulks, Aetherworks Marvel, Aether Revolt, World Walker Helm, doubling effects, more card draw (Thought Monitor, Skyscanner), counterspells favoring single-blue multicolor options, and removal. He avoids double-blue costs since the manabase mulligans for the triple-color commander. Final result: a roughly $25 (or $50 with Doubling Season) upgrade that keeps the deck's original copy-and-energy engine feel while smoothing consistency.
