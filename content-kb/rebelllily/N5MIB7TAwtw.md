## Key Clips

- To design a good ramp package, we want to pick a target and deliver on that target. First, the most important concept of ramp is time: ramping is accelerating you in time and giving you access to cards ahead of schedule.
- A fundamental turn, as defined by SV Mowitz in the early 2000s, refers to when a deck wins the game or becomes unfair. Unique to Commander, there are four fundamental turns at the table, creating a greater meta fundamental turn ΓÇö if every deck is playing zero rocks and rituals, you must also meet that pace. Build your ramp to hit your deck's fundamental turn and not fall behind the table's pace.
- Easy rule for what to deliver with: focus on ramp cards with mana value two less than the mana value of your target. Three-mana commanders want one-mana ramp (Llanowar Elves, Utopia Sprawl). Four-mana commanders want two-mana ramp (Arcane Signet, guild signets, talismans, Rampant Growth, Nature's Lore). Five-plus-mana commanders want a mix of two- and three-mana ramp (Worn Powerstone, Relic of Legends) and can consider rituals.
- Mana dorks shine for casting three-mana commanders on turn two, but their weakness is they can't tap for mana immediately and are exposed to mass creature removal and board wipes. Having a third to half of your ramp suite be dorks maximizes your chances of casting three-mana spells on turn two.
- In 2022 on dexstats.net, a user called Aetherium Slinky calculated the best land-to-ramp combinations for drawing a keepable hand (defined as two lands + one or two ramp spells, three lands, or three lands + one ramp spell), considering the London mulligan with three mulligans max. The sweet spot is 12 ramp spells and 36 lands for an 89% chance of success.
- The hot garbage model by EDH Power Level: ramp is awful when you don't draw a land and all you did was spend mana to stay on pace. At 36 lands, one-mana ramp is hot garbage 22% of the time, two-mana ramp 38% of the time, and three-mana ramp 54% of the time. This means you really can't cut lands, and two-mana ramp at 36 lands is less efficient than one-mana ramp.
- Sample 12-slot package: Sol Ring takes the first spot. Best one-mana dorks are Birds of Paradise, Delighted Halfling, Deathrite Shaman, Noble Hierarch, and Ignoble Hierarch. Optimal two-mana colorless rocks include Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Thought Vessel, and Mind Stone; in green add Rampant Growth, Nature's Lore, and Farseek for fixing and deck thinning. Spend the last three slots on three-mana rocks like Worn Powerstone.
- For expensive ramp spells you don't need high frequency since you have a few turns to draw them. With three slots of big-mana ramp, the chance of drawing it within 10 cards is 27%; playing seven gives a 50% chance within 10 cards. You can also fill flex slots with rituals, or adopt a cEDH model with around six ritual cards alongside your ramp package.
