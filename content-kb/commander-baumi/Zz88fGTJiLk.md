## Summary

Kiora, the Rising Tide is a free two-mana mono-blue commander with two abilities: on entry, draw two and discard two; on attack, if seven-plus cards are in the graveyard, create a legendary 8/8 Sai of the Deep octopus token (limited to one at a time). The builder treats Kiora as thematic guidance for a graveyard-centric deck that plays nothing like normal mono-blue, closer to a Golgari self-mill reanimator shell. Core engine: constant looting (Lord Broker, Overlord of the Flood Pits, Smuggler's Copter, Kiora herself) fills the graveyard while sculpting the hand. Blue lacks true reanimation, so the deck uses recast-from-graveyard effects (Skoph of the Lost Trove, Torrential Gearhulk, flashback/encore, Reenact the Crime) plus clones (self-replication, Cackling Counterpart) and Mirror Box to break the legend rule on tokens. Wincons are giant sea monsters (Cataract Leviathan, Scourge of Fleets) and one-sided bounce sweepers, swinging for 20-30 at once. Ramp is land-based (no Mana rocks), using flip-lands like Mundle the Great Door, Dowsing Dagger, and Aminatou's Augury. Forty lands; loot excess away. Graveyard hate is survivable because nonstop looting keeps options open.

## Key Clips

- Kiora the Rising Tide is a 2-mana 2/2 with two abilities: when she enters the battlefield we draw two cards and then discard two cards, and when she attacks, if we have seven or more cards in our graveyard she creates a Sands of the Deep, a legendary 8/8 octopus creature token. Important to note this token is legendary, meaning we can only have one at a time.
- Mono blue has no real reanimation, but it has cards that resemble it: Skolar of the Lost Trove is a seven-mana 5/5 with flying that when it enters lets us cast an instant, sorcery, or artifact card from our graveyard without paying its mana cost. Torrential Gearhulk does the same except not for artifacts. If we accept that recasting counts as reanimation, then flashback, encore, and similar mechanics also count, and we can discard those cards to Kiora without it feeling like a real discard.
- Large sea monsters are the main win condition. Cataract Leviathan is an 8-mana 5/5 that bounces all nonland permanents when it enters and has unearth, fitting perfectly as a giant monster that can also be played from the graveyard. With it in the deck we don't want mana rocks, which die incidentally, so we focus on land-based ramp.
- Mundle the Great Door is a three-mana artifact that taps to loot for four mana, and can transform into a land that taps for mana equal to the number of permanent cards in our graveyard. To flip it we need four or more different permanent types in the graveyard, so we spread out our card-type selection and minimize sorceries and instants.
- We run a significant looting package: Loot the Core, LW Broker, Malcolm, Shoreline Looter, Smuggler's Copter, Moon Circuit Hacker, and Overlord of the Flood Pits. Looting works better than self-mill because it lets us make precise selections and sculpt our hand while filling the graveyard. Discarding cards we can recast from the graveyard means the loot is effectively a draw with upside.
- Only three counterspells, since the deck is permanent-focused. Circular Logic is a three-mana counter that makes opponents pay one extra mana per card in our graveyard, essentially a hard counter, and has Madness letting us cast it for a single blue mana if we discard. Looting into Madness means the loot is essentially a free draw with upside.
- Reenact the Crime is a four-mana instant that exiles a nonland card from a graveyard put there this turn, copies it, and casts the copy for free. We can loot away an Artisan of Kozilek with Shoreline Looter, then Reenact the Crime to cast it and reanimate something else. It's not limited to our own graveyard so it can steal opponents' pieces; one of the strongest cards in the deck.
- Mirror Box is a three-mana artifact saying the legend rule doesn't apply to our permanents, letting us attack with multiple Kioras to make multiple Sands, or clone Kiora for multiple per turn. The deck plays like a golgari reanimation shell rather than blue; graveyard hate is a problem but the deck functions fine without the graveyard thanks to non-stop looting. It sits at about $58 on Moxfield.
