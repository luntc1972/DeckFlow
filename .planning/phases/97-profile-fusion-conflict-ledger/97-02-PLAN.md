<objective>
Build the two pure-Core lookup functions the fusion join depends on: `StatedMetricKeyMapper`
(translate a stated `StatedRuleCandidate.Metric` to the corresponding P95 `MeasuredMetric.Metric`
key) and `MetricClassification` (partition each metric key observable vs philosophy/stated-only).

Purpose: CONTEXT D-08 says "join on those exact keys" but the research (Pitfall 1) proved only 4
of 20 stated keys match a measured key verbatim — the 11 category keys are a PREFIX relationship
(`ramp` -> `category_ratio:ramp`), and 5 keys have no measured counterpart. This mapper IS the
concrete, testable artifact CS-16a's "highest-risk modeling decision" is asking for. Without it a
naive string-equals join silently drops the flagship ramp/removal/draw/wipes/counters rows.

Output: Two static pure-Core classes + exhaustive 20-key unit tests. No FusedTarget dependency
(operates on strings), so this plan runs in parallel with Plan 01 in Wave 1.
