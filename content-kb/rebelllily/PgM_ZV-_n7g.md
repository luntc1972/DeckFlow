## Summary

Argument: "mid-rangeify" a commander deck instead of building an all-in "protect the commander" storm list, because commander-centric decks are fragile and unfun. Example deck is a Vivi (?) spellslinger deck that ramps mana off cheap spells to cast Dragonstorm (fetching Terror of the Peaks (?), Twinflame (?) dragon, and a copy effect (Deceptive/Frostkite (?)) to copy Terror of the Peaks for damage). Mid-range = cards that flex roles and play double duty. Cited flexible cards: Blood Tithe Harvester (beater + removal + leaves an artifact), Graveyard Trespasser (attacker, graveyard hate, lifegain, ward value), plus Immerith Desert Doom (?) and Sword Coast Serpent (?). Method: sort deck into ramp/draw/removal/synergy/payoff buckets and convert about half of each into flexible permanents. His changes: cut Ponder, Faithless Looting, Preordain, Artist's Talent (?), and Frantic Search; added Kindred Discovery, Frost Cliff Siege (?), and Iworth (?). Cut temporary mana Rite of Flame and Mana Vault (pseudo-temporary); added Ojer's Incubator (?), Carnelian Orb of Dragonkind, and Galvanic/Galazeth Prismari (?). Also slightly raised land count to scale mana over time rather than rely on burst turns. Add a pivot that synergizes with your win condition (here: more dragons).
