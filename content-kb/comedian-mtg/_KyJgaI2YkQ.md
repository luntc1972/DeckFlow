## Summary

Host updates a cEDH Winota, Joiner of Forces "Snowball Stax" primer using a process he calls "skeleton shaping": strip the deck to its core, then rebuild to 100 with supplemental pieces. Cards locked as core include Ornithopter, Esper Sentinel, Signal Pest, Archivist of Oghma, Grand Abolisher, Voice of Victory, Loyal Apprentice, Magus of the Moon, Archon of Emeria, Drannith Magistrate, Combat Celebrant, Rionya, Slicer, Solitude, Blade Historian, and Rick, Steadfast Leader. He prizes non-humans (Tatara Taru, Archivist) to flip off—not into—and values high-toughness bodies. Stated principle: Stax is "control with permanence"; limiting spells-per-turn blanks opponents' interaction, and Winota flips resolve with no response window (great for Magus). Adds: Clarion Conqueror, Avalanche(?), Auratouched Mage + Breath of Fury, Raff and Leo(?), Ocelot Pride, both Thalias, Spirit of the Labyrinth, Anax and Cymede over Legion Warboss, Skyclave Apparition, Enlightened Tutor, Gingerbrute, Delina. Cuts/sideboards Charismatic Conqueror, Alexios, Vexing Bauble, Talismans, Kinsbaile Cavalier. Rejects Phyrexian Censor (shuts off own tokens/haste) and Gogo (worse than Roaming Throne). Keeps 31–32 lands—only six acceleration pieces. Ends ~24 humans of 51 creatures. Worst matchups: Kinnan, Sisay, cradle decks; Rog-Ishai folds to stax.
