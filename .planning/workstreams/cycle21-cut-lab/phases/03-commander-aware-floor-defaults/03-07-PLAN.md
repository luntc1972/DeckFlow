<objective>
Reconcile `CutLabCutRoundEngine.LockedOvershootRoleOrder` with commander-aware floors: the locked-overshoot
advisory sorts primarily by headroom — in-pool count minus effective floor, descending — and keeps the
existing least-to-most-structural array as the deterministic tiebreak.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 success criterion 5. The array puts **wincons first** on a least-structural theory,
but wincons carries the smallest floor in the table (band 2-3, commander p25 median 2) and therefore usually
the *least* slack — so the advisory currently points at the role most likely to break its own floor on the
next cut. `max()` sharpens the contradiction because floors only ever rise. The roadmap explicitly calls a
hardcoded order that contradicts commander-aware floors on the same page a defect, and D-13 chose to
reconcile rather than to write a rationale for it.
Output: a headroom-ranked advisory that degrades to today's exact behavior when no floor data is supplied.

**This plan is independent of the rest of Phase 3.** It threads data that already exists at the call site;
it does not depend on the commander snapshot, the provider, or the `max()` resolver. It runs in wave 1
alongside plan 03-01.

**Requirement note:** D-13 implements ROADMAP success criterion 5, which has no dedicated RFLR identifier.
RFLR-08 is listed in `requirements` because criterion 5 exists precisely so the same page does not contradict
RFLR-08's commander-aware floors.

**Scope fence — do NOT add any field to `CutLabUiPatchDto` or `CutLabDecideApiResponse`, and do not change
`CutLabUiPatchBuilder`.** The advisory already flows through `CutLabDecideApiResponse.Patch.LockedOvershootAdvisory`;
reordering its groups needs no DTO change. Task 3 proves that rather than assuming it.
</objective>
