## Summary

This video highlights four cheap, underplayed Commander cards and the roles they fill. Insidious Fungus is a one-mana green creature that sacrifices for two mana to either destroy an artifact/enchantment or draw a card and ramp a land in tapped, making it flexible removal that never becomes a dead draw; it can also enable an infinite with six. Malcolm, Alluring Scoundrel is a two-mana blue flyer with flash that loots on combat damage and, after four chorus counters, casts a discarded card for free—a strong looter that doubles as a wincon and baits removal. Rose, Cutthroat Raider is a four-mana red first striker whose raid creates junk tokens (impulse-draw artifacts that also make red mana when sacrificed), giving aggressive decks essential refueling since she only needs to attack, not connect. Live or Die is a five-mana black instant choosing between destroying a creature or instant-speed reanimating one from your own graveyard with no extra costs or sacrifice clauses—usable as a haste reanimator, combat trick, or emergency removal. The presenter favors removal that is flexible or broad, encourages players to attack more aggressively, and stresses that aggressive decks need heavy card advantage to survive multiple board wipes.

## Key Clips

- Insidious fungus is a green one mana 1/2 fungus. For two mana, we can sacrifice it to either destroy an artifact or an enchantment, or we can draw a card and then pull a land from our hand into play tapped.
- Insidious fungus crucially has the additional option of being sacrificed as a growth spiral. Turn one fungus into turn two sack to ramp is a totally fine play, especially since it can be done at instant speed. We can use it as removal, or if that doesn't work out we can just cycle it and look for something better, ramping a bit along the way. It's usually just an auto-include in green decks for me. It can also be used to go infinite with six.
- Malcolm Alluring Scoundrel is a blue two mana 2/1 Siren Pirate with Flash and Flying. Whenever he deals combat damage to one of our opponents, we draw a card and then discard a card. Flash on a creature like this is a huge deal in blue because it allows us to keep up interaction without taking the risk of stalling our board development.
- Whenever we loot with Malcolm, he gets a chorus counter. Once he has four of them, we get to cast the card we have discarded without paying its mana cost. He's just small and evasive enough that if you get to cast him during the early stages of the game, he's pretty much guaranteed to actually get four attacks in. From that point onwards, he's not just a looter, he's a wincon.
- Rose Cutthroat Raider is a red four mana 3/2 robot with first strike. She has a raid ability: at the end of combat on our turn, we create a junk token for each opponent we attacked, up to a total of three tokens. Junk tokens are artifacts that we can sacrifice to impulse draw the top card of our deck. Whenever we sacrifice a junk, Rose gives us a red mana. She doesn't need us to deal combat damage, only cares about us attacking at all, so she plays much better into opponents that have chump blockers.
- Aggressive creature decks need lots of resources because they'll be soaking up most of the removal available. We won't just need to play through one board wipe, but multiple, so it's essential we have the cards available to rebuild. That's where Rose comes in. While four mana is a bit expensive, she quickly refunds that cost as every junk we crack is in essence also a treasure. And because she doesn't need to attack herself, we don't have to take any risks with our card draw engine.
- Live or Die is a five mana black instant that lets us choose one of two: reanimate a creature from our graveyard or destroy target creature. It's an instant speed reanimate with the only condition being that the creature has to come from our graveyard. It doesn't ask for any additional costs and doesn't have timing restrictions or end of turn sacrifice clauses. This basically lets us reanimate a creature but also give it haste since we can cast the spell at the end step before our turn.
- Live or Die is removal in more than one way because we can reanimate a creature and then immediately use it to block. It's genuinely the cheapest black instant with this kind of effect, and it has the optional mode of just killing a thing. Sometimes we happily pay five mana to get rid of the biggest problem. If you're in a deck that has ways to throw big creatures into the graveyard, Live or Die is a fantastic inclusion as a reanimation spell, combat trick, and emergency removal option.
