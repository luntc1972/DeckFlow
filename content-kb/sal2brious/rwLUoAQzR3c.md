## Summary

Two creators discuss card categories for Commander decks. Core warning: don't over-categorize—giving weak cards a "nice hat" (a Skullbriar deck with a 4-card self-mill category) makes useless cards look useful. They reject "plan cards": every card should serve the game plan. Don't categorize the obvious (a flicker deck has flicker); instead track how flicker becomes removal or card draw. Building a birds deck around Choco (?), they cut birds that only fit the theme (vanilla flyers, lifegain, self-mill) and keep birds that draw, ramp, or interact. One creator prefers few large categories (a single "card advantage" bucket, ~30 pieces, generous—scrying repeatedly is fine); the other tracks small setup/payoff subcategories to spot gaps, especially parasitic packages like cast-from-exile payoffs, which need paired enablers. Impulse-draw support has grown (a free retrace sorcery from Outlaws of Thunder Junction Commander). Other notes: Valduk struggles as a defensive Voltron commander (too few shield cards); Malfegor discard runs a graveyard package; Bane of Progress punishes mana rocks; a playgroup soft-bans Sol Ring by treating it as a second Command Tower. Deckbuilding goal: 40 lands and heavy card draw so you can always participate.
