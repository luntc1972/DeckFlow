## Summary

This stream is a patron-submission segment where the host reviews sets of "nine cards that made me" and grades each, inferring the submitter's player type. Before that, he brews around a modern blue Commander that lets you cast a lesser-mana-value spell sharing a card type with your first instant/sorcery each turn (or make a 2/1 monkey pirate with haste). He likes pairing it with Dig Through Time and Treasure Cruise (cheap to cast, high mana value enables a free spell), spell-copy/twincast effects, and free-spell tricks like Unwind, Misdirection, and Turnabout; he notes spree cards work off free-cast since you still pick and pay one additional mode cost, and that it anti-synergizes with Three Steps Ahead. He resists full polymorph, preferring just Reality Scramble plus a few utility creatures to avoid samey Blightsteel games. Card opinions: Paradox Engine is cEDH-strong and he argues it could be unbanned in Commander (banned only due to bad Thrasios durdle decks, not its own power); price should never justify a ban. Other praise: Turnabout is underrated, Spymaster's Vault belongs in every black deck, Golden Guardian is solid ramp, and Null Chamber naming Maelstrom Wanderer shuts off ramp-into-commander hands.

## Key Clips

- You cast your first instant or sorcery each turn, you may cast a spell with lesser mana value that shares a card type with it. If you don't, you make a 2/1 monkey pirate that gets haste. Things like Dig Through Time and Treasure Cruise are good ΓÇö you go Dig Through Time, you're only spending two mana, but it's an eight-drop so you can cast something for free.
- If an effect allows you to cast a spell with spree without paying its mana cost, you must still choose at least one mode and pay the additional cost. So the spree cards do work ΓÇö you just have to pay at least one of the costs.
- Paradox Engine could be unbanned in Commander. It was only banned because Thrasios was printed the same year and people built very bad Thrasios Paradox Engine decks. Suddenly the Maelstrom Wanderer hand, which was all ramp into Maelstrom, was really bad because they couldn't cast Maelstrom ΓÇö usually the one thing that stops Maelstrom Wanderer, since normally it double cascades and you don't care about removal.
- Golden Guardian: this creature fights another target creature you control; when it dies, return to the battlefield transformed. So it has to lose a fight ΓÇö you need a bigger thing than a 4/4. Once flipped it adds two mana of any one color, and you pay four to make a 4/4. I think this card might actually just be good.
