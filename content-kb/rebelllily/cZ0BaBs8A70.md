## Summary

With Edge of Eternity making vehicles legal as commanders, the video brews a Grond deck. Grond is a four-mana battering ram that self-animates into a 5/5 trampling siege engine if you control an army during your turn, so the plan is to run every orc-amassing spell to keep fodder on board. Because auras and equipment fall off when a vehicle reverts to artifact mode, the deck leans on board-wide pump: anthems (Bad Moon, Death Pit Offering), D&D backgrounds (sacrifice payoff, +3/+3 flying deathtouch ward, +1/+1 counters on attack), and counter sources that persist through reversion (Unspeakable Symbol, Thran Vigil, Spymaster's Vault). Flinthawk-style re-equip aggression comes from Fireshrieker and Genji Glove for extra siege swings. Theme cards include both Witch-Kings, the nine Nazgul, and Sauron the Necromancer. Consistent amass comes from Orcish Bowmasters, War Glider, Barad-dur, Black Market Connections (changelings count as armies), plus ETB amassers. Graveyard recursion (Phyrexian Reclamation, Oversold Cemetery, Hell's Caretaker, Cathartic Reunion-style loops) refuels fodder. Sacrifice payoffs (Grave Pact, Braids, attrition) and board wipes (Damnation, Toxic Deluge, Phyrexian Scriptures) work because Grond survives as an artifact when you hold no army.
