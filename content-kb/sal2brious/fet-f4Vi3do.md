## Summary

This compilation collects a year of submitter deck reviews, each breaking down a deck's gameplan, strengths, and possible improvements. Recurring analysis centers on whether a commander supports the deck's plan, whether enablers and payoffs line up, and whether ramp, draw, and filtering are sufficient to avoid stall-outs. The reviewer repeatedly flags unfocused decks running too many small packages, recommending cutting weak payoffs to add ramp and card draw. Commander vulnerability is a common concern (e.g., Neheb needing protection or replay via ramp), as is thin interaction—several decks are told to add instant-speed removal, counterspells, or graveyard hate. Manabase notes cover budget five-color builds using gates plus Gond Gate, and low land counts being raised by two or three or via MDFCs. Combo decks (Slimefoot, Braids) are praised for cohesion but warned about large combo footprints and weak disruption. Across reviews, suggestions favor cards that pull double duty—legendary creatures for ramp in a Sisay/Jodah deck, sac outlets that also trigger payoffs, evasive equipment for voltron. The reviewer values ambition and unique synergies (Necrobloom cycling lands, commander-storm Rograkh, Astral Slide toolbox) while pushing toward tighter focus, smoother mana, and enough interaction to survive faster matchups.
