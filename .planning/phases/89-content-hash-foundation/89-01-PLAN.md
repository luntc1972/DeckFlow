<objective>
Establish the two shared surfaces every other Phase 89 plan consumes: the `ContentSiteIndexRow.BodySha256` property, and a single `ComputeBodySha256` helper that decodes UTF-8, normalizes line endings to LF, and SHA-256s the post-`SplitHeader` body (D-01/D-02). Output: `BodySha256` on the row model; `ComputeBodySha256` + extended `BuildSignature` on the signature class; xUnit tests proving LF/CRLF parity and mojibake divergence.
</objective>
