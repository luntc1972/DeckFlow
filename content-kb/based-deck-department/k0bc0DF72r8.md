## Summary

Video argues dead turns are self-inflicted deck flaws, and the fix is maximizing impactful game actions on every turn. Impact means affecting opponents' life totals or stopping them from affecting yours: Dauthi Voidwalker, Orcish Bowmasters, Swords to Plowshares and counterspells rate high, while a vanilla 2/2 rates low. Avoid stacking expensive cards that sit in hand and get answered cheaply. Stack synergy streams (e.g. Sauron the Dark Lord with Mauhur, Uruk-hai Captain and discard-into-flashback cards like Mystic Retrieval and Deep Analysis). ## Key Clips

- **[02:13]** Core question to ask of your deck: do you have the right combination of spells at different mana costs to let you do something impactful on every turn of the game? Impactful game actions matter, not just many actions.
- **[02:53]** A simple two-two creature has no real impact, but Dauthi Voidwalker, a Badger Maul Cub (?), or Orcish Bowmasters can have a massive impact despite similar cost and stats. Removal like Swords to Plowshares or a counterspell can flip the game.
- **[08:54]** To find one of a card by about turn seven or eight, you want six or more copies in the deck, which is a rough guide for how many individual win condition cards you need. Too many leaves a hand full of win conditions with no streams to focus into a river.
- **[09:28]** Have a plan for early, mid and late game. In bracket three, early is turns one and two, mid is turns three to five, and late is turn six and beyond. Decks that do nothing early or have no impactful plays after turn six are lopsided.
- **[11:44]** Run about 38 to 40 lands (elf or cEDH decks can go into the low 30s). If the deck wants ramp, aim for around 50 mana sources: 38 lands hit land drops through about turn four, and 12 ramp pieces reliably find one by turn two.
- **[12:45]** Absolute maximum of about nine tapped lands, so roughly one of the first four lands is tapped and can be played turn one. A second tapped land on turn two blocks your ramp source, and late tapped lands can leave you one mana short of a game-winning spell.
- **[13:54]** Run around 12 or more card draw sources as a starting point. Incremental engines like Mentor of the Meek, Beast Whisperer, or Whirlwind of Thought have the highest ceiling but the lowest floor, since they do nothing if they die to removal with an empty hand.
- **[15:17]** Prefer interaction over removal. Sorcery-speed board wipes just suck in Commander unless they are one-sided and leave you in a strong position. Well-timed instant-speed spells like Negate or Swords to Plowshares create the highest highs, and protection like counterspells, indestructible, hexproof or flicker matters equally.
