## Summary

This is a tournament recap, not a single deck strategy talk: the host reviews the top 16 of an SCG Con DC cEDH 5K event after a banlist shakeup that removed fast mana (Mana Crypt, dockside). Goo (Underworld Breach/artifact engine) won, now leaning on asymmetrical hate like Karn and Blasphemous Act rather than pure ramp; the slower meta makes Magus/Throne-style mana and stax pieces more relevant. Tyvar's Yawgmoth-style creature-combo deck ('the yam') uses vigilance-counter loops (Devoted Druid, Rosie Cotton + Brutal Cathar/Basking Broodscale, Ashnod's Altar with undying Young Wolf/Strangleroot Geist) to deck through Tyvar and win; counters, not mana, are its bottleneck, fixed by Hexdrinker and Manaform Hellkite. Other decks: Bjorna lifegain-extort combo, small Blue Farm with Lurrus, Sans-white wheel-draw shells (Brallin/Glint-Horn, Psychosis Crawler), and a Master of Keys reanimator using displacer kitten + rograkh-style Amber loop and Animate Dead via Abdel Adrian. Takeaway: removing fast mana flattened speed, rewarding stax, asymmetrical taxes, and grindy creature-combo lines. Host plugs his own Rograkh-Thrasios ('rock thrass') deck as the best.
