## Summary

A "brotherhood" subtheme builds flying tokens through Fey Wild Visitor, Thopter Fabricator, and Sword of Truth and Justice, leveraging card-draw synergies. The creator demonstrates the package by removing 20 cards from the Miss Bumbleflower precon and goldfishing several turns, showing how draw synergies, growing flyers, and combat tricks combine to turn any commander into a hidden assassin.

## Key Clips

- The assassin package is meant to complement the main game plans of whatever commander deck you're designing that has blue, giving you a clever pivot that you can hide under your main game plan to surprise your opponents with another set of synergies.
- To help your hidden one accomplish their task, we're playing five cards to give them unblockability: Assassin Den, the Brotherhood Regalia, Security Bypass, Smoke Bomb, and Whisper Silk Cloak.
- Security bypass is a cheap aura that makes your assassin unblockable whenever they attack alone and also connive each time they deal combat damage, allowing you to strengthen your assassin with a plus one plus one counter and filter for better tools.
- By making our damage triggers draw cards or cycle cards in hand, like security bypass, we can accrue more resources to beat our opponents through a longer game with cards like Coastal Piracy, Mask of Memory, and Assassin Gauntlet, which lets our commander cycle a card in hand while also tapping out an opponent's blockers.
- While it seems like assassins work alone, they're nothing without their brotherhood, which is why we're playing cards like Fay Wild Visitor, who lets our unblocked commanders create flying attackers off each combat. Thopter fabricator also makes flying token creatures every time we draw our second card.
- The package also plays Lost in the Maze and Sleep to tap out our opponent's blockers, similar to the way Assassin Gauntlet does. I've also added Molir Storm Hammer to help continue tapping down key blockers while also dealing a huge storm of damage to each of our opponents equal to the number of tapped creatures the blocking opponent controls.
- The iconic hidden blade can be flashed in to help win combat by snapping onto a commander to give it first strike.
- To demonstrate, I removed 20 cards from the Miss Bumbleflower precon, preserving the politics theme of the deck, and added the assassin package. It turns out she's really good with the draw synergies.
