## Summary

A casual Commander stream centered on building and piloting a Gates deck around a five-color commander that, on entry, looks at the top ten cards and adds one card of each color pair to hand. The deck leans on Gate lands (Guild Gates, Gond Gate which enters untapped and taps for five) and a Maze's End win condition the pilot can "turbo out" if unopposed. Ramp includes Cultivate/Kodama's Reach, Hour of Promise, Plaza of Genesis, Circuitous Route, and Open the Gates, plus Double Gang to copy several Gates at once for explosive mana. A surveil sub-theme (Enhanced Surveillance, Doom Whisperer, a self-reanimating creature that grows with counters and recurs threats via a finality counter) fuels card flow. Removal is heavy (Anguished Unmaking, Beast Within, Supreme Verdict, board wipes), with Assemble the Legion as a slow backup win. The builder favors casual, owned-card, non-combo decks, refuses to play Sol Ring, and argues life gain is a weak strategy. Much of the stream is meta commentary: Commander damage is a poorly implemented mechanic requiring app tracking, and could be fixed via doubled combat damage or unified per-player Commander-damage totals.
