## Summary

This video brews a Commander deck around Grond, the Hammer of the Underworld, a new vehicle commander legal as part of Edge of Eternity. Grond is a four-mana battering ram that self-animates into a 5/5 trampling siege engine when you control an army during your turn, so the deck packs every consistent Orc-amassing effect (Orcish Bowmasters, Wargdrake, Barad-dur, Black Market Connections, plus ETB amassers like Gothmog and Morgul Lieutenant). Because auras and equipment fall off when vehicles revert to artifacts, the build leans on board-wide pumpΓÇöanthems like Bad Moon, Dungeons & Dragons backgrounds, and especially +1/+1 counters (Unspeakable Symbol, Thran Vigil, Spymaster's Vault) that persist through reversion. Fire Streaker and Genji Glove grant extra combat steps. A Tolkien theme adds both Witch-Kings, the nine Nazgul, and Sauron. Sacrifice synergies recur via Grima Wormtongue, Dread Grave Pact, Braids, and Attrition, fed by graveyard recursion (Phyrexian Reclamation, Oversold Cemetery, Hell's Caretaker, Cathartic Reunion). Since Grond stays a safe artifact without an army, the deck freely runs board wipes like Damnation, Toxic Deluge, and Phyrexian Scriptures. The result is a flavorful, grindy sacrifice-and-recursion deck. A full decklist appears in the description.

## Key Clips

- Vehicles will be legal as commanders as part of the Edge of Eternity's release, which means we got a ton of new commanders to play with.
- Grond is a four mana battering ram that breaches any wall, self-animating into a 5/5 trampling siege engine if you control an army during your turn. The plan is to play every orc amassing spell we can so there's always fodder on the field.
- Unlike classic Voltron options, auras and equipment that pump up our vehicular commander fall off as soon as they revert to car mode. So we rely on board-wide pump effects like anthems such as bad moon or death pit offering.
- Plus one plus one counters are a great way to pump up vehicle commanders because counters stay on our vehicle even after it reverts to being just an artifact. Cards like unspeakable symbol, Thran vigil, and spy master's vault add counters to Grond.
- We only have four good ways to amass consistently: orcish bow masters, war glider with amass two on every combat for your turn, barad if a creature died this turn, and black market connections since changelings count as armies.
- This deck plays cards to recycle creatures out of the graveyard like Phyrexian Reclamation, Oversoul of Dusk cemetery effects, Hell's Caretaker to swap creatures between graveyard and battlefield, and Cathartic Reunion-style nightmare picks.
- Since our commander can stay as an artifact as long as we don't have an army, we can liberally play more board wipes like damnation, toxic deluge, and Phyrexian scriptures without worrying about our battering ram getting caught.
