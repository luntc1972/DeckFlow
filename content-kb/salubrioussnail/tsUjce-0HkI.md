## Summary

This video explores EDH/Commander deckbuilding philosophy through a thought experiment: with an Enlightened Tutor available, do you tutor for a powerful Skullclamp in a mid-power tokens deck? The creator identifies four player perspectives and examines the first two. Group one tutors for and plays powerful cards purely because winning and casting strong cards is funΓÇöpoking a 'good card dopamine button.' The creator notes this excitement often fades over time, and suggests keeping powerful cards fresh by playing them in unusual contexts or using them to enable offbeat, lower-power archetypes; tutors help guarantee access to scarce support pieces for janky gameplans. Group two owns tutors but won't always fetch the best option, treating tutors as freeing tools to match a card to the game's vibeΓÇösometimes choosing mundane targets.
