## Summary

Chamellia, the Seed Miser is a three-mana squirrel commander with Menace that grants squirrels Menace, creates a green squirrel whenever you sacrifice food, and pays mana plus exiles graveyard cards or sacrifices food to add +1/+1 counters to your squirrels. Food is the core engine, but testing showed the food card pool is weaker than expected, so the deck runs 17 food generators and six food spells—about a quarter of the deck. Sources include Bloomburrow cards, removal that leaves food, Giant Skewer, Lord of the Rings Hobbit cards, Peregrin Took, the Underworld Cookbook, Tireless Provisioner, and food-synergy cards like Elanor Gardner, Sweets' Revenge, and Trail of Crumbs. Squirrel generators are scarce, so the deck plays every continuous token-maker. Token doubling (Parallel Lives, Second Harvest) and ETB-trigger value matter; Witherbloom lifegain recursion, Hell's Caretaker, and Dark-Side Chef accelerate token output, while Seedborn Muse refunds activation mana. Finishers come from Natural Order into Craterhoof Behemoth (or budget Bellowing Crier), plus Overwhelming Stampede effects. Card draw uses Orvar/Frost-Fang, Toski, and Braids. The Ozolith preserves counters through board wipes. Mulligan for food generators and early mana.

## Key Clips

- Chamellia the Seed Miser is a three-mana squirrel warlock who has Menace and gives other squirrels you control Menace. Whenever you sacrifice one or more Foods you create one green squirrel creature token. You can also pay two mana and exile three cards from your graveyard or sacrifice a food to pump each other squirrel you control with a +1/+1 counter.
- Food is crucial to making this whole engine work because everything Chamellia does needs food: food creates squirrels and food also pumps your board. At first food looked like a well-supported theme after recent sets, but digging into the card pool it's actually still quite weak.
- Best food sources include cards from Eldraine that tap to create food, removal that leaves food behind, Giant Skewer (creates food on combat damage and synergizes with Menace), Lord of the Rings Hobbit cards, Paragan Took (creates food whenever Chamellia creates a squirrel), the Underworld Cookbook, Tireless Provisioner, and Trail of Crumbs for card advantage.
- They surprised by how few good squirrel generators exist, so the deck plays any card that continuously creates squirrel tokens: Acorn Catapult, Chatterfang, Chitterspitter, Cloakwood Hermit, Squirrel Nest, Nut Collector. Making tokens directly speeds things up because you don't have to spend two mana cracking a food to make one squirrel.
- Testing showed not enough food early left Chamellia with a weak board, so the list runs 17 food generators and six food spells — about a quarter of the deck. Token doubling matters but most doublers are expensive, so the list only runs Parallel Lives and Second Harvest. The best sources for both food and squirrels come from ETB triggers.
- Life gain cards from the Witherbloom precon return permanents from the graveyard after gaining life; paired with Hell's Caretaker and a sac outlet like Dark-Side Chef you can cycle creatures to create a large number of tokens in one go. Seedborn Muse is very useful since a lot of mana gets spent on activations — it lets you crack food or make tokens each turn cycle.
- Finishers come from older competitive elf decks: Natural Order turns a squirrel token or early elf into Craterhoof Behemoth; on a budget Blossoming Bogbeast is a good alternative that synergizes with life gain from food. End-Raze Forerunners is another cheap overrun. Card draw comes from Hoarfrost Fang (leveraging Menace) and Braids, Arisen Nightmare.
- The Ozolith was added because Chamellia gives all your squirrels counters — when an opponent wraths your board you keep all the counters and beef up another creature later. To play the deck, mulligan for hands with a lot of food generators plus some early mana so you can start making food and cracking it for squirrels.
