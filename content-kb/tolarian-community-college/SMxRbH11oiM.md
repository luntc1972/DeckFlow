## Summary

Responding to the May 18 ban-and-restricted update (five bans, three unbans across multiple formats; Modern hit hardest with Valakut and Lotus Field banned, Violent Outburst and Umezawa's Jitte unbanned), the presenter challenges himself to name one card to ban from every format. Picks: Pioneer—Monument to Endurance (Orzhov Greasefang backup engine). Premodern—Abeyance (shuts off interaction during Opalescence/Parallax Wave combo). Brawl (via Amy)—Force of Will (free counterspell). Modern—Neoform, which evolved from inconsistent turn-one combo into a consistent fair deck putting Griselbrand or Ghalta into play backed by Pact of Negation and Consign to Memory, enabled by Modern Horizons 3. Pauper—Writhing Chrysalis, a devoid Gruul ramp value engine hard to interact with, castable turn two via Arbor Elf/Utopia Sprawl. Legacy—Tamiyo, Inquisitive Student, broken alongside Brainstorm since flipping needs drawing three cards. Commander—Rhystic Study, the format's most-hated card-draw engine. Standard—Slickshot Show-Off, the most obnoxious Izzet Prowess threat, also fueling Leyline Red. He stresses most formats are actually healthy or diverse; these are forced picks, not endorsements of needed bans.

## Key Clips

- Last Monday on May 18th, Wizards of the Coast released a massive ban and restricted update. Five cards banned across four formats, as well as three cards unbanned across two formats. Notably, zero cards were banned in Standard.
- Modern received the largest shake-up with two bans and two unbans apiece. Valakut, Titan of Fire's Fury and Lotus Field have been banished to nerf both Boros Energy and Amulet Titan. Violent Outburst was returned, and Umezawa's Jitte is now Modern legal.
- For Pioneer, since WotC already banned Korvold, my pick is Monument to Endurance — the Orzhov Greasefang backup plan that turns instant-speed discards into fresh cards, treasure, or damage, and kills on its own within a few turns.
- The card I would ban in Modern is Neoform. It's no longer an all-in combo deck — it puts a turn-one Griselbrand or Ghalta into play backed by Pact of Negation, running four main-deck Consign to Memory to counter the upkeep lose-to-gain triggers. Not the best deck, but the most egregious.
- For Pauper (Popper), my pick is Writhing Chrysalis — a four-mana devoid Eldrazi that makes two Spawn tokens on a cast trigger, can't be hit by Hydroblast, dodges counters, and casts turn two off Arbor Elf plus Utopia Sprawl. An auto-include four-of in Gruul ramp.
- For Legacy, my pick is Tamiyo, Inquisitive Student. The interaction with Brainstorm is broken — drawing three cards in a turn flips her into a planeswalker, and Legacy's whole identity revolves around Brainstorm. It's a question of when, not if, she gets banned.
- For Commander, the card I would ban is Rhystic Study — an absurd card-draw engine never intended for Commander, a 10-cent common for decades, now maybe the single most hated card in the format.
- For Standard, my pick is Slickshot Show-Off. If your opponent plots it, you're in check the rest of the game — they can cast the bird wizard for free and dome you after a flurry of noncreature spells. Even scarier in Leyline Red, where a zero-mana Show-Off trivially kills with a Leyline out.
