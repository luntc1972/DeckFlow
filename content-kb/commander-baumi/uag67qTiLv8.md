## Summary

This video catalogs Magic: The Gathering cards that transform into lands, presented as a ramp alternative for non-green Commander decks frustrated by mana rocks getting destroyed. Cards are ranked by quality tiers. "Too slow/expensive" includes Roiling Vortex-style flip creatures, Azor's Gateway, and Mazemind Tome. "Reasonable" covers scary Solemn Similacrum variants (Ojer Axonil, Ojer Pakpatiq), Brass's Tunnel-Grinder (needs three descends), Golden Guardian (great with creatures big enough to fight into it), and Conqueror's Galleon. "Great in the right deck" includes spellslinger cards (Vance's Blasting Cannons, Primal Amulet), artifact-payoff Storm the Vault, creature-deck cards (Legion's Landing, Thousand Moons Smithy), Path of the Maze (random non-targeting destroy bypasses hexproof/shroud), and Malevolent Hermit/the Great door for graveyard-fill decks. "Always good" cards: Dowsing Device (cheap, flips passively via treasures), Dowsing Dagger (game-winning Lost Vale), Treasure Map (efficient, makes treasures on flip), and the pairing of Search for Azcanta plus the Everflowing Well, which share a graveyard-filling path and produce strong mana lands. The presenter favors these flip-lands as fun, varied, removal-resistant ramp, while acknowledging they are slower than mana rocks and the Myriad Pools remains artifact-removal vulnerable.

## Key Clips

- The video researches all cards that flip into lands, so you can ramp with lands even when you're not playing green — an alternative to Mana rocks that 'coincidentally die' to incidental removal like Lorien of the third path, Farewell, or Bane of Progress.
- Too slow/expensive category: Ojer/'deepest Foundation' is a six-mana creature that flips into a land when it dies (good but too pricey for ramp); 'thought's deepest betrayal' has the same problem at five mana; Grasping Shadows takes four mana and three turns to become a land.
- Shout-outs that are slightly too slow: Prophetic/Chromatic Compass flips only once you have seven lands in play; Azor's Gateway takes five turns and five mana to flip but its backside taps for Mana equal to your life total (tap for 40, win the game); Tarian's Journal is a two-mana artifact that sacrifices to draw, and its backside casts creatures from the graveyard.
- Reasonable 'scary Solemn' options: Orazca/O'Hare Axel and O'Have Pakati are Solemn Simulacrum-like creatures that bait removal — good when opponents waste a removal spell on your ramp, bad when they decide to kill you instead. Brass's Tunnel Grinder requires descending three times across three turns; its backside land taps for red and discovers for the spell's mana value.
- Golden Guardian: a four-mana 4/4 defender with a two-mana ability to fight your OWN creature; if it dies that turn it returns as a land tapping for two mana of any color, or four mana to make another 4/4 Golem. Needs a deck reliably fielding creatures at least 4/5 big to fight into it. Conqueror's Galleon is easier to flip (crew 4, attack once) but its land only taps for one colorless.
- Great in the right deck: Vance's Blasting Cannons and Primal Amulet need spellslinger decks to flip. Spitfire Bastion is a land with a free repeatable lightning bolt; Primal Wellspring duplicates instants/sorceries. Storm the Vault flips with five+ artifacts and generates treasure on combat damage, then taps for blue equal to artifacts controlled.
- Creature-deck flips: Legion's Landing is a one-mana enchantment that flips when you attack with three+ creatures. Thousand Moons Smithy makes a gnome as big as your artifacts+creatures and flips by tapping five permanents; its backside Barracks creates a gnome whenever you cast an artifact/creature spell. Path of Mettle's backside can destroy a random attacking creature for free — bypassing hexproof/shroud since it doesn't target.
- Top picks — always good: Dowsing Device (two mana, flips at four artifacts, easy in red with treasures) and Dowsing Dagger (flips on combat damage into Lost Vale, taps for any color). Treasure Map flips into Treasure Cove with free treasures. Best pair: Search for Azcanta + The Everflowing Well, which fill the graveyard together then flip into Mana lands (Myriad Pools copies permanents), ideal for blue graveyard-fill decks. Mawloc/'matal lundle' (the great door) backside taps for Mana equal to permanent cards in your graveyard — described as the strongest card in a Kiora deck.
