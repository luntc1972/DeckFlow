## Summary

This video builds a cEDH Thoracle-style deck ("otter TNT") around Valley Floodcaller, which gives noncreature spells flash and untaps Birds/Frogs/Otters/Rats when you cast a noncreature spell. The core combo pairs Floodcaller (or Rona, Herald of Invasion) with Banishing Knack or Retraction Helix on Mox Amber: tap for mana, bounce Amber, replay, untap, repeat for infinite mana, then draw the deck with Thassa's Oracle. Kitsa, Otterball Elite copies instant/sorceries (e.g., Dramatic Reversal) for another infinite line. Support creatures that benefit from untapping include Polywog Prodigy, Mockingbird, and Marvin (gains all creatures' activated abilities). EarthCraft (plus Fertile Ground/Wild Growth and Barad-dur/Herbork for fixing), Kiora's Follower, Tyvar, and Freed from the Real add untap/mana value. Psychic Frog with Agatha's Soul Cauldron lets you bank activated abilities and grant counters so Floodcaller untaps them too. The deck runs many creature tutors (Spellseeker, Trophy Mage, Recruiter of the Guard, Trinket Mage) to assemble pieces and feed Gaea's Cradle. Playtesting won via a Snapcaster/Eternal Witness Spellseeker chain into infinite mana. Author plans to cut Whispers of the Muse and add Mystical Tutor, Trinket Mage, and possibly Mystical Teachings/Waterlogged Teachings.

## Key Clips

- Valley Floodcaller lets you cast noncreature spells as though they have flash, and whenever you cast a noncreature spell, the Birds, Frogs, Otters, and Rats you control get +1/+1 and you untap them. Every deck in the SCG con 5K top 16 was playing this card.
- Banishing Knack combo: target Floodcaller with it, tap Floodcaller to bounce Mox Amber to owner's hand, replay Mox Amber to make a color, which untaps the otter, and repeat infinitely. Works with Sol Ring or Mana Vault too.
- Kitsa, Otterball Elite has prowess and can tap to copy an instant or sorcery you control of power 3 or greater. Resolve two prowess triggers to get its power to three, then use Kitsa to copy Dramatic Reversal, untap all dorks/artifacts, tap Kitsa again for infinite copies.
- Rona, Herald of Invasion combo mirrors the Floodcaller line: play Retraction Helix or Banishing Knack on Rona, target Mox Amber (the only legal target), tap Amber for a color, untap Rona, bounce and replay Amber for infinite mana, then draw your deck.
- EarthCraft plus Valley Floodcaller: even without the infinite, untapping creatures to make mana with EarthCraft is strong. Run fertile ground and wild growth so EarthCraft makes more, and use Bontu's/Cabal-style Urborg so basics tap for black too.
- Agatha's Soul Cauldron smooths the deck: if opponents kill your creatures with activated abilities, exile a card and put the +1/+1 counter on the Frog or Otter so Valley Floodcaller can untap it and you gain those abilities ΓÇö e.g. put Bloom Tender's ability on Polywog Prodigy.
- Snapcaster Mage plus Eternal Witness is a free combo to include. With Kiki/untap effects and the tutor package (Spellseeker, Trophy Mage, Recruiter of the Guard, Demonic Tutor, Imperial Seal), you can chain pieces like Retraction Helix and Dramatic Reversal together.
- Final verdict: a fun, piece-it-together combo deck. Cut Whispers of the Muse (does nothing once you have haste from Floodcaller), add Trinket Mage and Mystical Tutor. Recruiter was very good; Psychic Frog maybe too cute. Mystical Teachings/Waterlogged Grove are nice spice.
