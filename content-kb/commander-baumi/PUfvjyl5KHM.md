## Summary

A streamer showcases a budget Commander deck built around Shorikai, Genesis Engine. The core combo is Isochron Scepter imprinting Dramatic Reversal: Scepter taps for two mana to recast Dramatic Reversal, which untaps all nonland permanents including the Scepter. With three or more mana rocks (the deck runs ~12), each loop nets mana, producing infinite mana. Shorikai then taps repeatedly to draw the whole deck, and Blind Obedience's extort drains all opponents to win. The combo is slim, needing only the two key cards since Shorikai sits in the command zone and Blind Obedience is a fine standalone card. On the way there, the deck plays a control game: heavy counterspells, single-target removal, and multiple board wipes (End Hostilities, Out of Time), aiming to wipe opponents twice so they can't recover, plus lots of card draw to dig for the combo. The manabase is nearly mono-blue using mostly basics plus Command Tower and Prairie Stream, easing color requirements versus the streamer's three-color Revel deck. He argues budget Commander allows powerful cards unlike pauper, reviews viewer decklists, and prefers Moxfield over Archidekt for usability. In a game, he assembles the combo and wins via Blind Obedience extort.
