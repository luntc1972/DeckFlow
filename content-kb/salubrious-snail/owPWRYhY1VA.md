## Summary

The creator argues that a card can be a poor fit even when powerful and mechanically synergistic, if it "shortcuts the fun" a deck is built around. Casual EDH means intentionally sub-optimal decks, and preferences extend past win conditions to how you build resources and interact. Examples: in his budget Malfegor discard deck, Kroxa stays (it advances discard/resilience/pressure), but Waste Not is cut because it hands easy resources and lifts the deck out of the grindy low-resource state he enjoys. In his Sharuum/Astral Slide control deck, Monument to Endurance is cut because it becomes the default draw engine and collapses tutoring/flicker decisions into one route (wizardcycle Trophy Mage for it); draw doublers Alhammarett's Archive and Teferi's Ageless Insight are cut for the same reason. By contrast his Radha deck happily runs simple engines (Beast Whisperer, Garruk's Packleader) because how he builds advantage there doesn't matter. In Hans' Lilah deck, Thousand Year Storm is cut for negating value and mana, replaced by Swarm Intelligence; new Niv-Mizzet is acceptable. Heuristics: identify what you find fun; avoid high-powered wincons that end games prematurely; don't add cards that do a signature task better but less interestingly; optimize for a specific goal/vibe, not raw power.

- **[13:17]** Ask yourself—are there cards that might be winning your games prematurely, before your deck has spent the expected time building up? Higher-level tools that rapidly guarantee an all-out victory should be treated with care if your deck doesn't want to win as fast as possible.
