## Summary

Quips & Guac presents his first and favorite Commander deck: Both, Baloth Entertainer with the Noble Heritage background, a Boros (red-white) "goad" deck. Both forces opponents' creatures weaker than his power to attack each combat, and Noble Heritage grows him each upkeep while granting protection from opponents who add counters, creating a political arms-race where opponents are pressured to "take the deal." The deck leans into goad as a fun, interactive control alternative rather than stax. Key cards: Prisoner's Dilemma and Sound of Drums for political damage, Brash Taunter and Boros Reckoner plus Deflecting Palm and Comeuppance as Ikido-style win conditions for the late 1v1, Rogue's Passage and Sunforger to push Both through, and equipment (Silver Shroud Costume, Pure Steel Paladin) for protection and Voltron recasting since Both gets removed often. Card draw is the deck's main weakness, patched with Esper Sentinel and Reign of Riches (treasures enable cascade). Hot Pursuit and Take the Bait redirect goaded attackers. Agitator Ant adds more goad. He pegs it as bracket 2, no game changers, built around theme and politics over winning—the "moral victory" of subjecting friends to the goad deck.

## Key Clips

- **[01:44]** Both Barital Entertainer: creatures your opponents control with power less than Baloth's are goaded — a static effect forcing those creatures to attack each combat, and one-power tokens have to get used immediately.
- **[02:44]** Put Noble Heritage on Both so each upkeep he grows; anything with power three or less, then five, then seven has to fight. Opponents pile counters on a creature they want to keep back, and for each who does you gain protection from them — so their stuff is goaded by you and you're protected from them.
- **[14:38]** Prisoner's Dilemma: each opponent secretly chooses Silence or Snitch. All Silence deals 4 to each, all Snitch deals 8, otherwise it deals 12 to each who chose Silence — and it has flashback so you can do it twice.
- **[27:03]** Sunforger is one of the best cards in the deck — it makes Both four power stronger (toughness doesn't matter, he just wants power), and you can unattach it to search for your Deflecting Palm or Comeuppance to win.
- **[32:08]** Hot Pursuit: at the start of your combat, if two or more players have lost the game, gain control of all goaded and suspected creatures, untap them and give them haste — so in the 1v1 everything goaded coming at you becomes yours to swing back.
- **[35:51]** Pure Steel Paladin makes equip cost zero, so after a board wipe you can instantly re-equip everything onto Both; Silver Shroud Costume also gives shroud and unblockable the turn he comes down, acting as a protection spell.
- **[60:22]** Card draw is the hardest thing red-white suffers from — Esper Sentinel is one of the bigger upgrades; he's one mana and you can put equipment on him to guarantee the draws, squeezing value since Both only makes occasional treasures.
- **[60:21]** Reign of Riches: the first spell each turn cast with treasure mana gets cascade. With Both's incidental treasures it's basically extra draw, so it's really three mana not five because it gives you treasures and value across turns.
