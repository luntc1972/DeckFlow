## Summary

This Single Scoop episode features a mono-black/Rakdos midrange deck piloted in Pioneer, framed as a return to the format ahead of its 2027 competitive revival. The core plan is a grindy, attrition-based strategy built around Tragedy Feaster paired with Unholy Annex, supported by lifegain enablers like a Lifelink/Deathtouch two-drop and Graveyard Trespasser (which exiles graveyard cards and gains life so creatures need not be sacrificed). Card advantage and disruption come from Liliana of the Veil and Ral, Xeric guest lecturer, the latter reanimating three-mana-or-less creatures like Trespasser via minus-two. Removal and hand attack include Fatal Push, Thoughtseize, and Meathook Massacre, with Mutavault and Field of Ruin as utility lands. Across several games the deck repeatedly out-grinds control opponents wielding board wipes (Settle the Wreckage, Supreme Verdict), Shark Typhoon, and Wandering Emperor by forcing them to spend removal, stripping key answers with Thoughtseize, and rebuilding pressure with demons and recursion. The pilot notes Pioneer feels surprisingly fair and grindy, considers adding Cavern of Souls to fight counterspells and a Wandering Emperor, and emphasizes patient sequencing—holding threats, denying lands, and playing around sweepers—to win long attrition battles.
