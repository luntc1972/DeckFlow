## Summary

Two extreme €50 budget Commander decks. Deck one, Vineix Verbose Host, exploits the speed mechanic: evasive one-drops attack to reach max speed (4), at which point drawing one card draws two. This turns normally fair "looters" (Ledger Shredder, Shoreline Looter, Malcolm, tap looters, synergy looters like Baral) into card-advantage engines. Coastal Piracy and Reconnaissance Mission add draws on combat damage; Teferi's Ageless Insight stacks with Vineix for quadruple draws. The huge resulting hand (15-30 cards) makes normally-prohibitive free interaction cheap: Foil, Commandeer, Disrupting Shoal, Forbid with buyback, and hyper-specific counters (Turn Aside, Wash Away). Win condition is Laboratory Maniac by literally drawing the entire deck, no combo. Walking Atlas/Pew UFO ramp from the excess lands in hand; it's a long-game deck. Deck two, Lumra Bellow of the Woods, spends €36 (70% budget) on nine combo pieces. With ~60% lands (60 forests), it sacrifices lands, recasts Lumra (dies to no-lands, returns to command zone, ETB mills four and returns all lands tapped), netting ~2 lands per loop. Mirror Pool copies Lumra for an infinite loop; Sunscorch Desert pings opponents to death. It runs zero interaction—strategically forcing opponents to spend theirs—making it a toxic, feast-or-famine turbo deck.
