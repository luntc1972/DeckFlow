## Summary

This video argues Magic: The Gathering's Commander format should abolish its ban list entirely in favor of the new four-bracket power system, analyzing the design problem from a developer's perspective. The creator frames Commander through the rules committee's three principlesΓÇösocial, creative, and stableΓÇöand shows a ban list fails the creative goal (maximizing card pool) and the stability goal (banning is disruptive), while only partially helping social communication. The real root problem is pregame communication: players need to align expectations within a 30-60 second window, using shared pre-prepared language, with minimal differentiation increments to avoid mismatched tables. Brackets solve this better than a 1-10 scale because a smaller four-point scale improves placement accuracy.
