<objective>
Build and run a throwaway `DeckFlow.CLI` research command that re-derives per-commander role-floor
divergence for Cut Lab's five role floors (interaction, protection, engines, payoffs, win
conditions) using the REAL production classifiers (`DeckStatClassifier`, `PlanRoleClassifier`,
`CutLabRoleAssigner`) and the REAL production Scryfall resolver against the Postgres
category-knowledge corpus, applies one explicit, uniformly-applied statistical bar, and publishes a
committed findings document ending in a named go/no-go recommendation for Phase 2.

Purpose: the prior ad hoc session's 4-commander Python reimplementation cannot be trusted to
predict what the shipped C# classifiers would say, and its sample was too small to separate
signal from corpus noise. This phase replaces both problems: real classifiers, in-process, on a
defensible sample, judged by a bar that is written down before the data is read.

Output: `DeckFlow.Core/Research/RoleFloorDivergenceStats.cs` (pure, unit-tested statistical bar),
an additive, backward-compatible board filter on
`CategoryKnowledgeRepository.GetCategoryDeckMembershipForCommanderAsync`,
`DeckFlow.CLI/RoleFloorResearchCommandRunner.cs` (the `role-floor-research` CLI command wired
into `Program.cs`), and the committed `RESEARCH-FINDINGS.md` with its go/no-go line. Nothing
under `DeckFlow.Web`'s shipped controllers/views/services is touched.
</objective>
