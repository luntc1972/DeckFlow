## Finding 1 — short-viewport overlap (advisory, not a blocker)

At **390×664** (iPhone 13 / SE class), sticky chrome plus the pinned card occupy y=0…343 of a 664px
viewport — **52% of the screen**. Anything centred by `scrollIntoView({block:'center'})` lands at
y≈332 and is therefore under the pinned card:

```json
{ "targetTop": 310, "pinnedBottom": 343, "pinnedStuck": true,
  "atScrollLimit": false, "hit": "DIV.cutlab-proposal--pinned" }
```

Three qualifications, each measured:

1. **Not a WebKit bug.** Chromium at 390×664 reproduces it identically (`hit:
   DIV.cutlab-proposal--pinned`); Chromium at 390×844 does not (`hit: SUMMARY.`). The variable is
   viewport **height**, not engine.
2. **Not a scroll-clamp artifact.** `atScrollLimit: false` — the page had room to scroll further.
3. **Content stays reachable.** Nudging the scroll by the overlap clears it and the click lands on
   the summary (`reachable: true`). Nothing is permanently unreachable.

This is inherent to any sticky element and does not block 07-05. Worth a follow-up ticket only if
the pinned card's mobile height is considered too large a share of a short viewport.

## Finding 2 — jump-nav lands sections behind sticky chrome (PRE-EXISTING, not 07-05)

The in-page anchor nav calls `scrollIntoView({block:'start'})` (`cut-lab.ts:487`) and relies on the
global `scroll-margin-top` rule at `site-common.css:122`. That rule is a hardcoded `4rem`; the real
chrome height is computed at runtime. On mobile they disagree:

| Project | chrome height | scroll-margin | landed at | hidden behind chrome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| chromium-mobile 390×844 | 158.5px | 60px | y=60 | **98px** |
| chromium-desktop 1280×900 | 44px | 60px | y=60 | none (16px clear) |

So on mobile, jumping to *Cut rounds* or *Tune quantities* buries the section heading ~98px behind
the sticky nav. Desktop is unaffected.

**Attribution, by `git log -S`:** `scroll-margin-top` came from `f8492dc93` (v1.3). The sticky
elements it under-shoots are `nav.cutlab-anchor-nav` and `.cutlab-sticky-bar`, both pre-existing.
The pinned card is a **consumer** of `--cutlab-pinned-offset`, not a contributor to it
(`cut-lab.ts:943-946` sums only those two candidates). 07-05 did not introduce this; by computing
the chrome height it merely made the mismatch measurable. Ticket separately.
