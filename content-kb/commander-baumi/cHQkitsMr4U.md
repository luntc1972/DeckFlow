## Summary

This is a casual Commander stream where the creator tests a self-built "Speed Demon" deck centered on the upcoming speed / start-your-engines mechanic. The deck's theme is going fast by doing illegal-feeling things: flash, madness, haste, and instant-speed plays. The Speed Demon draws X cards equal to speed each end step (losing X life), so the build leans into emptying the hand via discard synergies and madness rather than raising maximum hand size, which the creator calls a coward's approach. Key cards seen: Olivia making vampires off discards, Aatchik/Akki Battle Squad, rankle, marrow, unforgiving one reanimation, and Unholy Annex. The creator notes start-your-engines effectively begins at speed two because you usually already have an attacker, and criticizes the mechanic's name for locking it to this set rather than a reusable term like momentum. Practical takeaways: the deck depends heavily on its commander, lacks ramp and removal, and tends to kill its own pilot through self-damage. Both games are lost by self-deck-out, but the creator considers staying competitive after missing early land drops a win. He also mentions running a budget cEDH Six (Sythis-style) list for an upcoming tournament.
