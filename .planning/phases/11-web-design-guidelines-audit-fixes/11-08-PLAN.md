<objective>
Land Sweep 8 of the WDG audit: convert the two `<span class="info-tooltip" title="...">i</span>` patterns on SuggestCategories + CommanderCategories to `<details><summary>i</summary><p>…</p></details>` per D-10.

Purpose: The current pattern shows tooltip text only on mouse hover; screen readers hear the bare letter "i", and keyboard users cannot trigger the tooltip at all. Per D-10 the chosen replacement is the HTML5 disclosure widget pattern — simpler than `<button aria-describedby>`, no JavaScript, keyboard + SR accessible out of the box.

Output: Two Razor views updated; the title-only `<span>` pattern is removed.
</objective>
