## Summary

Card evaluation of Black Widow, Agile Avenger from the Marvel Superheroes set for cEDH/Commander. She is a 2/2 menace legendary human spy hero: whenever an opponent draws their second card each turn, put a +1/+1 counter on her and you draw a card, making her a card-advantage parity piece. The reviewer compares her to Fairy Mastermind, conceding Fairy Mastermind looks stronger on rate (a 1U flash creature that also forces draws), but argues Black Widow sees play in different decks — Naya, Boros, and Mardu shells that care about the +1/+1 counters, since power on a menace creature is real. He calls her a very obvious Winota inclusion, a good combat threat that maintains card advantage, and a staple going forward. He praises the design for giving tools to traditionally unloved colors (Boros) and for letting those decks maintain parity in pods full of Rhystic Study effects, saying it helps forgive weaker printings like Flash Photography. This is the kind of card he wants to see for cEDH.
